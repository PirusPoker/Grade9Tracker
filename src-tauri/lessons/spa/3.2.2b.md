## What it is

At Foundation you learned the future, conditional and imperfect in the **singular** only. This **Higher-only** lesson completes them: the **plural persons** (*viajaremos, iríamos, jugábamos*), **five more irregular future and conditional stems** (*sabr-, querr-, vendr-, dir-, saldr-*), and the plural of the irregular imperfects (*éramos, íbamos, veíamos*). It also covers the details that become **credit-bearing at Higher**: **spelling changes** (*cojo, busqué, llegué, empecé, leyó*) and **preterite stem changes** in *-ir* verbs (*pidió, durmió, prefirieron*), plus **commands to more than one person** (*¡Escuchad!*).

**Where it turns up.** Higher Writing tasks often ask about the family or a group: *what did you use to do together, what will you do next year*. That means *nosotros* and *ellos* forms in every time frame. The translation into Spanish at Higher regularly includes a plural future or imperfect and a third-person preterite such as *she asked for* (*pidió*) or *they slept* (*durmieron*). In Reading and Listening, *-íamos* (we would / we used to) and *-emos* (we will) must be recognised instantly.

**The core structures.**
- **Future**: whole infinitive + *-é, -ás, -á, -emos, -éis, -án*. Irregular stems: *tendr-, har-, podr-, pondr-* (Foundation) and **sabr-** (saber), **querr-** (querer), **vendr-** (venir), **dir-** (decir), **saldr-** (salir).
- **Conditional**: the same stems + *-ía, -ías, -ía, -íamos, -íais, -ían*.
- **Imperfect**: *-aba, -abas, -aba, -ábamos, -abais, -aban*; *-ía, -ías, -ía, -íamos, -íais, -ían*. Irregular: **ser** (*era, eras, era, éramos, erais, eran*), **ir** (*iba ... íbamos, ibais, iban*), **ver** (*veía ... veíamos, veíais, veían*).
- **Spelling changes** (now individually credited): present *g → j* before *o* (*coger → cojo*, *escoger → escojo*, *proteger → protejo*); preterite *yo* forms *c → qu* (*busqué*), *g → gu* (*llegué*), *z → c* (*empecé*), and **y** in the third persons of verbs like *leer, oír, construir, caer, creer* (*leyó, oyeron, construyó*).
- **Preterite stem changes in -ir verbs** from the present-tense clusters, in the *él* and *ellos* forms only: *e → i* (*pedir → pidió, pidieron*; *preferir → prefirió*; *sentir → sintió*; *seguir → siguió*) and *o → u* (*dormir → durmió, durmieron*; *morir → murió, murieron*).
- **Commands**, positive only, *tú* and *vosotros*: replace the *-r* of the infinitive with **-d**: *hablad, comed, escribid, id, venid*. With *os* the *d* drops: *¡Levantaos!*

Present participles of some *-ir* verbs change too (*durmiendo, muriendo*); these are not individually credited, but the *pedir* cluster (*pidiendo, siguiendo*) is.

**Grade 9** answers move between *nosotros* forms in three tenses without slips (*Antes íbamos ..., ahora vamos ..., el año que viene iremos ...*) and get the third-person preterite stem changes right.

::: terms
- **future plural endings**: *-emos, -éis, -án* on the whole infinitive: *viajaremos, comeréis, vivirán*
- **conditional plural endings**: *-íamos, -íais, -ían*: *compraríamos* = we would buy
- **Higher irregular stems**: *sabr-, querr-, vendr-, dir-, saldr-* in the future and conditional: *vendremos, diría*
- **imperfect plural**: *-ábamos, -abais, -aban* and *-íamos, -íais, -ían*: *jugábamos* = we used to play
- **éramos / íbamos / veíamos**: the *nosotros* imperfects of *ser, ir, ver*, all with an accent
- **spelling change**: a letter change that keeps the sound: *cojo* (coger), *llegué*, *busqué*, *empecé*, *leyeron*
- **preterite stem change**: *e → i* or *o → u* in the *él* and *ellos* preterite of *-ir* stem-changers: *pidió, durmieron*
- **vosotros command**: infinitive with *-r* replaced by *-d*: *¡Mirad!, ¡Venid!*; before *os* the *d* drops: *¡Sentaos!*
:::

::: method
1. **Choose the tense** from the time frame: will → future; would → conditional; used to / was -ing → imperfect; did (once) → preterite.
2. **Future / conditional**: find the stem (whole infinitive, or one of the nine irregular stems), then add the ending for the person. Every form of both tenses has an accent except the future *nosotros* (*-emos*).
3. **Imperfect**: *-ar* verbs → *-aba-* forms; *-er/-ir* → *-ía-* forms; *nosotros* takes an accent (*-ábamos, -íamos*). Check *ser, ir, ver*.
4. **Preterite, él or ellos, of an -ir stem-changer?** Apply *e → i* or *o → u*: *pidió, sintieron, durmió*.
5. **Spelling**: *yo* preterite of *-car, -gar, -zar*; third-person preterite of *leer*-type verbs; *yo* present of *-ger*.
6. **Command to a group of friends?** Infinitive *-r* → *-d*.
:::

| | future | conditional | imperfect (-ar) | imperfect (-er/-ir) | ser (imperfect) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| yo | hablaré | hablaría | hablaba | comía | era |
| tú | hablarás | hablarías | hablabas | comías | eras |
| él / ella | hablará | hablaría | hablaba | comía | era |
| nosotros | hablaremos | hablaríamos | hablábamos | comíamos | éramos |
| vosotros | hablaréis | hablaríais | hablabais | comíais | erais |
| ellos | hablarán | hablarían | hablaban | comían | eran |

| infinitive | future stem | yo future | ellos conditional |
|---|---|---|---|
| saber | sabr- | sabré | sabrían |
| querer | querr- | querré | querrían |
| venir | vendr- | vendré | vendrían |
| decir | dir- | diré | dirían |
| salir | saldr- | saldré | saldrían |

::: example Three time frames with nosotros
**When we were small we used to go to the beach. Now we go to the mountains, and next year we will go to Mexico, where we would like to visit the museums.**

1. *When we were small* → *ser*, *nosotros*, imperfect: **Cuando éramos pequeños**.
2. *we used to go* → *ir*, imperfect: **íbamos a la playa**.
3. *Now we go* → present: **Ahora vamos a la montaña**.
4. *next year we will go* → future: **el año que viene iremos a México**.
5. *where we would like* → **donde nos gustaría** + infinitive (*gustar* stays singular with an infinitive): **visitar los museos**.

**Cuando éramos pequeños íbamos a la playa. Ahora vamos a la montaña, y el año que viene iremos a México, donde nos gustaría visitar los museos.**
:::

::: example Preterite stem changes in the third person
**My sister asked for fish, but my parents preferred meat. Afterwards they slept for two hours.**

1. *asked for* → *pedir*, *ella*: stem change *e → i*: **pidió pescado**.
2. *preferred* → *preferir*, *ellos*: **prefirieron la carne**.
3. *they slept* → *dormir*, *ellos*: *o → u*: **durmieron**.
4. *for two hours* → **durante dos horas**.

**Mi hermana pidió pescado, pero mis padres prefirieron la carne. Después durmieron durante dos horas.**

The *yo* and *nosotros* forms do not change: *pedí, pedimos, dormí, dormimos*.
:::

::: example Spelling changes that now earn marks
Put each verb into the preterite:

1. *yo / llegar* → the *g* must stay hard before *é*: **llegué**.
2. *yo / buscar* → *c* before *é* would sound like *th/s*, so **busqué**.
3. *yo / empezar* → *z* is not written before *e*: **empecé**.
4. *ellos / leer* → *le-* + *-ieron*: an unstressed *i* between vowels becomes *y*: **leyeron**.
5. *él / oír* → **oyó**.

And one present form: *yo / escoger* → *g* before *o* would sound hard, so **escojo**.
:::

::: example The future with the Higher irregulars
**My cousins will come on Saturday, but they won't want to go out, and they will tell us that they know nothing.**

1. *will come* → *vendr-* + *-án*: **Mis primos vendrán el sábado**.
2. *they won't want to go out* → **no querrán salir**.
3. *they will tell us* → *dir-* + *-án*, with *nos* before: **nos dirán**.
4. *that they know nothing* → present: **que no saben nada**.

**Mis primos vendrán el sábado, pero no querrán salir, y nos dirán que no saben nada.**
:::

::: watch
- **Irregular future stems**: *vendremos, saldrán, diré, querrá, sabréis*; never *veniremos* or *deciré*.
- **Accents**: future *-éis, -án*; every conditional form (*-íamos*); imperfect *-ábamos, -íamos, éramos, íbamos*.
- **Preterite stem changes only in *él* and *ellos***: *pidió / pidieron*, but *pedí, pedimos*.
- **Spelling in the *yo* preterite**: *llegué, busqué, empecé, jugué* now cost a mark each if wrong.
- **y, not i**: *leyó, oyeron, construyó*.
- **vosotros commands end in -d**, with no accent: *¡Comed!*
:::
