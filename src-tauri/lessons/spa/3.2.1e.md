## What it is

This lesson covers four verb forms that sit around the preterite and let you say more than 'I did': what is **happening right now** (*Estoy leyendo* = I am reading), what you **have done** (*He visitado* = I have visited), what you **used to do** (*Jugaba* = I used to play) and what **was happening** when something else happened (*Estaba leyendo cuando ...* = I was reading when ...).

**Where it turns up.** The imperfect and the present perfect are how you get a **second past tense** into the 90- and 150-word tasks, and *Cuando era pequeño/a ...* is the classic way into a 'how things have changed' bullet point. The translation into Spanish regularly contrasts 'used to' with 'now'. In Reading and Listening, *había* (there was/were), *tenía* (had) and *era/estaba* (was) are among the commonest verbs in any past-tense text.

**The core structures.**
- **Present continuous**: *estar* (present) + present participle. *-ar → -ando*, *-er/-ir → -iendo*. Stems ending in a vowel take **-yendo** (*leer → leyendo*, *construir → construyendo*); the *pedir* cluster changes *e → i* (*pedir → pidiendo*, *seguir → siguiendo*). Use it only for what is in progress **now**.
- **Present perfect**: *haber* (*he, has, ha, hemos, habéis, han*) + past participle, *-ar → -ado*, *-er/-ir → -ido*. Irregular participles are on the vocabulary list: *abierto* at Foundation; *hecho, dicho, visto, puesto, vuelto, escrito, cubierto* on the Higher list.
- **Imperfect (Foundation: singular only)**: *-ar* → *-aba, -abas, -aba*; *-er/-ir* → *-ía, -ías, -ía*. Three irregulars: **ser** (*era, eras*), **ir** (*iba, ibas*), **ver** (*veía, veías*). Use it for **'used to'** (habits) and **'was/were + -ing'** (ongoing). Four very common forms: *tenía* (had), *había* (there was/were), *estaba* (was, for a state or place), *era* (was, for a trait). The plural forms (*jugábamos, íbamos, eran*) are Higher-only and are in the Higher verbs lesson.
- **Imperfect continuous**: *estaba* + present participle for an action in progress: *Estaba estudiando cuando llegó mi madre.*

**Grade 9** answers contrast time frames inside one paragraph (*Antes jugaba al fútbol, pero ahora estoy aprendiendo a tocar la guitarra y he ganado un concurso*), choose preterite versus imperfect correctly, and never put anything between *haber* and the participle.

::: terms
- **present participle (gerund)**: the '-ing' form after *estar*: *hablando, comiendo, viviendo, leyendo, pidiendo*
- **present continuous**: *estar* + present participle for what is happening now: *Estoy estudiando* = I'm studying
- **past participle**: the '-ed' form after *haber*: *hablado, comido, vivido*; it never changes its ending in this tense
- **present perfect**: *haber* + past participle, 'have done': *He terminado* = I have finished
- **irregular participle**: a participle not ending in *-ado/-ido*: *abierto* (opened), *hecho* (done), *visto* (seen), *escrito* (written)
- **imperfect**: the past tense for habits and ongoing actions: *Jugaba* = I used to play / I was playing
- **habitual action**: something done regularly in the past, English 'used to': *Iba al parque todos los días*
- **había**: 'there was / there were / there used to be': *Había un cine en mi pueblo*
- **era vs estaba**: both mean 'was'; *era* for a lasting trait (*era alto*), *estaba* for a state or location (*estaba cansado, estaba en casa*)
- **imperfect continuous**: *estaba* + participle, 'was doing': *Estaba leyendo cuando llegó mi padre*
:::

::: method
1. **Decide what the English means.** Happening now → present continuous. 'Have done' → present perfect. 'Used to' or 'was doing' → imperfect (or *estaba* + -ing). One finished event at a known time → preterite (previous lesson).
2. **Present continuous**: conjugate *estar* (*estoy, estás, está, estamos, estáis, están*), then add *-ando / -iendo* (vowel stem → *-yendo*; *pedir* cluster → *i*).
3. **Present perfect**: conjugate *haber*, then add the participle. Check the irregular list. Object pronouns go **before** *haber*: *Lo he visto*.
4. **Imperfect**: stem + *-aba / -ía* endings; *era, iba, veía* are the only irregulars.
5. **Interrupted action**: the ongoing background in the imperfect (or *estaba* + -ing), the interruption in the preterite.
:::

| | present participle | past participle | imperfect (yo / tú / él) |
|---|---|---|---|
| hablar | hablando | hablado | hablaba, hablabas, hablaba |
| comer | comiendo | comido | comía, comías, comía |
| vivir | viviendo | vivido | vivía, vivías, vivía |
| leer | leyendo | leído | leía, leías, leía |
| pedir | pidiendo | pedido | pedía, pedías, pedía |
| ser | siendo | sido | era, eras, era |
| ir | yendo | ido | iba, ibas, iba |
| ver | viendo | visto | veía, veías, veía |

::: example Building the present continuous
**My sister is reading and my grandfather is asking for a coffee.**

1. *is reading* → *estar* for *ella*: **está**; *leer* has a vowel stem (*le-*), so **leyendo**, not *leiendo*.
2. *is asking for* → **está pidiendo**: *pedir* is in the *pedir* cluster, so *e → i* in the participle.
3. *a coffee* → **un café**.

**Mi hermana está leyendo y mi abuelo está pidiendo un café.**

Check: is it happening now? Yes, so the continuous is fine. For 'I'm going to Madrid tomorrow' Spanish uses the present (*Mañana voy a Madrid*), never *estoy yendo*.
:::

::: example Used to, and now
**When I was little I used to live in a village. There was a park and I went there every day. Now I live in the city.**

1. *When I was little* → a trait/state of the time, imperfect of *ser*: **Cuando era pequeño/a**.
2. *I used to live* → habit: **vivía**.
3. *There was a park* → **había un parque**.
4. *I went there every day* → repeated, so still imperfect, even though English says 'went': **iba todos los días**.
5. *Now I live* → back to the present for the contrast: **ahora vivo en la ciudad**.

**Cuando era pequeña vivía en un pueblo. Había un parque e iba allí todos los días. Ahora vivo en la ciudad.**
:::

::: example Imperfect continuous interrupted by the preterite
**I was watching TV when my mother phoned.**

1. The background action in progress → **estaba viendo la tele** (or simply **veía la tele**).
2. The event that cut in, at one moment → preterite: **llamó**.
3. *when* → **cuando**.

**Estaba viendo la tele cuando mi madre llamó.**

Compare: *Vi la tele* = I watched TV (finished); *Veía la tele* = I used to watch / I was watching TV.
:::

::: example The present perfect with a pronoun and a negative
**I haven't seen it yet, but my brother has opened the door.**

1. *I haven't seen it* → *no* + pronoun + *haber* + participle: **No lo he visto**. *Visto* is an irregular participle.
2. *yet* → **todavía**: *todavía no lo he visto* is the most natural order.
3. *has opened* → **ha abierto** (irregular).

**Todavía no lo he visto, pero mi hermano ha abierto la puerta.**
:::

::: watch
- **Nothing between *haber* and the participle**: *Lo he visto*, *No he comido*, never *He lo visto*.
- **The participle does not agree** after *haber*: *Mis padres han salido*, not *salidos*.
- **Used to = imperfect**, even when English uses a simple past for a habit: *Every summer we went to the beach* → imperfect.
- **One finished event = preterite**: *Ayer vi una película*, not *veía*.
- **-yendo after a vowel stem**: *leyendo, construyendo*; *pidiendo, siguiendo* in the *pedir* cluster.
- **era vs estaba**: *era simpático* (personality), *estaba enfermo* (state at the time).
:::
