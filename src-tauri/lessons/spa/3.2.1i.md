## What it is

This lesson covers the small words that link ideas and the endings that build new words from old ones. **Prepositions** (*a, de, en, con, sin, para, por, antes de, después de*) show place, time, purpose and possession. Spanish adds an **extra *a*** before a person who is the object of a verb (*Veo **a** mi abuela*), uses **de** for possession (*la casa **de** Hugo* = Hugo's house) and follows a preposition with an **infinitive**, never an '-ing' form (*antes de **salir*** = before leaving).

The second half is **word building**: *-ito/-ita* (little, dear: *hermanito*), *-ísimo/-ísima* (very: *facilísimo*), *-mente* (-ly: *normalmente*), *-idad* (-ity: *seguridad*) and *-able* (-able: *evitable*).

**Where it turns up.** 'Before/after + -ing' and 'X's house' are classic translation-into-Spanish tests, and *para* + infinitive (in order to) is the easiest way to add a reason in Writing. In Reading, AQA can use a derived word that is not on the list if its base word is (*librito* from *libro*, *seguridad* from *seguro*), so the suffixes let you work out words you have never seen.

**The core structures.**
- **Personal *a***: before a specific person (or pet) as direct object: *Visito a mis abuelos*, *Conozco a Juan*, *No veo a nadie*. Not before things (*Visito el museo*) and not after *tener* (*Tengo dos hermanos*).
- **de for possession**: *el coche de mis padres*, *la habitación de mi hermana*, *la casa del profesor* (*de + el = del*). Spanish has no 's.
- **Preposition + infinitive**: *para aprender* (in order to learn), *sin hablar* (without speaking), *antes de comer* (before eating), *después de salir* (after going out).
- **Verbs with a preposition** before an infinitive or noun: some just link (*empezar a, aprender a, jugar a, ir a*); others change the meaning and are listed: *llegar a* (manage to), *dejar de* (stop), *volver a* (do again), *parecerse a* (look like), *olvidarse de* (forget), *preocuparse por* (worry about).
- **para and por**: *para* = for (a person, a purpose, a deadline) and *para* + infinitive = in order to; *por* = through/along, per, because of, 'in' with parts of the day (*por la mañana*) and in exchange for (*gracias por*).
- **Suffixes**: *-ito/-ita* on nouns (*libro → librito*, *casa → casita*), *-ísimo/-ísima* on adjectives (*fácil → facilísimo*, *caro → carísimo*), *-mente* on the **feminine** singular or an invariable adjective (*rápida → rápidamente*, *alegre → alegremente*), *-idad* on adjectives (*seguro → seguridad*, *real → realidad*) and *-able* on verb stems (*evitar → evitable*).

**Grade 9** answers use *para* + infinitive and *antes de / después de* + infinitive to build long, linked sentences, never forget the personal *a*, and use *-ísimo* and *-mente* forms to add precision.

::: terms
- **preposition**: a word showing place, time or relationship: *en, con, sin, entre, durante, sobre, hasta, desde*
- **personal a**: the *a* before a person as direct object, with no English equivalent: *Llamo a mi madre*
- **de for possession**: 'of' replacing English 's: *el perro de Ana* = Ana's dog
- **preposition + infinitive**: Spanish uses the infinitive after a preposition: *después de comer* = after eating
- **para + infinitive**: 'in order to': *Trabajo para ganar dinero* = I work (in order) to earn money
- **por**: through, along, per, because of, 'in' the morning: *por la tarde*, *por el parque*, *gracias por todo*
- **diminutive -ito / -ita**: 'little' or affection: *hermano → hermanito*, *casa → casita*
- **superlative -ísimo / -ísima**: 'very, extremely': *bueno → buenísimo*, *caro → carísimo*, *fácil → facilísimo*
- **-mente adverb**: 'ly', added to the feminine singular: *tranquila → tranquilamente*, *fácil → fácilmente*
- **-idad noun**: '-ity', from an adjective: *seguro → seguridad*, *real → realidad*, *activo → actividad*
- **-able adjective**: '-able', from a verb stem: *evitar → evitable*, *aceptar → aceptable*
:::

::: method
1. **Is the object a specific person?** Add *a* before it (*a + el = al*): *Ayudo al vecino*.
2. **'X's thing'?** Turn it round: thing + *de* + owner: *la bici de mi hermano*.
3. **Preposition + verb?** Use the infinitive: *sin pagar*, *antes de salir*, *para ahorrar*.
4. **Verb + preposition?** Check whether the verb needs one (*dejar de fumar*, *volver a empezar*, *aprender a nadar*) and keep it.
5. **para or por?** Purpose, recipient, deadline, destination → *para*. Route, cause, exchange, per, part of the day → *por*.
6. **Building a word?** *-mente* on the feminine form; *-ísimo* after removing the final vowel, with a spelling change if needed (*rico → riquísimo*); *-idad* after a consonant or after removing the final vowel.
:::

::: example Translation: before, after and in order to
**Before going out, I help my grandmother. After eating, we watch TV to relax.**

1. *Before going out* → preposition + infinitive: **Antes de salir**.
2. *I help my grandmother* → a person as object: **ayudo a mi abuela**.
3. *After eating* → **Después de comer**.
4. *we watch TV* → **vemos la tele**.
5. *to relax* → purpose: **para relajarnos** (the reflexive pronoun matches 'we').

**Antes de salir, ayudo a mi abuela. Después de comer, vemos la tele para relajarnos.**
:::

::: example Possession and the personal a
**I know Pedro's brother, but I don't know his parents. I visited the teacher's house.**

1. *I know Pedro's brother* → *conocer* + a person, so personal *a*; possession with *de*: **Conozco al hermano de Pedro** (*a + el = al*).
2. *I don't know his parents* → still people: **no conozco a sus padres**.
3. *I visited the teacher's house* → a house is a thing, so no personal *a*; *de + el = del*: **Visité la casa del profesor**.

**Conozco al hermano de Pedro, pero no conozco a sus padres. Visité la casa del profesor.**
:::

::: example Working out unknown words from suffixes
A Reading text says: *Mi hermanito dice que el examen fue facilísimo, pero normalmente la seguridad en el instituto es muy importante y los problemas no son evitables.*

1. *hermanito* → *hermano* + *-ito*: little brother.
2. *facilísimo* → *fácil* + *-ísimo*: very easy. The accent moves to *-ísimo*.
3. *normalmente* → *normal* + *-mente*: normally, usually.
4. *seguridad* → *seguro* minus *-o* + *-idad*: safety, security.
5. *evitables* → *evitar* + *-able*, plural: avoidable.

My little brother says the exam was very easy, but normally safety at school is very important and the problems are not avoidable.
:::

::: example Verbs that change meaning with a preposition
1. *Llegué a las ocho* → I arrived at eight. *Llegué a entender el problema* → I managed to understand the problem.
2. *Dejé el libro en casa* → I left the book at home. *Dejé de comer carne* → I stopped eating meat.
3. *Volví a casa* → I went back home. *Volví a leer el libro* → I read the book again.
4. *Mi hermana se parece a mi madre* → My sister looks like my mother.
:::

::: q
Add the personal *a* where it is needed: *Veo ___ mi amigo. Veo ___ la película. Tengo ___ tres primos.*
---
*Veo a mi amigo.* *Veo la película* (a thing). *Tengo tres primos* (no *a* after *tener*).
:::

Translate into Spanish: *my father's car*.
---
*el coche de mi padre*.
:::

::: q
Spot and correct the error: *Después de hacer los deberes, voy a salir sin comiendo.*
---
*sin comer*: an infinitive after a preposition.
:::

::: q
Fill with *por* or *para*: *Voy a la playa ___ la mañana. Este regalo es ___ ti. Estudio ___ aprobar.*
---
*por* la mañana; *para* ti; *para* aprobar (purpose).
:::

::: q
Form the adverb: *fácil, lento, alegre, tranquilo*.
---
*fácilmente, lentamente, alegremente, tranquilamente* (feminine form + *-mente*; the accent of *fácil* stays).
:::

::: q
What do these mean? *carísimo, casita, realidad, aceptable*.
---
very expensive; little house; reality; acceptable.
:::

*Trabajo los sábados para ganar dinero.*

::: watch
- **Personal *a* before people**: *Visito a mis abuelos*, *Llamo a mi amiga*. It is one of the most frequently missed words in the translation.
- **Infinitive after prepositions**: *antes de salir*, *sin pagar*, *para ser*; never *-ando / -iendo*.
- **No 's in Spanish**: *el libro de Carlos*, not *Carlos libro*.
- **de + el → del, a + el → al**: *la casa del vecino*, *voy al centro*. But *de él* (of him) does not contract.
- **-mente on the feminine**: *rápidamente*, not *rápidomente*.
- **Keep the verb's preposition**: *Aprendo a conducir*, *Dejé de fumar*, *Empiezo a trabajar*.
:::
