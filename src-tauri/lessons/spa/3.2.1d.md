## What it is

The **preterite** is the Spanish simple past: *I walked, she ate, we went*. Use it for a **completed action at a known, finished time**: *ayer, anoche, el sábado pasado, en 2023, hace dos años*. If you can put 'yesterday' or 'last week' in front of the English, the preterite is almost always right.

**Where it turns up.** The past is one of the three time frames every Writing task expects, and the 90- and 150-word tasks nearly always have a bullet point about something you **did** (a trip, a celebration, last weekend). The translation into Spanish usually has at least one preterite sentence, often with an irregular verb. In Reading and Listening, telling *habló* (he spoke) from *hablo* (I speak) and *fue* (went/was) from *va* (goes) is often the whole answer.

**The core structures.**
- **Regular endings**: *-ar* verbs take *-é, -aste, -ó, -amos, -asteis, -aron*; *-er* and *-ir* verbs share *-í, -iste, -ió, -imos, -isteis, -ieron*. The accents on *-é* and *-ó* matter: *hablo* = I speak, *habló* = he spoke.
- **ir and ser are identical** in the preterite: *fui, fuiste, fue, fuimos, fuisteis, fueron*. Context tells you which: *Fui a Madrid* (I went), *Fue divertido* (it was fun). **dar** takes *-er* endings without accents: *di, diste, dio, dimos, disteis, dieron*. **ver** does the same: *vi, vio*.
- **Irregular stems** with one set of unaccented endings (*-e, -iste, -o, -imos, -isteis, -ieron*): **tuv-** (tener), **pud-** (poder), **hic-** (hacer, with *hizo*), **vin-** (venir), **estuv-** (estar), **pus-** (poner), **quis-** (querer), **dij-** (decir) and **traj-** (traer), where the *ellos* form drops the *i*: *dijeron, trajeron*.

**Spelling changes.** To keep the sound, the *yo* form changes **c → qu** (*saqué, toqué, busqué*), **g → gu** (*llegué, jugué, pagué*) and **z → c** (*empecé, organicé*), and *leer*-type verbs take **y** in the third persons (*leyó, leyeron*). At Foundation these are not individually credited; at **Higher they are**, so learn them now. The same goes for *-ir* stem changes in the third persons (*pidió, durmió*), taught in the Higher verbs lesson.

**Grade 9** answers use a range of irregular preterites accurately (*tuve, hice, pude, estuve, vine*), mark the time clearly (*el verano pasado, hace un mes*), and link past events with *primero, luego, después, al final*.

::: terms
- **preterite**: the simple past for completed actions at a known time: *Ayer comí paella* = Yesterday I ate paella
- **time marker**: a phrase that signals the preterite: *ayer, anoche, la semana pasada, el año pasado, hace dos años*
- **hace + time**: 'ago': *hace tres días* = three days ago
- **irregular stem**: a new stem with unaccented endings: *tener → tuve*, *hacer → hice*, *decir → dije*
- **fui**: the *yo* preterite of both *ir* and *ser*: *Fui al cine* (I went), *Fui feliz* (I was happy)
- **hizo**: 'he/she did, made'; the *c* becomes *z* to keep the sound: *¿Qué hizo? = What did he do?*
- **spelling change**: a letter change in the *yo* form to keep the sound: *llegar → llegué*, *sacar → saqué*, *empezar → empecé*
- **written accent**: the mark on *-é* and *-ó* that separates *hablé* (I spoke) and *habló* (he spoke) from present forms
:::

::: method
1. **Check the time frame**: a finished event at a known time → preterite.
2. **Is the verb on the irregular list?** *ir/ser → fu-*; *dar → di*; *tener → tuv-*, *poder → pud-*, *hacer → hic-/hiz-*, *venir → vin-*, *estar → estuv-*, *poner → pus-*, *querer → quis-*, *decir → dij-*, *traer → traj-*. Add *-e, -iste, -o, -imos, -isteis, -ieron* with **no accents** (*-eron* after *j*).
3. **Regular?** Remove *-ar/-er/-ir* and add the right set of endings.
4. **Yo form of a verb ending -car, -gar, -zar?** Apply *qu, gu, c*: *toqué, jugué, empecé*.
5. **Put the accents on** *-é* and *-ó* for regular verbs, and check the person matches the subject.
6. **Place a time marker** at the start or end of the sentence.
:::

| | visitar | comer | salir | tener | ir / ser | dar |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| yo | visité | comí | salí | tuve | fui | di |
| tú | visitaste | comiste | saliste | tuviste | fuiste | diste |
| él / ella | visitó | comió | salió | tuvo | fue | dio |
| nosotros | visitamos | comimos | salimos | tuvimos | fuimos | dimos |
| vosotros | visitasteis | comisteis | salisteis | tuvisteis | fuisteis | disteis |
| ellos | visitaron | comieron | salieron | tuvieron | fueron | dieron |

::: example Forming irregular preterites step by step
**hacer, he/she form**: stem *hic-* + *-o* would give *hico*, which would be pronounced with a hard *k*. The spelling changes to keep the *th/s* sound: **hizo**.

**decir, they form**: stem *dij-* + *-ieron*, but after *j* the *i* is dropped: **dijeron**. Same for *traer*: **trajeron**.

**estar, I form**: stem *estuv-* + *-e*: **estuve**, no accent. Compare the regular *hablé*, which has one.

**poder, we form**: *pud-* + *-imos*: **pudimos**. Note that *nosotros* forms of *-ar* and *-ir* verbs look the same as the present (*hablamos, vivimos*); the time marker tells you which.
:::

::: example Translation into Spanish
**Last summer I went to Spain with my family. We visited Seville and my brother played football on the beach.**

1. *Last summer* → **El verano pasado**.
2. *I went* → *ir*, *yo*: **fui**.
3. *with my family* → **con mi familia**. *to Spain* → **a España**.
4. *We visited* → regular *-ar*: **visitamos**.
5. *my brother played* → *jugar*, *él*: **jugó** (the *yo* form would be *jugué*).
6. *football* → **al fútbol**; *on the beach* → **en la playa**.

**El verano pasado fui a España con mi familia. Visitamos Sevilla y mi hermano jugó al fútbol en la playa.**
:::

::: example Rewriting a paragraph in the past
Present: *Llego al instituto a las ocho. Tengo clase de inglés y hago un examen. Mis amigos vienen a mi casa y vemos una película.*

1. *Llego* → *yo*, *-gar* verb: **Llegué**.
2. *Tengo* → **Tuve**; *hago* → **hice**.
3. *vienen* → *ellos*, stem *vin-*: **vinieron**.
4. *vemos* → *ver*, *nosotros*: **vimos**.
5. Add a time marker: *Ayer*.

**Ayer llegué al instituto a las ocho. Tuve clase de inglés e hice un examen. Mis amigos vinieron a mi casa y vimos una película.** (*y* becomes *e* before a word starting with an *i* sound: *e hice*.)
:::

::: q
Give the preterite *yo* and *él* forms of: *comprar, beber, escribir*.
---
*compré / compró*; *bebí / bebió*; *escribí / escribió*.
:::

::: q
Fill the gaps: *El sábado pasado yo ___ (ir) al cine y mis padres ___ (quedarse) en casa.*
---
*fui*; *se quedaron*.
:::

::: q
Spot and correct the error: *Ayer estuví en casa y hicé los deberes.*
---
*estuve* and *hice*: irregular stems take endings with no accent.
:::

::: q
Translate into English: *Hace dos años mi hermana tuvo un accidente, pero no fue grave.*
---
Two years ago my sister had an accident, but it wasn't serious.
:::

::: q
Translate into Spanish: *They said nothing and brought a present.*
---
*No dijeron nada y trajeron un regalo.* (No *i* after *j*: *dijeron, trajeron*.)
:::

::: q
Is *fue* from *ir* or *ser* in (a) *Fue a la playa* (b) *Fue un día fantástico*?
---
(a) *ir*: he/she went to the beach. (b) *ser*: it was a fantastic day.
:::

::: q
Write the *yo* preterite of *tocar, pagar, empezar*.
---
*toqué, pagué, empecé* (c → qu, g → gu, z → c before *é*).
:::

::: q
Rewrite in the preterite with *anoche*: *Mi padre pone la mesa y yo preparo la cena.*
---
*Anoche mi padre puso la mesa y yo preparé la cena.*
:::

::: watch
- **Accents on regular *-é* and *-ó***: *compré, compró*. Without them the word can become a different tense (*compro* = I buy).
- **No accents on irregular stems**: *tuve, hice, estuve, puso, vino*.
- **ir/ser share forms**: *fui, fue, fueron* can mean 'went' or 'was/were'; read the rest of the sentence.
- **dijeron, trajeron**: no *i* after *j*.
- **Mixing up *hice* and *hizo***: *hice* = I did; *hizo* = he/she did.
- **Don't use the preterite for 'used to'**: *Jugaba* (used to play) is the imperfect.
:::
