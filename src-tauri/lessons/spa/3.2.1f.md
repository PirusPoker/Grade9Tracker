## What it is

This lesson covers the ways Spanish looks forward and tells people what to do: **ir a + infinitive** (*Voy a estudiar* = I'm going to study), the **future tense** (*Estudiaré* = I will study), the **conditional** (*Estudiaría* = I would study) and **tú commands** (*¡Estudia!* = Study!).

**Where it turns up.** The future is the third time frame that every 90- and 150-word task rewards, and there is almost always a bullet point about plans (*next summer, when you leave school, in the future*). The conditional lets you say what you *would* do, which is a quick Higher-looking structure. Commands turn up in role-plays, instructions in Reading texts and adverts, and occasionally in the translation.

**The core structures.**
- **ir a + infinitive** works in **every person** at Foundation: *voy a, vas a, va a, vamos a, vais a, van a* + infinitive. It translates both 'going to' and 'will'.
- **The future tense** adds endings to the **whole infinitive**: *-é, -ás, -á* (Foundation: singular only). Four irregular stems are on the Foundation list: **tendr-** (tener), **har-** (hacer), **podr-** (poder), **pondr-** (poner), plus **habrá** (there will be).
- **The conditional** adds *-ía, -ías, -ía* to the same stems: *tendría, haría, podría, pondría*, plus **habría** (there would be).
- The plural forms (*estudiaremos, iríamos*) and five more irregular stems (*sabr-, querr-, vendr-, dir-, saldr-*) are Higher-only and are in the Higher verbs lesson.
- **Positive tú commands**: use the *él/ella* present form (*¡Habla!, ¡Come!, ¡Escribe!, ¡Vuelve!, ¡Piensa!*). Eight are irregular and on the list: **sé** (ser), **ve** (ir), **ten** (tener), **ven** (venir), **haz** (hacer), **di** (decir), **pon** (poner), **sal** (salir). A single pronoun is stuck on the end, with an accent if the stress needs it: *¡Míralo!, ¡Dame!, ¡Lávate!*

Negative commands (*don't ...*) use a different form that is not on the AQA list, so you will not be asked to produce them.

**Grade 9** answers mix *ir a* with the true future and a conditional (*Voy a estudiar ciencias, luego iré a la universidad y me gustaría ser médico*), use irregular stems accurately, and add a condition with *si* + present: *Si hace sol, iré a la playa.*

::: terms
- **near future**: *ir a* + infinitive, 'going to': *Voy a jugar al fútbol* = I'm going to play football
- **future tense**: infinitive + *-é, -ás, -á*, 'will': *Viajaré* = I will travel, *Comerá* = she will eat
- **conditional**: infinitive + *-ía, -ías, -ía*, 'would': *Viviría en Madrid* = I would live in Madrid
- **irregular future stem**: a shortened stem used in the future and the conditional: *tendr-, har-, podr-, pondr-*
- **habrá / habría**: 'there will be' and 'there would be', from *hay*: *Habrá más coches*
- **imperative**: a command; a positive *tú* command uses the *él* present form: *¡Escucha!* = Listen!
- **irregular tú command**: one of eight short forms: *sé, ve, ten, ven, haz, di, pon, sal*
- **si + present, future**: a real condition: *Si tengo dinero, compraré un móvil* = If I have money, I'll buy a phone
:::

::: method
1. **Choose the future form.** A plan or intention → *ir a* + infinitive. A prediction or 'will' → the future. 'Would' → the conditional. All three are correct for most plans, so vary them.
2. **ir a**: conjugate *ir* in the present, add **a**, then the infinitive: *vamos a ver*.
3. **Future**: take the whole infinitive and add *-é, -ás, -á*. For *tener, hacer, poder, poner*, use the short stem: *tendré, haré, podré, pondré*.
4. **Conditional**: same stem, add *-ía, -ías, -ía*: *jugaría, tendría*.
5. **Command to a friend**: *él* present form, or one of the eight irregulars. Add a pronoun to the end if needed, and put an accent on the syllable that was stressed before: *compra → cómpralo*.
:::

::: example Three ways to talk about the future
**Next year I'm going to study history. After that I will go to university and I would like to work abroad.**

1. *Next year* → **El año que viene** (or *El próximo año*).
2. *I'm going to study* → *ir a*: **voy a estudiar historia**.
3. *After that* → **Después**.
4. *I will go* → future of *ir*, whole infinitive + *-é*: **iré a la universidad**.
5. *I would like to work* → the fixed phrase **me gustaría trabajar** (conditional of *gustar*).
6. *abroad* → **en el extranjero**.

**El año que viene voy a estudiar historia. Después iré a la universidad y me gustaría trabajar en el extranjero.**
:::

::: example Irregular stems in context
**If I have time, I will do the shopping and my sister will set the table.**

1. *If I have* → *si* + present, never future after *si*: **Si tengo tiempo**.
2. *I will do* → *hacer* has the stem *har-*: **haré la compra**.
3. *my sister will set* → *poner la mesa*, stem *pondr-*, *ella* ending *-á*: **mi hermana pondrá la mesa**.

**Si tengo tiempo, haré la compra y mi hermana pondrá la mesa.**
:::

::: example The conditional for what you would do
**In an ideal world there would be no poverty and I would have a big house.**

1. *In an ideal world* → **En un mundo ideal**.
2. *there would be no poverty* → **no habría pobreza**.
3. *I would have* → *tendr-* + *-ía*: **tendría una casa grande**.

**En un mundo ideal no habría pobreza y tendría una casa grande.**
:::

::: example Commands with and without pronouns
A parent's instructions to a teenager:

1. *Come here!* → *venir*, irregular: **¡Ven aquí!**
2. *Do your homework!* → *hacer*: **¡Haz los deberes!**
3. *Put it on the table!* (the phone) → *pon* + *lo*, one syllable, so no accent: **¡Ponlo en la mesa!**
4. *Call your grandmother!* → *llama* + *la*: stress on *lla*, now third from last, so **¡Llámala!**
5. *Get up!* → *levanta* + *te*: **¡Levántate!**
6. *Be good!* → *ser*: **¡Sé bueno!** The accent separates *sé* (be / I know) from *se* (the pronoun).
:::

::: watch
- **Future endings go on the whole infinitive**: *hablaré*, not *hablé* (that is the preterite).
- **Irregular stems**: *tendré, haré, podré, pondré*; *teneré, haceré, poderé* are always wrong.
- **Accents**: every future form except *nosotros* has one (*hablaré, hablarás, hablará*); every conditional form has one on the *í*.
- **Don't forget the *a*** in *ir a*: *Voy a jugar*, not *Voy jugar*.
- **No future after *si***: *Si llueve, me quedaré en casa*.
- **Commands with pronouns** usually need an accent: *¡Cómpralo! ¡Escúchame!*, but not the one-syllable irregulars: *¡Dime! ¡Hazlo! ¡Ponlo!*
:::
