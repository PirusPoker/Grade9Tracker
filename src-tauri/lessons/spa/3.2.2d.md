## What it is

This **Higher-only** lesson gathers the multi-verb structures and phrases that turn correct Spanish into impressive Spanish. Most of them do **not** translate word for word, which is exactly why AQA tests them in the translation into Spanish:

- **acabar de + infinitive** = 'have just done': *Acabo de comer* = I have just eaten.
- **desde hace + present tense** = 'have been doing for': *Estudio español desde hace tres años* = I have been studying Spanish for three years.
- **llevar + time + present participle** = the same idea: *Llevo tres años estudiando español.*
- **seguir + present participle** = 'still / keep on doing': *Sigo viviendo en Leeds* = I'm still living in Leeds.
- **The passive**: **ser + past participle + por** (*La iglesia fue construida por los romanos*) and **se + 3rd person** (*Se habla español*, *Se venden entradas*).
- **Impersonal verbs** on the list: *parece, basta, falta, hace falta, vale la pena*.
- **lo + adjective** as a noun: *lo bueno* (the good thing), *lo malo, lo mejor, lo peor, lo importante*.
- **Superlatives**: *el más alto de la clase*, *la peor película*, and superlative adverbs: *Es el que corre más rápido*, *lo antes posible*.
- **Higher negatives**: **ya no** (no longer), **(no) tampoco** (not ... either / neither), **(no) ni ... ni** (neither ... nor).

**Where it turns up.** A 150-word answer that includes *desde hace*, *acabo de*, *lo mejor es que* and *ya no* hits several Higher structures in a few words. The translation into Spanish tests 'have been -ing for' and 'have just' every year across the languages; English speakers instinctively use the wrong tense. In Reading and Listening, *ya no* and *tampoco* change the meaning completely and are favourite distractors.

**Grade 9** answers use these structures accurately and in the right tense: present with *desde hace* (never the perfect), the infinitive after *acabar de*, and agreement of the participle in the *ser* passive.

::: terms
- **acabar de + infinitive**: 'to have just done': *Mi hermano acaba de llegar* = My brother has just arrived
- **desde hace**: with the present tense, 'have been -ing for': *Vivo aquí desde hace diez años*
- **llevar + time + participle**: 'to have been -ing for': *Llevo dos horas esperando* = I've been waiting for two hours
- **seguir + participle**: 'to still be / keep on -ing': *Sigue lloviendo* = It's still raining
- **passive with ser**: *ser* + past participle (agreeing) + *por*: *Las fotos fueron sacadas por mi padre*
- **passive with se**: *se* + 3rd person verb, singular or plural to match the noun: *Se venden móviles*
- **hace falta / vale la pena**: 'it's necessary' / 'it's worth it': *Hace falta dormir más*, *Vale la pena visitarlo*
- **lo + adjective**: 'the ... thing / part': *Lo bueno es que ...* = The good thing is that ...
- **superlative**: article + *más / menos* + adjective + *de*: *la ciudad más bonita de España*
- **ya no**: 'no longer, not any more': *Ya no juego al fútbol*
- **tampoco / ni ... ni**: 'not either' / 'neither ... nor': *No me gusta tampoco*; *No tengo ni perro ni gato*
:::

::: method
1. **'have just' + past participle in English?** → *acabar de* + infinitive, in the present: *acabamos de ver*.
2. **'have been -ing for + time'?** → **present tense** + *desde hace* + time, or *llevar* (present) + time + participle. Never the perfect.
3. **'still' + -ing?** → *seguir* + participle: *sigo estudiando*.
4. **'is/was done by'?** → *ser* in the right tense + participle agreeing with the subject + *por*. No agent and a general meaning? → *se* + verb agreeing with the noun.
5. **'the good / bad / best thing'?** → *lo* + masculine singular adjective: *lo mejor*.
6. **'the most / least ... in'?** → article + noun + *más / menos* + adjective + **de**: *el chico más alto de la clase*.
7. **Negative?** *ya no* before the verb; *tampoco* before the verb, or *no* + verb + *tampoco*; *no* + verb + *ni ... ni*.
:::

::: example 'have been -ing for' and 'have just'
**I have been learning Spanish for four years and I have just passed an exam.**

1. *I have been learning ... for four years* → English uses a perfect tense, Spanish uses the **present**: **Aprendo español desde hace cuatro años**. Alternative: **Llevo cuatro años aprendiendo español**.
2. *I have just passed* → *acabar de* + infinitive: **acabo de aprobar un examen**.

**Aprendo español desde hace cuatro años y acabo de aprobar un examen.**

A common wrong answer: *He aprendido español por cuatro años.* It uses the wrong tense and the wrong preposition.
:::

::: example The passive two ways
**The castle was built in the twelfth century. Today it is visited by many tourists, and tickets are sold online.**

1. *was built* → *ser* in the preterite + participle agreeing with *el castillo* (m sg): **fue construido**. *In the twelfth century*: **en el siglo doce** (or *XII*).
2. *it is visited by many tourists* → present of *ser*, agreeing, with *por*: **es visitado por muchos turistas**.
3. *tickets are sold online* → no agent, general: *se* + plural verb to match *entradas*: **se venden entradas online**.

**El castillo fue construido en el siglo doce. Hoy es visitado por muchos turistas y se venden entradas online.**
:::

::: example lo + adjective, superlatives and negatives in an opinion
**The best thing about my town is that it is the prettiest in the region. The bad thing is that there is no longer a cinema, and there isn't a theatre either.**

1. *The best thing* → **Lo mejor de mi pueblo**.
2. *it is the prettiest in the region* → superlative with *de* for 'in': **es el más bonito de la región** (agreeing with *pueblo*).
3. *The bad thing* → **Lo malo**.
4. *there is no longer a cinema* → **ya no hay cine**.
5. *there isn't a theatre either* → **y tampoco hay teatro** (or *y no hay teatro tampoco*).

**Lo mejor de mi pueblo es que es el más bonito de la región. Lo malo es que ya no hay cine y tampoco hay teatro.**
:::

::: example Impersonal verbs from the Higher list
1. **Parece que** va a llover. = It looks as if it's going to rain.
2. **Basta** con estudiar una hora al día. = It's enough to study an hour a day.
3. **Falta** una semana para las vacaciones. = There's a week to go until the holidays.
4. **Hace falta** más transporte público. = More public transport is needed.
5. **Vale la pena** visitar Granada. = It's worth visiting Granada.
:::

::: watch
- **desde hace + present**: *Vivo aquí desde hace dos años*. Using *he vivido* or *por* is the classic error.
- **acabar de + infinitive, in the present** for 'have just': *Acabo de comer*, not *He acabado de comer*.
- **Passive with ser: the participle agrees**: *fueron construidos*, *fue escrita*.
- **se passive agrees with the noun**: *Se vende la casa*, *Se venden casas*.
- **Superlative 'in' = de**: *el mejor jugador del equipo*.
- **lo, not el, with an adjective used as an idea**: *lo bueno*, *lo más importante*.
- **ya no and tampoco go before the verb** (or *no* + verb + *tampoco*): *Ya no fumo*.
:::
