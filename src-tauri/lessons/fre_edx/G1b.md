## What it is

This lesson covers the **determiners** that go in front of a noun (*ce, mon, quel, chaque, tout*) and the **Foundation pronouns** that replace a noun (*je, me, le, lui, se, moi, qui*). It is the second part of Appendix 2's first block. The Higher pronouns (*y, en, les, leur, eux, que, où, personne ne, rien ne*) are in G1c.

Where it earns marks:
- **Paper 3 (Reading):** *sa mère* can mean **his** mother, and *je lui parle* can mean I talk to **her**. Questions often ask "who does what?", and only the pronoun or the possessive tells you.
- **Paper 4 (Writing):** Q3's translation nearly always has an object pronoun ("I listen to her in the evening"), and pronouns are part of Pearson's "complex language" in Q1 and Q2.
- **Paper 1 (Speaking):** *Quel est ton avis ?* and *Quelle est ta matière préférée ?* are the questions you hear, and *avec eux*, *chez moi* and *qui* clauses make answers fluent.

**1. Ce, cet, cette, ces (this, that, these, those).**

| masculine | masculine before a vowel or silent h | feminine | plural |
|---|---|---|---|
| ce film | cet ami, cet hôtel, cet été | cette ville | ces livres |

*Cet* sounds exactly like *cette*, so in the dictation the noun's gender decides the spelling.

**2. Possessive adjectives.** They agree with the **thing owned**, not with the owner.

| owner | masculine | feminine | plural |
|---|---|---|---|
| my | mon | ma | mes |
| your (tu) | ton | ta | tes |
| his, her, its | son | sa | ses |
| our | notre | notre | nos |
| your (vous) | votre | votre | vos |
| their | leur | leur | leurs |

- **His or her?** *Sa mère* = his mother **or** her mother, because *mère* is feminine. *Son frère* = his brother **or** her brother. The context tells you who the owner is.
- **Before a feminine noun starting with a vowel**, use *mon, ton, son* for the sound: *mon amie, ton école, son histoire*.
- *Leur* (their) has a plural *leurs*: *leur maison, leurs enfants*.

**3. Quel, quelle, quels, quelles (which, what).** *Quel* agrees with its noun: *Quel âge as-tu ? Quelle heure est-il ? Quels sports aimes-tu ? Quelles matières préfères-tu ?* It can be separated from its noun by *être*: *Quelle est ta matière préférée ?* (*matière* is feminine). It also makes exclamations: *Quelle bonne idée !* All four forms sound the same, so in the dictation spell it to match the noun.

**4. Indefinite adjectives.**
- **chaque** (each, every) never changes and takes a singular noun: *chaque jour, chaque semaine*.
- **plusieurs** (several) never changes and takes a plural noun: *plusieurs amis, plusieurs fois*.
- **autre(s)** (other) adds -s in the plural: *un autre film, une autre idée, les autres élèves*. "Other" with no article in English becomes *d'autres*: *j'ai d'autres amis*.
- **tout, toute, tous, toutes** (all, every, the whole) agree with the noun and are followed by the article: *tout le temps, toute la journée, tous les jours, toutes les semaines*. *Tout le monde* = everyone (singular verb). As a pronoun, *tous* sounds its -s: *Ils sont tous venus*.

**5. Subject pronouns.** *je, tu, il, elle, on, nous, vous, ils, elles.*
- *Tu* is for one friend or family member; *vous* is for one adult you don't know well, or for more than one person.
- *Ils* is for a group of men or a mixed group; *elles* only for an all-female group.
- *On* means we, people, you or one, and takes the **il** form of the verb: *On va au cinéma ce soir* (We're going to the cinema tonight). *En France, on mange tard* (In France people eat late).

**6. Object pronouns (Foundation).** They replace a noun and go **in front of the verb**.

| | direct object (the thing or person the verb acts on) | indirect object (to / for someone) |
|---|---|---|
| me | me (m') | me (m') |
| you (tu) | te (t') | te (t') |
| him, it | le (l') | lui |
| her, it | la (l') | lui |
| us | nous | nous |
| you (vous) | vous | vous |

*Les* (them) and *leur* (to them) are on the Higher list: see G1c.

- **Direct:** *Je regarde le film* → *Je **le** regarde.* *Il aime Sarah* → *Il **l'**aime.*
- **Indirect** with verbs that take *à* + a person: *donner à, parler à, téléphoner à, envoyer à, dire à, écrire à, demander à, montrer à*. *Je parle à mon père* → *Je **lui** parle.* *Lui* means **to him or to her**.
- **Position:**
  - present: *Je **la** vois.* Negative: *Je ne **la** vois pas.*
  - perfect: before the auxiliary: *Je **l'**ai vu(e).* *Je ne **lui** ai pas parlé.* (The participle agrees with a preceding direct object: *je l'ai vue* for "her". That rule is G3b.)
  - with a modal or *aller* + infinitive: before the **infinitive**: *Je vais **le** voir. Je peux **t'**aider. Je ne veux pas **lui** parler.*
- **Never two object pronouns together.** Edexcel's list does not include pairs, so keep the noun: *Je l'ai envoyé à mon père*, not *je le lui ai envoyé*. *Il me donne un livre*, not *il me le donne*.
- **Elision:** *me, te, le, la, se* become *m', t', l', s'* before a vowel or silent h: *il m'aide, je t'écoute, je l'adore, elle s'appelle*.

**7. Reflexive pronouns.** *me, te, se, nous, vous, se* go before the verb and match the subject: *je me lève, tu te couches, elle s'habille, nous nous amusons, vous vous reposez, ils se disputent*. The pronoun changes with the subject even after another verb: *je veux me reposer* (not *se reposer*). Reflexive verbs are taught fully in G2b.

**8. Emphatic pronouns (Foundation).** *moi, toi, lui, elle, nous, vous, elles.* (*Eux* is Higher: G1c.) Use them:
- **after a preposition:** *chez moi, avec elle, pour toi, sans lui, à côté de nous*;
- **for emphasis or contrast:** *Moi, je préfère le thé. Lui, il aime le café.*
- **after c'est, et and que:** *C'est moi. Mon frère et moi, nous jouons au foot. Elle est plus grande que moi.*

Do not mix them up with object pronouns: *je lui parle* (I speak to him), but *je parle avec lui* (I speak with him).

**9. The relative pronoun qui (who, which, that).** *Qui* is the **subject** of the next verb and joins two sentences: *J'ai une sœur. Elle s'appelle Jade.* → *J'ai une sœur **qui** s'appelle Jade.* *C'est un film **qui** est très drôle.* *Qui* works for people **and** things, and it **never** elides: *un ami qui habite à Paris*, *un livre qui est intéressant*. The verb agrees with the noun *qui* replaces: *mes amis qui jouent au foot*. (*Que* and *où* are Higher: G1c.)

::: terms
- **ce / cet / cette / ces**: this, that, these; cet before a masculine noun starting with a vowel: cet été
- **l'adjectif possessif**: mon, ma, mes etc.; agrees with the thing owned, not with the owner
- **son / sa / ses**: his, her or its; sa mère can mean his mother or her mother
- **quel / quelle / quels / quelles**: which, what; agrees with the noun: Quelle est ta matière préférée ?
- **tout / toute / tous / toutes**: all, every, whole; agrees and is followed by the article: tous les jours
- **chaque / plusieurs**: each (+ singular noun) / several (+ plural noun); neither ever changes
- **on**: we, people, you, one; takes the il form of the verb: on mange à midi
- **le pronom complément d'objet direct**: me, te, le, la, nous, vous; replaces the object, before the verb
- **le pronom complément d'objet indirect**: me, te, lui, nous, vous = to/for me etc.; lui is to him or to her
- **le pronom réfléchi**: me, te, se, nous, vous, se; matches the subject: je veux me reposer
- **le pronom tonique**: moi, toi, lui, elle, nous, vous, elles; after prepositions: chez moi, avec elle
- **qui**: who, which, that; the subject of the next verb, never shortened to qu'
:::

::: method
1. **Determiner in front of a noun?** Find the noun's gender and number first, then choose *ce/cet/cette/ces*, *mon/ma/mes*, *quel/quelle/quels/quelles* or *tout/toute/tous/toutes*.
2. **His or her?** Ignore the owner. *Son / sa / ses* agree with the noun that follows.
3. **Replacing a noun?** Is it the direct object (no *à*) or the indirect object (*à* + a person)? Direct: *le / la / l'*. Indirect: *lui*.
4. **Place the pronoun.** Before the conjugated verb; in the perfect, before *avoir / être*; with *aller, vouloir, pouvoir, devoir* + infinitive, before the infinitive. In a negative, *ne* goes before the pronoun.
5. **One object pronoun only.** If there are two objects, keep one of them as a noun.
6. **After a preposition, use an emphatic pronoun** (*avec moi, chez elle*), never *me* or *le*.
7. **Joining two sentences** where the second starts with *il / elle / ils*? Use *qui* and drop the pronoun.
8. **Reading:** for every *le, la, lui, son, sa*, ask "who or what does this refer to?" before you answer.
:::

::: example His or her: reading for the owner (2)
*Lucas a une sœur, Inès. Sa chambre est petite, mais son vélo est neuf.*

Whose bedroom is small? Whose bike is new?

*Sa chambre* and *son vélo* both just mean "the owner's". Feminine *sa* is there because *chambre* is feminine, not because Inès is a girl. Out of context, the bedroom and bike could belong to Lucas or Inès. In an exam text the next sentence will tell you, for example *Elle adore sa chambre* or *Il prend son vélo pour aller au collège*. Never use the gender of *son / sa* to decide the owner.
:::

::: example Replacing nouns with pronouns, in three tenses (6)
Rewrite each sentence, replacing the words in bold with a pronoun.
(a) Je regarde **la télé** le soir. (b) J'ai vu **mon cousin** hier. (c) Je vais téléphoner **à ma tante**. (d) Nous ne parlons pas **à notre voisin**. (e) Il a donné **un cadeau** à son père. (f) Tu veux inviter **Hugo** ?

(a) *Je la regarde le soir.* (1) Direct, feminine.
(b) *Je l'ai vu hier.* (1) Before the auxiliary; *le* elides.
(c) *Je vais lui téléphoner.* (1) *Téléphoner à*, so indirect; before the infinitive.
(d) *Nous ne lui parlons pas.* (1) *Ne* goes before the pronoun.
(e) *Il l'a donné à son père.* (1) Replace one object only, never both.
(f) *Tu veux l'inviter ?* (1) Before the infinitive.
:::

::: example Agreement of determiners (4)
Translate into French: (a) every day (b) all week (c) several times (d) Which films do you like?

(a) *tous les jours* (or *chaque jour*) (1)
(b) *toute la semaine* (1): *toute* agrees with *semaine*.
(c) *plusieurs fois* (1)
(d) *Quels films aimes-tu ?* / *Tu aimes quels films ?* (1): *quels* agrees with *films*.
:::

::: example Translation into French with pronouns and qui (2)
Translate into French: "I have a friend who lives in Paris. I often phone her, and next summer I am going to visit her."

1. "a friend who lives": *une amie qui habite* (or *un ami*). *Qui* is the subject of *habite*.
2. "phone her": *téléphoner à*, so the indirect pronoun **lui**, before the verb: *je lui téléphone souvent*.
3. "visit her": *rendre visite à* takes **lui** too; *visiter* is for places. Before the infinitive: *je vais lui rendre visite*.

**Answer:** *J'ai une amie qui habite à Paris. Je lui téléphone souvent, et l'été prochain, je vais lui rendre visite.*
:::

---

::: watch
- **Son / sa / ses agree with the thing owned.** *Sa voiture* can be his car. A reading answer based on the gender of *sa* is a guess.
- **Lui = to him or to her.** Decide from context.
- **Direct or indirect?** *Téléphoner à, parler à, donner à, dire à* take *lui*; *regarder, écouter, attendre, chercher, aimer* take *le / la* (no *à* in French, whatever English does).
- **Position:** before the auxiliary in the perfect (*je l'ai vu*), but before the infinitive with *aller* or a modal (*je vais le voir*). *Je l'ai vais voir* and *je vais voir le* are both wrong.
- **One object pronoun at a time.** Pearson's list stops at single pronouns: *je l'ai donné à ma mère*.
- **Emphatic after a preposition**: *avec elle*, not *avec la*.
- **Cet / cette** and the four forms of *quel* sound alike: spell them from the noun's gender.
- **Qui never elides**: *un ami qui habite*, never *qu'habite*.
- **Reflexive pronoun matches the subject**: *je veux me coucher*. *Je veux se coucher* is a major error in Pearson's list.
:::

::: test
Translate the passage into English.

*Ma grand-mère habite près de chez nous. Je lui rends visite chaque dimanche et je l'aide dans le jardin. Elle me raconte des histoires de sa jeunesse, et moi, je lui montre mes photos. Tous les membres de ma famille l'adorent. Quelle femme extraordinaire ! Sans elle, notre vie serait très différente.* (10)
---
Holistic bands. 9–10: the meaning is fully transferred in natural English. 6–8: most of the meaning, with occasional wrong or missing words. 3–5: parts of the meaning, with pronouns or tenses confused. 1–2: isolated words or phrases.
**Model:** My grandmother lives near us. I visit her every Sunday and I help her in the garden. She tells me stories from her youth, and I show her my photos. All the members of my family love her. What an extraordinary woman! Without her, our life would be very different.
**Deciding details:** *près de chez nous* = near us (near our house); *je lui rends visite* = I visit her; *je l'aide* = I help her; *elle me raconte* = she tells me; *je lui montre* = I show her; *tous les membres* = all the members; *quelle femme* = what a woman; *sans elle* = without her; *serait* = would be.

::: test
Translate the paragraph into French.

This year, my best friend is in my class. Her name is Lucie and she lives with her parents. I phone her every evening and I help her with her homework. Last week, I saw her brother in town, but I didn't speak to him. She has several friends who play football, and everyone loves her. (10)
---

**Model:** *Cette année, ma meilleure amie est dans ma classe. Elle s'appelle Lucie et elle habite avec ses parents. Je lui téléphone tous les soirs et je l'aide avec ses devoirs.
