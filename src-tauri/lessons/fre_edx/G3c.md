## What it is

The imperfect tense (*l'imparfait*) is the second past tense. The perfect (G3b) tells you **what happened**; the imperfect sets the scene: **what used to happen, what was going on, and what things were like**. A strong past-tense answer in Paper 4 or the picture-task conversation uses both, and the reading paper often hinges on "used to" versus "now".

**What it means.** The imperfect covers:
- **habits** in the past: "used to" or "would": *Quand j'étais petit(e), je jouais au foot tous les samedis.* = When I was little, I used to play (would play) football every Saturday.
- **actions in progress**: "was / were -ing": *Je regardais la télé.* = I was watching TV.
- **description and states** in the past: weather, time, age, feelings, what someone or something was like: *Il faisait beau. J'avais dix ans. J'étais content(e).*

**What Edexcel lists.**

| Foundation + Higher: je, tu, il / elle / on (not formal *vous*) | Higher |
|---|---|
| -er verbs | the **plural** persons (*nous, vous, ils / elles*) of every verb in the first column |
| *choisir, partir, venir, ouvrir* and verbs like them | *connaître, écrire* and verbs like them, in all persons |
| *entendre, prendre, traduire* and verbs like them | |
| *aller, avoir* (including *avoir* + noun), *être, faire* | |

The imperfect of the modals (*je voulais, je pouvais, je devais*) is not on the list: not required, credited if correct. For "wanted to" or "had to" in a past narrative you have the Higher perfect: *j'ai voulu, j'ai dû* (G3b).

**1. Forming the imperfect.** Take the **nous form of the present**, remove *-ons*, and add the endings.

| Ending | jouer (nous jouons → jou-) | finir (nous finissons → finiss-) |
|---|---|---|
| je -ais | je jouais | je finissais |
| tu -ais | tu jouais | tu finissais |
| il / elle / on -ait | il jouait | elle finissait |
| nous -ions (Higher) | nous jouions | nous finissions |
| vous -iez (Higher) | vous jouiez | vous finissiez |
| ils / elles -aient (Higher) | ils jouaient | elles finissaient |

The *-ais, -ait* and *-aient* endings all sound the same ("ay"), so the dictation and Paper 4 test the spelling.

**Stems for every model:**

| Model | nous (present) | Imperfect stem | je form |
|---|---|---|---|
| -er: *regarder* | regardons | regard- | je regardais |
| *choisir* | choisissons | choisiss- | je choisissais |
| *partir* (and *sortir, dormir*) | partons | part- | je partais |
| *venir* | venons | ven- | je venais |
| *ouvrir* | ouvrons | ouvr- | j'ouvrais |
| *entendre* | entendons | entend- | j'entendais |
| *prendre* | prenons | pren- | je prenais |
| *traduire* (and *lire*) | traduisons | traduis- | je traduisais, je lisais |
| *aller* | allons | all- | j'allais |
| *avoir* | avons | av- | j'avais |
| *faire* | faisons | fais- | je faisais |
| *être* | sommes | **ét-** (the only exception) | j'étais |
| Higher: *connaître* | connaissons | connaiss- | je connaissais |
| Higher: *écrire* (and *décrire*) | écrivons | écriv- | j'écrivais |

**Spelling:** *manger* keeps its e before a (*je mangeais*, but *nous mangions*); *commencer* takes a cedilla before a (*je commençais*, but *nous commencions*). *Faisais* is spelt with *ai* but sounds like "fuh-zay".

**2. Set phrases you must have.**
- *c'était* = it was: *C'était génial. C'était ennuyeux.*
- *il y avait* = there was / there were: *Il y avait beaucoup de gens.*
- *il faisait* + weather: *Il faisait beau / chaud / froid.* Also *il pleuvait* (it was raining; not on the list, credited if correct).
- *j'avais* + age or *avoir* + noun: *Quand j'avais douze ans... J'avais faim. J'avais peur.*
- *j'étais* + feeling or description: *J'étais très content(e).*

For "it was" giving an opinion of an event, *c'était* is always the safe choice: *Le concert ? C'était génial !*

**3. Imperfect or perfect?**

| Imperfect | Perfect |
|---|---|
| a habit: "used to", "would" | a single completed action |
| an action in progress: "was -ing" | the action that interrupts it |
| description: weather, age, feelings, what it was like | a sequence of events: *d'abord, ensuite, puis, enfin* |
| time markers: *quand j'étais petit(e), avant, d'habitude, tous les jours, souvent, le samedi, pendant que* | time markers: *hier, un jour, samedi dernier, une fois, l'année dernière* |

The classic sentence uses both: the imperfect is the background, the perfect is the event.
- *Je regardais la télé quand ma mère est arrivée.* = I was watching TV when my mother arrived.
- *Il faisait beau, donc nous sommes allés à la plage.* = It was fine, so we went to the beach.
- *Pendant que je faisais mes devoirs, mon frère est sorti.* = While I was doing my homework, my brother went out. (*Pendant que* + imperfect is a Higher-sounding link.)

**"I was" is not always the imperfect.** "I was ill for a week" (a completed period) is *j'ai été malade pendant une semaine*. "I was tired, so I went to bed" (a state in the background) is *j'étais...*. If you can add "at the time", it is the imperfect.

**4. Then and now: the reading trap.** Paper 3 often contrasts the past with the present. The tense tells you which is which:
- *Avant, je mangeais beaucoup de sucre. Maintenant, je mange des fruits.* = I used to eat a lot of sugar; now I eat fruit.
- *Quand j'étais petite, j'habitais à la campagne, mais j'habite en ville depuis deux ans.*

A question asking what someone does **now** is answered by the present; an option describing the *-ais / -ait* verb is the distractor, and the other way round.

::: terms
- **l'imparfait**: the imperfect: habits (used to), actions in progress (was -ing) and description in the past
- **le radical de nous**: the imperfect stem is the present nous form minus -ons: nous prenons → je prenais
- **les terminaisons**: -ais, -ais, -ait, -ions, -iez, -aient; -ais, -ait and -aient sound the same
- **ét-**: the only irregular stem: j'étais, c'était, ils étaient
- **c'était**: it was, used for opinions and descriptions of the past: c'était génial
- **il y avait**: there was / there were: il y avait un grand jardin
- **il faisait**: weather in the past: il faisait beau, il faisait froid
- **l'action en cours**: was -ing, often interrupted by a perfect: je dormais quand il est arrivé
- **l'habitude**: used to / would: tous les étés, nous allions au bord de la mer
- **pendant que**: while, followed by the imperfect for the background action
- **avant / maintenant**: before / now: the classic reading contrast between imperfect and present
:::

::: method
1. **Decide what the verb is doing.** A habit, something in progress, or a description? Imperfect. A single event, a sequence or an interruption? Perfect.
2. **Look for time markers**: *quand j'étais petit(e), d'habitude, tous les jours, avant* point to the imperfect; *hier, un jour, samedi dernier*, or "suddenly" in the English, point to the perfect.
3. **Find the stem**: present *nous* form minus *-ons*. *Être* is *ét-*.
4. **Add the ending** for the subject: *-ais* (je, tu), *-ait* (il / elle / on), and at Higher *-ions, -iez, -aient*.
5. **Check the spelling changes**: *mangeais, commençais*; *faisais* with *ai*.
6. **In reading**, mark every *-ais / -ait / -aient* verb as "used to / was" and every present verb as "now". Answer the time frame the question asks about.
:::

::: example Forming the imperfect from the stem (4)
Write the imperfect: (a) je (prendre) (b) tu (choisir) (c) il (être) (d) on (manger)

(a) *je prenais* (1): *nous prenons* → *pren-*.
(b) *tu choisissais* (1): *nous choisissons* → *choisiss-*.
(c) *il était* (1): the one irregular stem.
(d) *on mangeait* (1): keep the e before a.
:::

::: example Higher: the plural persons (4)
Write the imperfect: (a) nous (aller) (b) vous (faire) (c) ils (avoir) (d) elles (écrire)

(a) *nous allions* (1) (b) *vous faisiez* (1) (c) *ils avaient* (1) (d) *elles écrivaient* (1): the *écriv-* stem from *nous écrivons*.
:::

::: example Imperfect or perfect in a story (5)
Put each verb into the imperfect or the perfect: *Samedi dernier, il (faire) beau, donc je (aller) au parc. Je (lire) un livre quand mon ami Hugo (arriver). C'(être) génial !*

*il faisait* (1): weather, description. *je suis allé(e)* (1): a single event. *je lisais* (1): in progress. *Hugo est arrivé* (1): the interruption. *C'était* (1): an opinion of the day.
:::

::: example Reading: used to versus now (2)
*Avant, je n'aimais pas le sport et je passais tout mon temps sur mon portable. Maintenant, je fais de la natation trois fois par semaine.*

(a) What did the writer use to do? (b) What does the writer do now?

(a) Spent all their time on their phone (1). Accept "didn't like sport" as part of the answer, but the activity is the phone. (b) Goes swimming three times a week (1). An option saying "used to go swimming" is the distractor: *fais* is present.
:::

::: example Translating "would" and "was" (3)
Translate into French: (a) Every summer we would go to the seaside. (b) I was ill, so I stayed at home. (c) While my sister was sleeping, I watched a film.

(a) *Tous les étés, nous allions au bord de la mer.* (1) "Would" for a past habit is the imperfect, not the conditional (Higher plural; accept *on allait*).
(b) *J'étais malade, donc je suis resté(e) à la maison.* (1) Background state, then the event.
(c) *Pendant que ma sœur dormait, j'ai regardé un film.* (1)
:::

::: q
Translate into French: "There was a big garden and it was great." (2)
---
*Il y avait un grand jardin et c'était génial.* (2) Accept *c'était super*. 1 = one error, such as *il y a eu*, *c'est* or *un jardin grand*.
:::

::: q
Translate into English: *Quand Yanis était petit, il allait au collège à vélo, mais maintenant il prend le bus.* (2)
---
When Yanis was little (young), he used to go to school by bike, but now he takes (catches) the bus. (2) 1 if "used to" is lost ("he went... he took") or the tenses are swapped.
:::

::: watch
- **Habits are imperfect**: *quand j'étais petit(e), je jouais*, not *j'ai joué*.
- **c'était and il y avait** for "it was" and "there was / were" in a description; not *c'est* or *il y a* in a past narrative.
- **"Was -ing" is the imperfect**: *je regardais*, never *j'étais regarder* (a major error, like *je suis regarder* in the present).
- **The interruption is perfect**: *je dormais quand ma mère est rentrée*. Two imperfects there lose the meaning of a sudden event.
- **Être is the only odd stem**: *j'étais*, not *j'êtais* or *je sommais*.
- **Silent endings**: *je jouais / il jouait / ils jouaient* sound alike, and *j'ai joué* sounds close. Check the subject and the time frame before you spell.
- **Spelling changes**: *je mangeais, je commençais*.
- **Reading: then versus now.** Answer the time frame in the question. "Used to" answers come from imperfect verbs; "now" answers from the present.
:::

Translate the passage into English.

---
Holistic bands. 9–10: the meaning is fully transferred in natural English. 6–8: most of the meaning, with occasional wrong or missing words. 3–5: parts of the meaning, with the imperfect and perfect confused or phrases missing. 1–2: isolated words or phrases.
**Model:** When I was little, I lived (used to live) in a small village. There wasn't a cinema, but there was a beautiful forest. Every summer, my grandparents would come (used to come) to our house and we would walk (went walking) together in the forest. One day, while we were walking, my brother fell and hurt his arm. It was a difficult moment, and we quickly went home (went home quickly).
**Deciding details:** *il n'y avait pas de* = there wasn't (a); *chaque été* + *venaient* = every summer... would come / used to come; *chez nous* = to our house; *pendant que nous marchions* = while we were walking; *est tombé* and *s'est blessé* = single events (fell, hurt himself); *vite rentrés* = quickly went home (accept "hurried home").
