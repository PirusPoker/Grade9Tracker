## What it is

**My personal world** is the first of Edexcel's six thematic contexts, covering **family, friends, relationships and equality**. Edexcel's vocabulary list is arranged by part of speech, not by theme, so this lesson gathers the listed words for this context and flags the **Higher only** ones.

**Where it turns up in 1FR1.**
- **Paper 1 Speaking:** a **read-aloud card** can be set on this context. If you pick it for the **picture task**, you describe a photo of family or friends, answer two questions (the second in the past), then hold a 5-minute conversation starting on a set subject such as friends or daily life.
- **Papers 2 and 3:** blogs, emails, forums and podcasts about families, friendship and fairness, with questions in English.
- **Paper 4 Writing:** Q1 (to a friend, 80–90 words) could be about your best friend or your family; Q2 (an article, 130–150 words) could ask what makes a good friend or the pros and cons of living alone; the Q3 translation loves family sentences because they test possessives, agreements and *depuis*.

**Describing people.** Adjectives agree with the person described, not with you. The list gives you a solid core; words outside it still earn **equal credit** in your own speaking and writing, and in reading texts they will be glossed or have to be inferred.

| | On the list | Useful extras (fine in your own work) |
|---|---|---|
| Looks | *grand, petit, gros/grosse, jeune, vieux/vieille, âgé* (H), *beau/belle, blond, châtain, roux, les cheveux longs/courts, les yeux bleus/verts/marron, porter des lunettes* | *mince, joli* |
| Character | *sympa, amusant, patient, calme, sérieux, sportif, travailleur/travailleuse, responsable, indépendant, strict*; Higher *sévère, fidèle, agaçant, fier/fière* | *drôle, gentil(le), bavard, timide, têtu, généreux* |
| Feelings | *content, heureux, triste, stressé* (H), *inquiet/inquiète* (H) | *fâché, jaloux* |

Watch the patterns: *sérieux → sérieuse*, *travailleur → travailleuse*, *sportif → sportive*, *fier → fière*, *vieux → vieille*, *beau → belle* (and *bel* before a vowel: *un bel homme*). *Marron* never changes; *sympa* only adds -s.

**Family.** *le père, la mère, le frère, la sœur, le demi-frère, la demi-sœur* (half- or step-brother/sister), *le fils, la fille, le mari, la femme, les parents, l'enfant unique* (only child), *l'oncle, la tante*, the prefixes *beau-/belle-* (step-: *mon beau-père*) and *grand-* (*ma grand-mère*), and Higher *le jumeau / la jumelle / les jumeaux* (twin) and *aîné(e)* (older, oldest: *ma sœur aînée*).

**Getting on (or not).** *s'entendre bien/mal avec* (to get on well/badly with), Higher *se disputer* (to argue), *aider, écouter, partager, respecter*, Higher *soutenir* (to support), *compter sur* (to count on), *se fier à* (to trust), *ressembler à* (to look like), *s'occuper de* (to look after), *se séparer* (to separate). The noun *la confiance* (trust) is Higher: *j'ai confiance en elle*, *je lui fais confiance*. Reflexive verbs work **reciprocally** in the plural: *nous nous entendons bien* (we get on well with each other), *ils se disputent* (they argue with each other).

**Friendship and relationships.** *un ami / une amie*, Higher synonyms *un copain / une copine* (which can also mean boyfriend / girlfriend), Higher *l'amitié* (friendship). *Un bon ami, c'est quelqu'un qui...* is your all-purpose opener, using *qui* as the subject. For relationships: *le couple, le/la partenaire, se marier, le mariage, habiter seul(e)*. (*Vivre* is Higher vocabulary but its present forms are irregular and not on the grammar list, so *habiter* is the safer verb.)

**Equality.** The list is generous here. Foundation: *l'égalité, égal, le droit* (*les droits de l'enfant*), *le racisme, le sexisme, la violence, la victime, lutter contre, respecter, le respect, accepter, l'identité, le genre, transgenre, non-binaire, bisexuel, gay, lesbien, hétéro, handicapé, le fauteuil roulant, la religion*. Higher: *la discrimination, le harcèlement* (harassment, bullying), *la communauté, la minorité, tolérer, juger, défendre, le soutien* (support), *la liberté, la justice, la loi* (law). The prefix *in-* (G7) builds *l'inégalité* (inequality) from *l'égalité*.

**Inclusive language.** Pearson's spec says it equally credits non-binary pronouns (such as *iel*) and masculine, feminine or non-binary agreements in speaking and writing, as long as they are used correctly and consistently.

**Grammar in context.** Possessives agree with the thing owned (*sa mère* = his **or** her mother; *mon amie* before a vowel); reflexive pronouns match the subject, even after a modal (*je ne veux pas me disputer*); *depuis* + present for "have known for" (*je la connais depuis six ans*); and the imperfect for childhood (*quand j'étais petit(e), je partageais une chambre avec mon frère*).

::: terms
- **s'entendre avec**: to get on with; je m'entends bien avec ma mère, nous nous entendons mal
- **se disputer (avec)**: Higher; to argue (with); ils se disputent tout le temps
- **le demi-frère / la demi-sœur**: half-brother or step-brother / half-sister or step-sister
- **aîné(e)**: Higher; older or oldest (of siblings), e.g. mon frère aîné
- **le jumeau / la jumelle**: Higher; twin brother / twin sister; plural les jumeaux
- **la confiance**: Higher; trust, e.g. j'ai confiance en lui; se fier à is the Higher verb
- **soutenir**: Higher; to support, e.g. mes parents me soutiennent toujours
- **le copain / la copine**: Higher; friend, or boyfriend / girlfriend depending on context
- **l'amitié (f)**: Higher; friendship, e.g. l'amitié est essentielle pour moi
- **l'égalité (f)**: equality; égal = equal; l'inégalité = inequality (prefix in-)
- **le harcèlement**: Higher; harassment or bullying, e.g. le harcèlement en ligne
- **lutter contre**: to fight against, e.g. lutter contre le racisme et le sexisme
- **non-binaire / transgenre**: non-binary / transgender; both are Foundation list words
:::

::: method
1. **Reading (Paper 3):** read the English questions first; they tell you who and what to look for. Answer in English with the **detail** asked for. A single copied French word scores 0.
2. **Hunt the traps:** time markers (*avant, au début, maintenant*), negatives (*ne...plus, ne...jamais*), and *même* or *par contre*, which turn a statement round. Most wrong options in "two/three correct statements" items are built on these.
3. **Inferred words:** if a word is not familiar, look at the sentence after it, which usually explains it (*il est têtu : il ne change jamais d'avis*). Choose the A/B/C option that fits that explanation.
4. **Writing Q1 (to a friend):** one or two developed sentences per bullet. Give a description (present), an opinion with *parce que / car*, a past event (perfect plus a *c'était* comment) and a future plan (*je vais* + infinitive, or *j'aimerais*).
5. **Speaking picture task:** people (how many, age, looks, mood), location (*au premier plan, à l'arrière-plan*), activity (*ils sont en train de...*), then an opinion. Answer the two questions with a full sentence plus a reason, the second in the perfect.
6. **Check:** agreements with the person described, the reflexive pronoun matching the subject, *depuis* + present, and *être* verbs agreeing in the perfect (*elle est venue*).
:::

::: example Reading: three correct statements
Read Léa's blog.

*Mes parents travaillent à l'étranger, donc depuis deux ans, j'habite chez ma grand-mère. Elle est assez sévère : je dois rentrer avant vingt heures, même le samedi. Au début, je pleurais souvent parce que mes parents me manquaient. Maintenant, ça va mieux. Je les vois pendant les vacances et on se parle tous les soirs sur l'ordinateur. Ma grand-mère n'est plus seule, et elle dit qu'elle est fière de moi.*

Put a cross next to each one of the **three** correct statements. (3)
A Léa's parents work abroad.
B Léa has lived with her grandmother for two years.
C Léa can come home late at weekends.
D Léa still cries every evening.
E Léa talks to her parents every evening on the computer.
F Léa's grandmother feels lonely now.

**Answer:** A (1), B (1), E (1).
- B rests on **depuis + present**: *depuis deux ans, j'habite* = I have been living for two years.
- C is wrong because of *même le samedi* (even on Saturday).
- D is a **time-frame trap**: *au début, je pleurais* (imperfect, at the start), but *maintenant, ça va mieux*.
- F is a **negative trap**: *n'est plus seule* = is no longer alone.
:::

::: example Model Q1 answer: my family (18)
**Task.** Write to your friend about your family. You must include the following points:
- the people in your family
- your opinion of family life, with reasons
- a family celebration you went to recently
- where you would like to live in the future.

Write your answer in French. You should aim to write between 80 and 90 words.

**Model (about 90 words):**
Salut Hugo ! Chez moi, nous sommes six : ma mère, mon beau-père, mes deux demi-frères, qui sont jumeaux, ma sœur aînée et moi. J'adore la vie de famille parce qu'on ne s'ennuie jamais et qu'on peut toujours compter sur quelqu'un. Par contre, la salle de bains est toujours occupée ! Le mois dernier, nous avons fêté les cinquante ans de ma mère. Toute la famille est venue et nous avons dansé jusqu'à minuit. À l'avenir, j'aimerais habiter seul en ville, mais pas trop loin de ma famille. Et toi ?

**Why it reaches the top bands.**
- **AO2:** every bullet is covered and developed (who, plus the twins detail; an opinion with two reasons and a contrast; what happened at the party; where and why).
- **AO3:** three time frames (*nous sommes*, *nous avons fêté / est venue*, *j'aimerais*), Higher items (*jumeaux, aînée, compter sur, jusqu'à*), a relative clause (*qui sont jumeaux*), a negative (*ne...jamais*) and the agreement *Toute la famille est venue*.
- A female writer would write *habiter seule*.
:::

::: example Translation into French, step by step (10)
Translate the paragraph into French.
*I have two older sisters. My best friend is called Emma and she is very patient. We have known each other for ten years. Last year, we went to Paris with her parents. In the future, I would like to fight against racism because everyone deserves respect.*

1. *J'ai deux sœurs aînées.* **Aîné** agrees: feminine plural *aînées*. (*Deux sœurs plus âgées que moi* is also fine.)
2. *Ma meilleure amie s'appelle Emma et elle est très patiente.* *Meilleure* and *patiente* agree with Emma.
3. *Nous nous connaissons depuis dix ans.* "Have known each other for" = **present + depuis**, with the reciprocal *nous nous*. *Nous avons connu* here is a tense error.
4. *L'année dernière, nous sommes allé(e)s à Paris avec ses parents.* *Aller* takes *être*; *ses* = her, agreeing with *parents*.
5. *À l'avenir, j'aimerais lutter contre le racisme parce que tout le monde mérite le respect.* *Mériter* is on the list. "Everyone" is *tout le monde* + singular verb.

**Marking:** AO2 meaning (6) plus AO3 accuracy (4). Sentences 3 and 5 decide the top band.
:::

Give the feminine singular form of: travailleur, sérieux, fier, vieux, beau, sportif. (3)
---
travailleuse, sérieuse, fière, vieille, belle, sportive. Two correct = 1 mark, four = 2, all six = 3.

::: q
Translate into French: "My twin brother and I argue every day." (2)
---
*Mon frère jumeau et moi, nous nous disputons tous les jours.* (2) Accept *Mon jumeau et moi, on se dispute tous les jours.* 1 mark if the meaning is clear but the reflexive is wrong (e.g. *nous disputons*).
:::

::: q
*Ma demi-sœur est jalouse : elle n'aime pas quand son copain parle avec d'autres filles.* What does *jalouse* mean, and which words told you? (2)
---
Jealous (1). The explanation after the colon: she does not like it when her boyfriend talks to other girls (1).
:::

::: q
Fill each gap with *son*, *sa* or *ses*: (a) Lucas adore ___ sœur. (b) Emma s'entend bien avec ___ frère. (c) Clara partage ___ secrets avec ___ mère. (2)
---
(a) sa (b) son (c) ses, sa. The possessive agrees with the thing owned, not the owner: *sa sœur* is "his sister" here. All four correct = 2; two or three = 1.
:::

::: q
Say in French: "A good friend is someone who listens to me and who never judges me." (2)
---
*Un bon ami, c'est quelqu'un qui m'écoute et qui ne me juge jamais.* (2) The object pronoun *me* goes before the verb and *ne...jamais* wraps round it. 1 mark if the meaning is clear with one error.
:::

::: q
Put into the imperfect: *Je me dispute avec ma sœur. Nous partageons une chambre.* (2)
---
*Je me disputais avec ma sœur.* (1) *Nous partagions une chambre.* (1) Take the *nous* stem (*disput-*, *partage-*) and add the imperfect endings. *Partager* keeps its -e- before -a- (*je partageais*) but not before -i- (*nous partagions*).
:::

::: q
Develop this answer by adding a reason, a contrast and a past-tense detail: *Je m'entends bien avec mon père.* (3)
---
For example: *Je m'entends bien avec mon père parce qu'il est patient et qu'il m'écoute.* (reason, 1) *Par contre, je me dispute parfois avec ma mère car elle est très stricte.* (contrast, 1) *Le week-end dernier, mon père et moi, nous sommes allés voir un match de foot.* (past, 1)
:::

::: watch
- **Isolated French words** copied as answers to Paper 3 questions score nothing. Answer in English with the detail the question asks for.
- **Answers that are true but don't answer the question** also score 0. If the question asks why, give the reason, not the fact.
- **Agreement with the person described**: *ma sœur est sportive, mes frères sont sérieux*. Remember the irregular ones: *vieille, belle, fière, travailleuse*.
- **Possessives agree with the thing owned**: *sa mère* can mean his mother.
- **Reflexive pronoun mismatch**: *je veux m'amuser / je ne veux pas me disputer*. Writing *se* after *je* is a major error.
- **Depuis + present**: *je le connais depuis trois ans*, never a perfect tense.
- **Traps in reading and listening**: *ne...plus, avant / maintenant, au début, même, par contre*.
- **A missing bullet** in Q1 or Q2 caps the AO2 mark at the 8–10 band, however good the rest is.
:::
