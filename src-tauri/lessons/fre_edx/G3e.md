## What it is

Two verb forms that are short to learn and easy to show off with.
- The **imperative** gives instructions, advice and invitations: *Prenez la deuxième rue à gauche. Mange tes légumes ! Venez découvrir la ville !* You meet it in the role play (directions, the teacher's prompts), in Paper 3 adverts and brochures, and you can use it in Paper 4 to give advice.
- The **present participle after en** (*en travaillant*) means "while doing" or "by doing". One *en* + *-ant* phrase in Paper 4 is exactly the "structure unlike English" that Pearson calls complex language.

**What Edexcel lists.**

| Foundation + Higher | Higher |
|---|---|
| the *tu* and *vous* imperative (not reflexive) of -er verbs, the *choisir / partir / venir / ouvrir* patterns, the *entendre / prendre / traduire* patterns, *aller* and *faire* | the *tu* and *vous* imperative of *être* and the *connaître / écrire* patterns |
| | the *nous* imperative ("let's") of -er verbs and the -ir and -re patterns |
| | the present participle **after en**, including adjectival use, for -er verbs, the -ir and -re patterns, *connaître / écrire*, *avoir, être, faire* |

Reflexive imperatives (*lève-toi, asseyez-vous*) and imperatives with object pronouns after them (*prends-le*) are not on the list: recognise them, but they are not required. Note that *asseyez-vous* (sit down) is a common classroom phrase you may still hear.

**1. The tu and vous imperative.** Take the present-tense *tu* or *vous* form and **drop the subject**.

| Model | tu | vous | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| -er: *regarder* | **regarde** (no -s) | regardez | look, watch |
| *choisir* | choisis | choisissez | choose |
| *partir* | pars | partez | leave |
| *venir* | viens | venez | come |
| *ouvrir* | **ouvre** (no -s) | ouvrez | open |
| *entendre* (*attendre*) | attends | attendez | wait |
| *prendre* | prends | prenez | take |
| *traduire* | traduis | traduisez | translate |
| *aller* | **va** (no -s) | allez | go |
| *faire* | fais | **faites** | do, make |

- **The -s rule:** -er verbs, *aller*, and *ouvrir* verbs (which take -er endings) drop the -s of the *tu* form: *tu manges* → *Mange !*, *tu vas* → *Va !*, *tu offres* → *Offre !*. Every other verb keeps it: *Prends ! Choisis ! Viens !*
- *Faites* is irregular, like the present: never *faisez*.
- *Tu* for a friend or family member; *vous* for an adult you don't know, or for more than one person. Signs and brochures use *vous*: *Réservez en ligne. Venez nombreux !*
- The negative goes round the verb: *Ne mange pas ça ! Ne partez pas ! N'oublie pas ton billet !*

**Directions** (role play, "in town" and the tourist office):
- *Prenez la première rue à droite.* (Take the first street on the right.)
- *Tournez à gauche.* (Turn left.)
- *Allez tout droit.* / *Continuez tout droit.* (Go straight on.)
- *Traversez la place.* (Cross the square.)

**2. Higher: être, connaître, écrire.**

| | tu | vous |
|---|---|---|
| *être* | **sois** | **soyez** |
| *connaître* | connais | connaissez |
| *écrire* | écris | écrivez |

*Sois patient(e) !* (Be patient!) *Soyez prudents.* (Be careful: *prudent* agrees with the people.) *Écrivez votre nom ici.* *Fais attention !* (Be careful!) is the everyday way, with *faire*.

**3. Higher: the nous imperative = "let's".** Take the *nous* form and drop *nous*: *Regardons un film !* (Let's watch a film!) *Choisissons un restaurant. Partons tôt. Prenons le bus. Attendons ici.*
- *Allons-y !* (Let's go!) is a fixed phrase. The plain *allons* + place (*Allons au parc !*) is credited if correct.
- The negative: *Ne partons pas trop tard.*

**4. Higher: en + present participle.** Take the *nous* form of the present, drop *-ons*, add **-ant**. It never changes after *en*.

| Verb | nous | en + -ant |
|---|---|---|
| *travailler* | travaillons | en travaillant |
| *manger* | mangeons | en mangeant |
| *choisir* | choisissons | en choisissant |
| *partir* | partons | en partant |
| *venir* | venons | en venant |
| *ouvrir* | ouvrons | en ouvrant |
| *attendre* | attendons | en attendant |
| *prendre* | prenons | en prenant |
| *traduire* (and *lire*) | traduisons | en traduisant, en lisant |
| *connaître* | connaissons | en connaissant |
| *écrire* | écrivons | en écrivant |
| *faire* | faisons | en faisant |
| *avoir* | (irregular) | en **ayant** |
| *être* | (irregular) | en **étant** |

**What it means.** The same person does two things:
- **while / when doing**: *J'écoute de la musique en faisant mes devoirs.* = I listen to music while doing my homework. *En arrivant à la gare, j'ai vu Lucas.* = On arriving (when I arrived) at the station, I saw Lucas.
- **by doing** (how): *En recyclant, on protège la planète.* = By recycling, we protect the planet. *On apprend une langue en parlant.* = You learn a language by speaking.

The *-ant* phrase belongs to the subject of the main verb. Don't write *en* + *-ant* for "I like reading": that is *j'aime lire* (the infinitive, G1a).

**Adjectival use.** Many *-ant* words are adjectives too, and then they **agree** like any adjective: *un film intéressant, une soirée amusante, des livres intéressants*. *En* + *-ant* never agrees; the adjective always does.

*Après avoir* + past participle ("after doing") is a preposition structure: see G6.

::: terms
- **l'impératif**: the command form: the tu, vous or nous present without the subject: prends, prenez, prenons
- **la règle du -s**: -er verbs, aller and ouvrir verbs drop the -s in the tu form: mange, va, ouvre
- **faites**: the vous imperative of faire, irregular like the present: faites attention
- **sois / soyez**: Higher; the imperative of être: sois patient, soyez prudents
- **l'impératif en nous**: Higher; let's: regardons, partons, choisissons; allons-y is a fixed phrase
- **le participe présent**: Higher; the nous stem plus -ant: travaillant, choisissant, prenant
- **ayant, étant**: the irregular present participles of avoir and être
- **en + -ant**: while, on or by doing: en faisant mes devoirs, en recyclant
- **l'emploi adjectival**: an -ant word used as an adjective agrees: une histoire amusante
:::

::: method
1. **Who is being told?** One friend: *tu*. An adult stranger, a group or a sign: *vous*. "Let's": *nous* (Higher).
2. **Take the present-tense form** for that person and drop the subject.
3. **Apply the -s rule** in the *tu* form: drop it for -er verbs, *aller* and *ouvrir* verbs only.
4. **Learn the four odd ones**: *faites*, *va*, *sois / soyez*.
5. **For "while / by / on doing"**, take the *nous* form, drop *-ons*, add *-ant*, and put *en* before it. *Ayant* and *étant* are irregular.
6. **Check the meaning in reading**: an imperative is an instruction or invitation ("you should / come and..."); *en* + *-ant* tells you how or when.
:::

::: example The tu imperative and the -s rule (4)
Tell a friend to: (a) eat (*manger*) (b) go (*aller*) (c) come (*venir*) (d) open the window (*ouvrir*)

(a) *Mange !* (1) -er verb: no -s.
(b) *Va !* (1) No -s.
(c) *Viens !* (1) Not an -er verb: keep the -s.
(d) *Ouvre la fenêtre !* (1) *Ouvrir* takes -er endings, so no -s.
:::

::: example Role play: directions (2)
Transcript (the teacher, as a passer-by): *Pour aller au musée ? C'est facile. Allez tout droit, prenez la deuxième rue à gauche, et le musée est à côté de la banque.*

(a) Which street should you take? (b) What is the museum next to?

(a) The second street on the left (1). Reject "second on the right" or "first on the left". (b) The bank (1). The imperatives (*allez, prenez*) carry the instructions; the location comes after.
:::

::: example Higher: en + -ant (4)
Translate into English: (a) *Je chante en faisant la cuisine.* (b) *En travaillant beaucoup, tu vas réussir.* (c) *En sortant du cinéma, nous avons vu Emma.* (d) *C'était une soirée très amusante.*

(a) I sing while (I'm) cooking. (1)
(b) By working hard, you will succeed. (1)
(c) On leaving (as we left) the cinema, we saw Emma. (1)
(d) It was a very fun (entertaining) evening. (1) Adjectival use: *amusante* agrees with *soirée*.
:::

::: example Higher: let's (3)
Suggest: (a) Let's take the train. (b) Let's choose a present. (c) Let's not leave too late.

(a) *Prenons le train !* (1)
(b) *Choisissons un cadeau !* (1)
(c) *Ne partons pas trop tard !* (1)
:::

Higher. Translate into English: *En lisant beaucoup, on apprend beaucoup de choses.* (2)
---

:::

::: watch
- **Drop the -s in the tu form of -er verbs and aller**: *Mange ! Va !*, not *Manges ! Vas !*. Keep it for other verbs: *Prends ! Viens !*
- **Faites, not faisez**. *Soyez*, not *êtes*, for "be".
- **Use vous for strangers and signs** in the role play and in formal writing; *tu* for a friend in Paper 4 Q1.
- **The imperative has no subject**: *Prenez la rue*, not *Vous prenez la rue* (that is a statement, though it still communicates).
- **en + -ant means while / by / on doing**, and the same person does both actions.
- **Don't use -ant for English "-ing" after a verb**: "I love dancing" is *j'adore danser*.
- **Ayant and étant** are the irregular present participles; *en faisant* follows the *nous* rule.
- **Adjectives in -ant agree**: *une histoire intéressante*; *en* + *-ant* never does.
:::

::: test
Translate the passage into English.

*Tu veux être en forme ? C'est simple ! Mange des fruits tous les jours et bois beaucoup d'eau. Va au collège à pied ou à vélo. Ne passe pas tout ton temps devant un écran : sors avec tes amis ! En faisant du sport deux fois par semaine, tu vas te sentir mieux. Et sois patient : les résultats prennent du temps.* (10)
---
Holistic bands. 9–10: the meaning is fully transferred in natural English. 6–8: most of the meaning, with occasional wrong or missing words. 3–5: parts of the meaning, with imperatives turned into statements or *en faisant* mistranslated. 1–2: isolated words or phrases.
**Model:** Do you want to be fit? It's simple! Eat fruit every day and drink lots of water. Walk or cycle to school (go to school on foot or by bike). Don't spend all your time in front of a screen: go out with your friends! By doing sport twice a week, you'll feel better. And be patient: results take time.
**Deciding details:** *Mange, bois, va, sors* = instructions, not "you eat, you drink"; *ne passe pas tout ton temps* = don't spend all your time; *en faisant du sport* = by doing (playing) sport; *tu vas te sentir mieux* = you'll feel better; *sois patient* = be patient.
:::

::: test
Translate the paragraph into French.

Come to our new sports centre! Choose an activity and book online. Don't forget your trainers. Let's protect the planet: take the bus or come by bike. By playing sport with friends, you will have fun! (*vous*) (10)
---
**AO2 meaning (6):** 5–6 the meaning is transferred consistently, with rare lack of clarity; 3–4 most of it is transferred, with the occasional message unclear or missing; 1–2 some parts are transferred, and the message sometimes breaks down.
**AO3 accuracy (4):** 4 consistently accurate, any errors minor; 3 generally accurate, minor errors, major errors rare; 2 sometimes accurate, including some major errors; 1 limited accuracy, frequent major and minor errors.
**Model:** *Venez dans notre nouveau centre sportif ! Choisissez une activité et réservez en ligne. N'oubliez pas vos baskets. Protégeons la planète : prenez le bus ou venez à vélo. En faisant du sport avec des amis, vous allez vous amuser !*
**Key points:** the *vous* imperatives *venez, choisissez, réservez, prenez*; *N'oubliez pas* round the verb; the Higher *nous* imperative *protégeons* (keep the e before o); *nouveau* before the noun; *en faisant* for "by playing sport"; *vous allez vous amuser* (accept *vous vous amuserez*, credited if correct). Minor errors: *nouvelle centre*, *centre sportive*, *à le centre*, *protégons*. Major errors: *Venir* (infinitive for a command), *Faisez*, *Nous protégeons* (a statement, not "let's"), *par jouer* for "by playing".
:::
