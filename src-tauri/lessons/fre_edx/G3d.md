## What it is

Every Edexcel paper needs the future. Paper 4 Q1 and Q2 both end with a future-plans bullet, the Higher role play has one prompt in a future time frame, and the picture-task conversation is marked on three time frames. French gives you three tools: **aller + infinitive** (the near future), the **future tense** (*le futur simple*) and the **conditional** (*le conditionnel*, "would").

**What Edexcel lists.**

| Foundation + Higher | Higher |
|---|---|
| *aller* + infinitive for "will" and "going to", all persons, with every verb model | *aller* + *connaître / écrire* patterns |
| the conditional of *vouloir* in the singular: *je voudrais, tu voudrais, il / elle / on voudrait* (not formal *vous*) | the **future** of -er verbs in all persons: *je jouerai, nous jouerons, ils joueront* |
| *il y aura* (there will be) | the future of *aller, avoir, être, faire* **in the singular**: *j'irai, tu auras, il sera, je ferai* |
| | the **conditional** of -er verbs in all persons: *j'aimerais, nous aimerions* |
| | the conditional of *aller, avoir, être, faire* in the singular: *j'irais, j'aurais, ce serait, je ferais* |

So plural irregular futures (*nous irons, ils seront, vous ferez*) and the future or conditional of other irregular verbs (*je pourrai, je viendrai, je devrais*) are **not required, credited if correct**. You never need them: *nous allons partir* and *ils vont être* do the same job.

**1. Aller + infinitive (Foundation + Higher).** The present of *aller* + an infinitive. It means "going to" and "will".

| | aller + infinitive |
|---|---|
| je vais jouer | nous allons jouer |
| tu vas jouer | vous allez jouer |
| il / elle / on va jouer | ils / elles vont jouer |

- Any verb can go in the infinitive: *Je vais choisir une université. Ils vont prendre le train. On va faire du ski. Elle va écrire un livre.*
- The negative goes round *aller*: *Je ne vais pas sortir ce soir.*
- Pronouns and reflexive pronouns go before the infinitive: *Je vais le faire demain. Je vais me lever tôt.*
- *Aller aller* is fine: *Nous allons aller en France* (We're going to go to France).

**2. The future tense (Higher).** For -er verbs, take the **infinitive** and add endings that look like the present of *avoir*.

| jouer | |
|---|---|
| je jouerai | nous jouerons |
| tu joueras | vous jouerez |
| il / elle / on jouera | ils / elles joueront |

- *L'année prochaine, je travaillerai dans un café. Mes parents achèteront une maison.* (Verbs like *acheter* take their grave accent: *j'achèterai*.)
- The four irregular stems, singular only:

| | aller | avoir | être | faire |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| je | irai | aurai | serai | ferai |
| tu | iras | auras | seras | feras |
| il / elle / on | ira | aura | sera | fera |

*J'irai à l'université. J'aurai dix-huit ans. Ce sera génial. Je ferai le tour du monde.* (Not *allerai* or *êterai*.)

- *Il y aura* = there will be: *Il y aura un concert sur la plage.*

**3. The conditional (Foundation for *voudrais*, otherwise Higher).** The conditional means "would". Take the **future stem** (the infinitive for -er verbs; *ir-, aur-, ser-, fer-* for the four irregulars) and add the **imperfect endings**.

| aimer | |
|---|---|
| j'aimerais | nous aimerions |
| tu aimerais | vous aimeriez |
| il / elle / on aimerait | ils / elles aimeraient |

| | aller | avoir | être | faire | vouloir |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| je | irais | aurais | serais | ferais | voudrais |
| tu | irais | aurais | serais | ferais | voudrais |
| il / elle / on | irait | aurait | serait | ferait | voudrait |

- *Je voudrais un café, s'il vous plaît.* (would like: the polite form in every role play)
- *Je voudrais travailler à l'étranger.* (would like to + infinitive)
- *J'aimerais habiter au Canada. Ce serait génial.*
- *Mes amis aimeraient voyager.*

**Will or would?** The only written difference is the **s**: *je jouerai* (I will play), *je jouerais* (I would play). *J'irai* (I will go), *j'irais* (I would go). *Je voudrais* is "I would like"; *je voudrai* is "I will want", which is not what you mean in a café. Many speakers pronounce *-rai* and *-rais* almost the same, so in the dictation the context decides: a plan with a date is *-rai*; a wish or an "if" is *-rais*.

**4. Si + present, then future.** For a real possibility, French uses the **present** after *si* and the **future** (or *aller* + infinitive) in the main clause. *Si* + *il* becomes *s'il*.
- *S'il fait beau demain, j'irai à la plage.* = If it's fine tomorrow, I'll go to the beach.
- *Si j'ai le temps, je vais aider mes parents.*
- *Si nous gagnons le match, nous fêterons ça.*

Never the future after *si*: *si il fera beau* is wrong twice. For an imaginary situation, *si* + imperfect, then conditional, is not on Edexcel's list as a pattern, but it is credited if correct and it impresses: *Si j'avais beaucoup d'argent, j'achèterais une grande maison.*

A Higher flourish: after *quand* with a future meaning, French uses the future: *Quand j'aurai dix-huit ans, j'irai à l'université.*

**5. Which one to use?** In Paper 4, use **two or three** of these in the future bullet: it shows range (AO3).
- *aller* + infinitive for any verb, any person: it is the safest, because it only needs the present of *aller*.
- the future for -er verbs and the four irregulars in the singular;
- *je voudrais* / *j'aimerais* + infinitive for wishes;
- *ce serait* + adjective for an opinion of the plan.

*L'année prochaine, je vais apprendre l'espagnol, et je travaillerai dans un magasin pour gagner de l'argent. Un jour, j'aimerais habiter à l'étranger, parce que ce serait une expérience incroyable.*

**Time words**: *demain, ce soir, la semaine prochaine, l'été prochain, l'année prochaine, plus tard, à l'avenir, un jour, dans cinq ans*.

::: terms
- **le futur proche**: aller + infinitive: je vais partir, ils vont choisir; works with every verb
- **le futur simple**: Higher; the infinitive of an -er verb plus -ai, -as, -a, -ons, -ez, -ont: je jouerai
- **les radicaux irréguliers**: ir-, aur-, ser-, fer- for aller, avoir, être, faire: j'irai, j'aurai, je serai, je ferai
- **il y aura**: there will be: il y aura beaucoup de monde
- **le conditionnel**: Higher; the future stem plus the imperfect endings: j'aimerais, ce serait
- **je voudrais**: I would like; the conditional of vouloir, for requests and wishes
- **-rai / -rais**: will / would: je visiterai (I will visit), je visiterais (I would visit)
- **si + présent + futur**: a real condition: s'il pleut, je resterai à la maison
- **s'il**: si shortens before il and ils, but not before elle: si elle vient
- **les marqueurs du futur**: demain, l'année prochaine, plus tard, à l'avenir, dans dix ans
:::

::: method
1. **Identify the meaning**: "going to / will" (a plan), "would" (a wish or an imaginary situation), or "would like" (a request).
2. **For a plan, choose the structure.** Any verb, any person: *aller* + infinitive. An -er verb, or *aller / avoir / être / faire* in the singular: the future is available at Higher.
3. **Future**: the infinitive (or *ir-, aur-, ser-, fer-*) + *-ai, -as, -a, -ons, -ez, -ont*.
4. **Conditional**: the same stem + *-ais, -ais, -ait, -ions, -iez, -aient*. *Vouloir* is *voudr-*.
5. **After *si***: the present, never the future. Main clause: future or *aller* + infinitive.
6. **Check the s**: *-rai* = will, *-rais* = would. *Je voudrais*, with the s, for "I would like".
7. **In Paper 4**, show range in the future bullet: one *aller* + infinitive, one future, one conditional opinion.
:::

::: example Three ways to talk about the future (3)
Rewrite *Je travaille dans un hôtel* to mean: (a) I'm going to work in a hotel. (b) I will work in a hotel. (Higher) (c) I would like to work in a hotel.

(a) *Je vais travailler dans un hôtel.* (1)
(b) *Je travaillerai dans un hôtel.* (1)
(c) *Je voudrais travailler dans un hôtel.* (1) Accept *J'aimerais travailler*.
:::

::: example Higher: irregular futures and conditionals (4)
Translate into French: (a) I will go to Paris. (b) It will be great. (c) I would have more time. (d) She would do her homework.

(a) *J'irai à Paris.* (1) Not *j'allerai*.
(b) *Ce sera génial.* (1) Accept *Ça va être génial*.
(c) *J'aurais plus de temps.* (1)
(d) *Elle ferait ses devoirs.* (1)
:::

::: example Si clauses (3)
Complete with the correct form: (a) S'il ___ (pleuvoir) demain, nous ___ (rester) à la maison. (b) Si tu ___ (venir), on ___ (aller) au cinéma.

(a) *pleut* (1), *resterons* (1): present after *si*, future in the main clause. Accept *allons rester*.
(b) *viens*, *ira* (or *va aller*) (1): both needed for the mark.
:::

::: example Reading: will, would or now? (3)
*Cette année, je travaille dans un bureau le samedi. L'année prochaine, j'irai au lycée et j'étudierai les sciences. Un jour, j'aimerais travailler comme médecin.*

(a) What is the writer doing this year? (b) What will they study next year? (c) What would they like to do one day?

(a) Working in an office on Saturdays (1). (b) Science(s) (1): *étudierai* is the future. (c) Work as a doctor (1): *aimerais* is a wish, not a definite plan. An option saying "is going to be a doctor next year" mixes the time frames and is the distractor.
:::

::: example Role play: the future prompt (2)
Prompt: *Say what you will visit tomorrow.* (At the tourist office.)

Model 2 marks: *Demain, je vais visiter le musée.* / *Demain, je visiterai le château.* Both communicate a future time frame. 1 mark: *Je visite le musée* without *demain* (the time frame is ambiguous). 0: *J'ai visité le musée* (wrong time frame).
:::

---

::: q
Translate into English: *Plus tard, j'aimerais travailler à l'étranger, mais d'abord je vais aller à l'université.* (2)
---
Later (on), I would like to work abroad, but first I am going to go to university. (2) 1 if "would like" becomes "like" or "will", or *d'abord* is missed.
:::

::: watch
- **Irregular stems**: *j'irai*, not *j'allerai*; *je serai*, not *j'êterai*; *je ferai*, not *je fairai*.
- **Never the future after si**: *s'il fait beau, j'irai*. Not *s'il fera beau*.
- **Will versus would**: *-rai* = will, *-rais* = would. *Je voudrais* (with s) for "I would like".
- **Keep the infinitive after aller**: *je vais jouer*, not *je vais joue* or *je vais jouerai*.
- **Plural irregular futures are off the list**: use *nous allons partir* rather than risking *nous irons*. They are credited if correct.
- **Use a future time frame for the future bullet**: a present tense without a time word may not count as a future, which caps AO3.
- **Si + il = s'il**, but *si elle* stays as it is.
- **The future of -er verbs keeps the whole infinitive**: *je travaillerai* (not *je travailrai*), *ils joueront*.
:::

::: test
Translate the passage into English.

*L'année prochaine, je vais quitter le collège et j'irai au lycée en ville. Je travaillerai beaucoup, parce que je voudrais aller à l'université. Mes amis vont chercher du travail. S'ils trouvent un emploi, ils gagneront de l'argent. Moi, j'aimerais habiter à Paris plus tard. Ce serait génial ! Mais d'abord, je serai étudiant ici.* (10)
---
Holistic bands. 9–10: the meaning is fully transferred in natural English. 6–8: most of the meaning, with occasional wrong or missing words. 3–5: parts of the meaning, with "will" and "would" confused or phrases missing. 1–2: isolated words or phrases.
**Model:** Next year, I am going to leave school and I will go to sixth-form college (secondary school) in town. I will work hard (a lot), because I would like to go to university. My friends are going to look for work. If they find a job, they will earn money. As for me, I would like to live in Paris later. It would be great! But first, I will be a student here.
**Deciding details:** *vais quitter* = am going to leave; *j'irai* = I will go; *je voudrais* = I would like; *vont chercher* = are going to look for; *S'ils trouvent... ils gagneront* = If they find... they will earn; *Moi* = As for me (accept "I"); *ce serait* (would be) versus *je serai* (I will be): both must be right for 9–10.
:::

::: test
Translate the paragraph into French.

Tomorrow I am going to visit my grandparents. Next week we will play tennis together. If it rains, we will stay at home. One day, I would like to go to Martinique, because it would be great. My parents would prefer to travel in France, but I will go there with my friends! (10)
---
**AO2 meaning (6):** 5–6 the meaning is transferred consistently, with rare lack of clarity; 3–4 most of it is transferred, with the occasional message unclear or missing; 1–2 some parts are transferred, and the message sometimes breaks down.
**AO3 accuracy (4):** 4 consistently accurate, any errors minor; 3 generally accurate, minor errors, major errors rare; 2 sometimes accurate, including some major errors; 1 limited accuracy, frequent major and minor errors.
**Model:** *Demain, je vais rendre visite à mes grands-parents. La semaine prochaine, nous jouerons au tennis ensemble. S'il pleut, nous resterons à la maison. Un jour, je voudrais aller en Martinique, parce que ce serait génial. Mes parents préféreraient voyager en France, mais moi, j'irai là-bas avec mes amis !*
**Key points:** *aller* + infinitive (accept *je vais voir mes grands-parents*; *visiter* is for places, but *je vais visiter mes grands-parents* communicates and is a minor error); the -er future *jouerons*, *resterons* (accept *nous allons jouer*); *s'il pleut* with the present; *je voudrais* / *j'aimerais*; *ce serait*; Higher conditional plural *préféreraient*; *j'irai là-bas* (accept *je vais y aller*, *j'y irai*). Minor errors: *à Martinique*, *genial* without the accent, a missing accent on *préféreraient*. Major errors: *s'il pleuvra*, *nous allons jouerons*, *j'allerai*, *ce sera* for "would be" (wrong meaning).
:::
