## What it is

This topic turns proportion statements into **equations**. Every "is proportional to" sentence becomes a formula with one unknown constant $k$; one pair of values finds $k$, and then the formula answers anything else.

### The proportion symbol

$y \propto x$ means "$y$ is directly proportional to $x$". Replacing $\propto$ with "$= k \times$" gives the equation: $y \propto x$ becomes $y = kx$.

The quantity can be a power or a root of $x$:

| In words | Symbols | Equation |
|---|---|---|
| $y$ is proportional to $x$ | $y \propto x$ | $y = kx$ |
| $y$ is proportional to the square of $x$ | $y \propto x^2$ | $y = kx^2$ |
| $y$ is proportional to the cube of $x$ | $y \propto x^3$ | $y = kx^3$ |
| $y$ is proportional to the square root of $x$ | $y \propto \sqrt{x}$ | $y = k\sqrt{x}$ |
| $y$ is inversely proportional to $x$ | $y \propto \dfrac1x$ | $y = \dfrac{k}{x}$ |
| $y$ is inversely proportional to the square of $x$ | $y \propto \dfrac{1}{x^2}$ | $y = \dfrac{k}{x^2}$ |

### Inverse proportion is proportion to the reciprocal

"$X$ is inversely proportional to $Y$" is **the same statement** as "$X$ is proportional to $\dfrac1Y$", because

$$X = \frac{k}{Y} = k \times \frac{1}{Y}.$$

So if you plot $X$ against $\dfrac1Y$ (rather than against $Y$) you get a straight line through the origin with gradient $k$. In the same way, $y = kx^2$ gives a straight line through the origin if you plot $y$ against $x^2$.

### Constructing the equation

1. Write the proportion with $k$: "$F$ is inversely proportional to the square of $r$" gives $F = \dfrac{k}{r^2}$.
2. Substitute the given pair: $F = 20$ when $r = 3$, so $20 = \dfrac{k}{9}$ and $k = 180$.
3. Write the formula in full: $F = \dfrac{180}{r^2}$.
4. Use it: when $r = 6$, $F = \dfrac{180}{36} = 5$. When $F = 45$, $r^2 = \dfrac{180}{45} = 4$ so $r = 2$ (a distance, so positive).

### Interpreting the equation: what happens when $x$ changes

If $y = kx^n$, multiplying $x$ by a factor $m$ multiplies $y$ by $m^n$ — you do not need $k$ at all.

- $y \propto x^2$: double $x$, and $y$ is multiplied by $2^2 = 4$.
- $y \propto x^3$: increase $x$ by $20\%$ and $y$ is multiplied by $1.2^3 = 1.728$, a $72.8\%$ increase.
- $y \propto \dfrac{1}{x^2}$: treble $x$ and $y$ is divided by $9$.
- $y \propto \sqrt{x}$: to double $y$, $x$ must be multiplied by $4$.

### Recognising the graphs

- $y = kx$: straight line through the origin.
- $y = kx^2$: half of a parabola through the origin, getting steeper.
- $y = kx^3$: through the origin, getting steeper even faster.
- $y = k\sqrt{x}$: through the origin, getting **less** steep.
- $y = \dfrac{k}{x}$ and $y = \dfrac{k}{x^2}$: curves that fall towards the $x$-axis and never touch either axis; $\dfrac{k}{x^2}$ falls away more quickly.

All the direct ones pass through $(0, 0)$; the inverse ones never do.

### Deciding the relationship from a table

Try each form in turn. For $x: 1, 2, 4$ and $y: 3, 12, 48$: $\dfrac yx = 3, 6, 12$ is not constant, but $\dfrac{y}{x^2} = 3, 3, 3$ is, so $y = 3x^2$. Doubling $x$ multiplying $y$ by $4$ is the giveaway.

::: terms
- **Proportional to (∝)**: the symbol ∝ means one quantity is a constant multiple of another, so y ∝ x² means y = kx².
- **Constant of proportionality**: the fixed number k in an equation such as y = kx² or y = k/x, found from one pair of values.
- **Inversely proportional**: y is inversely proportional to x when y = k/x, which is the same as y being proportional to 1/x.
- **Inverse square law**: a relationship of the form y = k/x², so doubling x divides y by four.
- **Reciprocal**: one divided by a number, so the reciprocal of Y is 1/Y.
:::

::: method
1. **Translate the words into an equation with $k$.** "Square" means $x^2$, "cube" $x^3$, "square root" $\sqrt x$, "inversely" puts the power of $x$ on the bottom. This line is usually worth the first M1.
2. **Substitute the pair of values you are given** and solve for $k$ (M1). Keep $k$ exact — a fraction if necessary.
3. **Write the full formula** with the number in place of $k$. Many questions give a separate mark for "find a formula for $y$ in terms of $x$", so it must be written out.
4. **Use the formula** for the new value. When solving for $x$, undo the power last, and reject a negative root if $x$ is a length or time.
5. **"What happens to $y$ when $x$ is multiplied by $m$?"** Use $m^n$ — no need to find $k$. Percentage changes: turn them into multipliers first.
6. **Graph questions:** match shape and whether it goes through the origin; state both in an explanation.
7. **Non-calculator paper:** choose to square or root before multiplying, e.g. $\sqrt{49} = 7$ first. **Calculator paper:** store an awkward $k$ in the memory rather than rounding it.
:::

::: example Proportional to a square
$y$ is directly proportional to the square of $x$. When $x = 3$, $y = 45$. Find (a) a formula for $y$ in terms of $x$, (b) $y$ when $x = 4$, (c) the positive value of $x$ when $y = 125$.

(a) $y = kx^2$; $45 = 9k$, $k = 5$; so $y = 5x^2$.

(b) $y = 5 \times 16 = 80$.

(c) $125 = 5x^2 \Rightarrow x^2 = 25 \Rightarrow x = 5$.
:::

::: example Proportional to a square root
The distance, $d$ km, a person can see to the horizon is proportional to the square root of their height, $h$ m, above sea level. When $h = 25$, $d = 10$. Find $h$ when $d = 14$.

$d = k\sqrt h$; $10 = 5k$, so $k = 2$ and $d = 2\sqrt h$.

$14 = 2\sqrt h \Rightarrow \sqrt h = 7 \Rightarrow h = 49$ m.
:::

::: example Inverse square
$F$ is inversely proportional to the square of $r$. $F = 20$ when $r = 3$. Find $F$ when $r = 6$ and $r$ when $F = 45$.

$F = \dfrac{k}{r^2}$; $20 = \dfrac{k}{9}$, $k = 180$; $F = \dfrac{180}{r^2}$.

$r = 6$: $F = \dfrac{180}{36} = 5$. $\quad F = 45$: $r^2 = 4$, $r = 2$.

Check with the multiplier idea: $r$ doubled from 3 to 6, so $F$ is divided by $4$: $20 \div 4 = 5$. ✓
:::

::: example Percentage changes (grade 8)
The time, $T$ seconds, for one swing of a pendulum is proportional to the square root of its length, $L$ cm. The length is increased so that $T$ increases by $10\%$. By what percentage has $L$ increased?

$T = k\sqrt L$, so $L = \left(\dfrac{T}{k}\right)^2$ — $L$ is proportional to $T^2$.

$T$ is multiplied by $1.1$, so $L$ is multiplied by $1.1^2 = 1.21$: a $21\%$ increase.
:::

::: example Two linked proportions (grade 9)
$y$ is directly proportional to $x^2$, and $x$ is inversely proportional to $z$. When $z = 2$, $x = 6$; when $x = 3$, $y = 18$. Find $y$ when $z = 3$, and find a formula for $y$ in terms of $z$.

$x = \dfrac{a}{z}$: $6 = \dfrac a2$, $a = 12$, so $x = \dfrac{12}{z}$.

$y = bx^2$: $18 = 9b$, $b = 2$, so $y = 2x^2$.

Substitute: $y = 2\left(\dfrac{12}{z}\right)^2 = \dfrac{288}{z^2}$. When $z = 3$: $y = \dfrac{288}{9} = 32$. (Use a different letter for each constant.)
:::

::: q
$y$ is proportional to the cube of $x$. When $x = 2$, $y = 16$. Find $y$ when $x = 3$.
---
$y = kx^3$; $16 = 8k$, $k = 2$. $y = 2 \times 27 = 54$.
:::

::: q
$y$ is inversely proportional to $x^2$, and $y = 8$ when $x = 3$. Find $y$ when $x = 6$, and the positive value of $x$ when $y = 0.5$.
---
$y = \dfrac{k}{x^2}$; $k = 8 \times 9 = 72$. $y = \dfrac{72}{36} = 2$. $\quad 0.5 = \dfrac{72}{x^2} \Rightarrow x^2 = 144 \Rightarrow x = 12$.
:::

::: q
$p$ is proportional to the square root of $q$. When $q = 16$, $p = 12$. Find $q$ when $p = 21$.
---
$p = k\sqrt q$; $12 = 4k$, $k = 3$. $21 = 3\sqrt q \Rightarrow \sqrt q = 7 \Rightarrow q = 49$.
:::

::: q
Write "$a$ is inversely proportional to the cube of $b$" as an equation.
---
$a = \dfrac{k}{b^3}$.
:::

::: q
$y \propto x^2$. What happens to $y$ when $x$ is trebled?
---
$y$ is multiplied by $3^2 = 9$.
:::

::: q
The intensity of light, $I$, is inversely proportional to the square of the distance, $d$, from the source. Find the percentage change in $I$ when $d$ is doubled.
---
$I$ is divided by $2^2 = 4$, so it becomes $25\%$ of its value: a $75\%$ decrease.
:::

::: q
Find the equation connecting $x$ and $y$: $x: 2, 4, 8$ and $y: 40, 10, 2.5$.
---
Doubling $x$ divides $y$ by 4, so $y = \dfrac{k}{x^2}$. $k = 40 \times 4 = 160$: $y = \dfrac{160}{x^2}$. Check: $\dfrac{160}{64} = 2.5$. ✓
:::

::: q
$y \propto x^2$ and $y$ increases by $44\%$. Find the percentage increase in $x$.
---
$x^2$ is multiplied by $1.44$, so $x$ is multiplied by $\sqrt{1.44} = 1.2$: a $20\%$ increase.
:::

::: q
Which of $y = 3x^2$, $y = 3\sqrt x$ and $y = \dfrac3x$ has a graph that (a) does not pass through the origin, (b) passes through the origin and gets less steep?
---
(a) $y = \dfrac3x$ (it never meets either axis). (b) $y = 3\sqrt x$.
:::

::: watch
- **Read the power carefully.** "Proportional to the square of $x$" is $y = kx^2$, not $y = (kx)^2$ and not $y = kx$.
- **Write the formula out in full** once $k$ is found. Answering part (b) without ever stating $y = 5x^2$ can lose the formula mark.
- **Inverse means dividing**: $y = \dfrac{k}{x^2}$, so $k = y \times x^2$, not $\dfrac{y}{x^2}$.
- **Undo in the right order** when solving for $x$: divide by $k$ first, then take the root.
- For "what happens when $x$ doubles" questions, use $2^n$ — you do not need $k$, and working it out wastes time.
- Two linked proportions need **two different constants**; calling both $k$ gives nonsense.
:::
