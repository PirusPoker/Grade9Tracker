## What it is

Paper 2 is the Speaking exam: **50 marks, 25% of the GCSE**, conducted and recorded by your teacher in April or May and marked by an AQA examiner. At Higher it lasts **10 to 12 minutes**, and **marking stops at 12 minutes**: anything you say after that earns nothing. Before it you get **15 minutes' supervised preparation** with the three cards. You may write notes on the Additional answer sheet, as much as you like (full sentences and conjugated verbs are allowed), and take them into the exam room. You hand them in at the end. No dictionaries, and you may **not** read the reading aloud text out loud during preparation.

It has three parts:

1. **Role-play (10 marks, 1 to 1.5 minutes).** A card in English with **five tasks**; the teacher plays the other person. Each task is worth 2 marks: **2** if the message is conveyed **without ambiguity**, **1** if it is only partly conveyed or ambiguous (or if you give one of two required details), **0** if nothing is conveyed. One task **must be a question you ask**. The instructions tell you exactly what to say, so there is no unexpected question. The spec fixes only the question. At Higher, expect a task that points to another time frame (for example "say what you did yesterday"), so prepare a past and a future sentence for every card.
2. **Reading aloud and conversation (15 marks, 3 to 3.5 minutes).** You read a text of **at least 50 words**, then answer **four compulsory questions** on its topic, asked exactly as printed. Pronunciation of the reading is worth **5 marks**. The top band means pronunciation is always or nearly always accurate, with at most an occasional minor error. The words assessed are chosen to test the sound-symbol correspondences (see lesson 4.4b). The four answers are worth **10 marks, marked as a whole**. For **9 or 10 marks**, all four questions must be answered clearly, **at least two answers must be extended** and at least one other must be **developed well**.
3. **Photo card (25 marks, 6 to 7 minutes).** Two photos from one theme (never the same theme as your reading aloud).
   - **Describing the photos (5 marks, about 1.5 minutes)** in reply to "Parle-moi des photos". For 5 marks, a lot of information is conveyed and it is always clear. You must say at least one thing about **each** photo, or you lose a mark.
   - **Unprepared conversation (about 4.5 to 5.5 minutes)** on any of the three topics of that theme. It is worth **15 marks for communication and 5 for language**. Top communication band (13 to 15): a lot of information, consistent good development with **regular extended responses**, and it is always or nearly always clear. Top language band (5): a **wide variety of vocabulary and structures**, a few minor errors, and few or no major errors when you attempt complex language.

A **major error** is one that gets in the way of communication; a **minor error** does not. The grids reward **development**: a bare answer is not "developed". An extended response gives an opinion, a reason, an example and another time frame. Language beyond the AQA list gets equal credit if it is correct.

::: terms
- **supervised preparation**: 15 minutes with all three cards before the test; notes of any length are allowed and can be used throughout
- **candidate card**: the role-play card, in English, with five tasks that say exactly what to convey
- **without ambiguity**: the standard for 2 marks per role-play task; the listener cannot misunderstand what you mean
- **partially conveyed**: 1 mark; part of the message is missing (one of two details) or it could mean something else
- **reading aloud**: a text of at least 50 words at Higher, marked out of 5 for pronunciation of the sound-symbol correspondences
- **compulsory questions**: the four questions after the reading aloud, asked exactly as written and marked together out of 10
- **extended response**: an answer of several linked sentences: opinion, reason, example, and ideally a second time frame
- **developed well**: an answer that goes beyond the minimum with at least one extra detail or reason
- **major error**: an error that stops or confuses communication (wrong tense changing the meaning, wrong word)
- **minor error**: an error that does not affect meaning (a wrong gender, a missing agreement)
- **Parle-moi des photos**: the teacher's prompt for the photo description; say at least one thing about each photo
- **unprepared conversation**: the 4.5 to 5.5 minutes of questions on the photo card's theme, which you cannot know in advance
- **complex language**: Higher structures from the list: depuis, venir de, avant de, après avoir, en + -ant, y and en, que and où, the future, the conditional, superlatives, il vaut mieux
- **12-minute cut-off**: the examiner stops marking at 12 minutes at Higher, so a long role-play steals conversation time
:::

::: method
1. **Split your 15 minutes**: about 4 on the role-play, 4 on the reading aloud, 6 on the photo card and 1 to read your notes over.
2. **Role-play**: write one full French sentence for each task. Check the task's verb: "say what you did" needs the perfect tense, "say what you will do" the future or *aller* + infinitive. Write the question task as a complete question (*À quelle heure...? Combien coûte...? Est-ce qu'il y a...?*). Give exactly what is asked, no more.
3. **Reading aloud**: mark the text in pencil. Cross out silent endings, join liaisons with a curve, underline nasals and tricky words (*-ill-*, *-gn-*, *th*, *h*). Then predict the four questions on the topic and note an opinion, a reason, a past and a future idea for it.
4. **Photo card**: for each photo, write two or three sentences (who, where, what they are doing, what you think). Use *il y a*, *je vois*, *on peut voir*, *ils ont l'air* + adjective, *je pense que*, *peut-être*. Then list the three topics of the theme and one past, present and future sentence you could use for each.
5. **In the test**: speak for about 1.5 minutes on the photos. In the conversation, develop **every** answer: answer + reason + example + other time frame + opinion.
6. **If you do not understand**, ask: *Pouvez-vous répéter, s'il vous plaît ?* It costs nothing.
:::

::: example Role-play at a hotel (Theme 3: travel and tourism)
**Card:** You are at a hotel in France. The teacher is the receptionist.
1. Say how many nights you want to stay and for how many people.
2. Say what sort of room you would like and why.
3. Say what you did yesterday. (one detail)
4. Ask a question about breakfast.
5. Say what you are going to visit tomorrow.

**Model answers:**
1. *Je voudrais rester trois nuits pour deux personnes.* (both details, so 2 marks; missing one detail would give 1)
2. *Je voudrais une chambre avec vue sur la mer, parce que c'est plus joli.*
3. *Hier, j'ai visité le château.* (the perfect tense signals the past without ambiguity)
4. *À quelle heure est le petit-déjeuner ?* (a real question: intonation or a question word)
5. *Demain, je vais visiter le marché en ville.*

Short, complete and unambiguous is full marks. A 30-second speech on task 2 wastes time and risks errors.
:::

::: example Reading aloud with the traps marked
*Pour rester en forme, je fais du sport trois fois par semaine. Le lundi, je vais à la piscine avec ma sœur, et le jeudi, nous jouons au foot au collège. Je mange beaucoup de fruits, mais j'adore aussi les gâteaux ! Selon mon père, il faut dormir huit heures chaque nuit, mais c'est difficile pendant les examens.*

- **Silent endings**: *sport, trois, fois, fais, beaucoup, fruits, gâteaux, nuit, examens*. Not silent: the r of *dormir* and *pour*, and the c of *avec*.
- **Liaison**: *nous jouons* (no liaison before a consonant), *les examens* (z sound), *huit heures* (the t is sounded; the h is silent).
- **SSCs**: *fois* (oi = wa), *semaine* (ai), *lundi* (un), *sœur* (open eu), *jeudi* (closed eu), *collège* (è), *beaucoup* (eau), *chaque* (ch, qu), *pendant* (an/en).
- **The trap**: *examen* ends in the sound of *in*, not *an*.

**The four questions and grade 9 answers:**
1. *Qu'est-ce que tu fais pour être en bonne santé ?* **Je vais au collège à pied tous les jours, parce que c'est bon pour la santé. Depuis septembre, je joue aussi dans une équipe de foot, et j'adore ça parce que j'oublie le stress des examens.** (extended: two activities, reasons, depuis)
2. *Qu'est-ce que tu as mangé hier ?* **Hier, j'ai mangé du poisson à midi, et le soir, ma mère a préparé du poulet avec des légumes. C'était très bon, mais je n'ai rien mangé après, parce que je n'avais plus faim.** (extended: perfect and imperfect, opinion, ne...rien, ne...plus)
3. *À ton avis, est-ce que les jeunes dorment assez ?* **Non, pas du tout. Beaucoup de jeunes passent trop de temps sur leur portable le soir. Il vaut mieux laisser son portable dans la cuisine avant de se coucher.** (developed well: opinion, reason, advice with Higher structures)
4. *Qu'est-ce que tu vas faire à l'avenir pour rester en forme ?* **L'année prochaine, je mangerai moins de gâteaux et je marcherai plus. Je voudrais aussi essayer la natation.** (developed: future tense, conditional)
:::

::: example Describing the photo card (Theme 2: popular culture)
Photo 1: a family eating outside around a birthday cake. Photo 2: young people at an outdoor concert.

*Sur la première photo, il y a une famille qui célèbre l'anniversaire de la grand-mère. Ils sont assis autour d'une grande table dans un jardin, et il y a un gâteau au milieu. Tout le monde sourit, donc je pense qu'ils sont contents d'être ensemble. Il fait beau, alors c'est peut-être l'été.*

*Sur la deuxième photo, je vois beaucoup de jeunes à un concert dehors. Sur la scène, il y a une chanteuse, et les jeunes chantent avec elle. Ils ont l'air heureux, mais il y a beaucoup de monde. Moi, je préfère écouter de la musique chez moi, parce que je n'aime pas le bruit.*

Both photos covered, who / where / what / weather / opinion, relative *qui*, *donc*, *alors*, *avoir l'air* + adjective. About 90 seconds spoken.
:::

::: example Developing an answer in the unprepared conversation
Teacher: *Comment est-ce que tu as célébré ton dernier anniversaire ?*

- **Minimal** (1 to 3 band): *Je suis allé au restaurant.*
- **Developed**: *Je suis allé au restaurant avec ma famille. C'était très bon.*
- **Extended** (13 to 15 band): *Pour mon dernier anniversaire, je suis allé au restaurant avec ma famille. Après avoir mangé, nous sommes allés au cinéma. C'était génial, mais le meilleur moment, c'était quand mes amis sont arrivés avec un gâteau. L'année prochaine, j'aurai dix-sept ans, et je voudrais faire une fête chez moi, parce que ce serait moins cher.*

The extended answer uses past, present and future, *après avoir* + past participle, a superlative, an opinion and a reason.
:::

::: q
How long is the preparation time, and what may you take into the test?
---
15 minutes; you may take in notes of any length written on the Additional answer sheet, and hand them in at the end.
:::

::: q
How many marks is each role-play task worth, and what earns full marks?
---
2 marks; the message must be conveyed without ambiguity.
:::

::: q
Which role-play task type is guaranteed by the spec?
---
You must ask a question.
:::

::: q
How are the four questions after the reading aloud marked?
---
As a whole, out of 10, not one by one; the top band needs all four answered clearly, at least two extended answers and one other developed well.
:::

::: q
What happens if you talk about only one photo?
---
You lose one mark from the description mark (unless the mark was already 1).
:::

::: q
When does marking stop in the Higher speaking test?
---
At 12 minutes.
:::

::: q
Give three structures that count as complex language in the conversation.
---
Any three from the Higher list, such as depuis + present, venir de, après avoir + past participle, avant de + infinitive, the future, the conditional, y or en, relative que or où, superlatives, il vaut mieux.
:::

::: q
What is the difference between a major and a minor error?
---
A major error affects communication; a minor error does not.
:::

::: watch
- **Role-play task missing a detail**: "how many nights and for how many people" needs both, or it scores 1.
- **Statement instead of a question**: the question task needs a real question with a question word or rising intonation.
- **Wrong time frame** in the role-play: "what you did" needs the perfect tense.
- **Reading aloud**: pronouncing silent endings (*ils parlent* said as 'parlont'), English 'th', a sounded h, *-ill-* said as 'l'.
- **Only one photo described**: minus one mark.
- **Minimal answers**: "Oui, j'aime ça" scores in the bottom band. Develop every answer.
- **Long pre-learnt speeches that ignore the question**: the conversation is unprepared, and irrelevant answers do not convey information for that question.
- **Running out of time**: marking stops at 12 minutes.
:::

::: test
Role-play task: "Ask a question about the price of the tickets." Give an answer that would score 2 marks. (2)
---
A clear question about price (1) with correct question form, such as *Combien coûtent les billets ?* or *C'est combien, un billet ?* (1).
:::

::: test
Role-play task: "Say what you did last weekend (two details)." Write a full-mark answer in French. (2)
---
Two past-tense details, 1 mark each: for example *Le week-end dernier, j'ai joué au foot (1) et je suis allé(e) au cinéma (1).*
:::

::: test
A candidate answers task 1 ("Say how many nights you want to stay and for how many people") with *Trois nuits.* How many marks, and why? (2)
---
1 mark (1): only one of the two required details is conveyed (1).
:::

::: test
In the reading aloud text *Les enfants sont partis tôt*, which final letters are silent, and where is there a liaison? (3)
---
Silent: the -ts of *enfants* and the -t of *sont* (1); the -s of *partis* and the -t of *tôt* (1); liaison: *les enfants*, where the s of *les* is sounded as a z, 'lay-zahn-fahn' (1).
:::

::: test
Turn *J'aime le sport.* into an extended answer. Include an opinion with a reason, a past or future time frame, and one Higher structure. (4)
---
Opinion with reason (1), a second time frame (1), a Higher structure (1), accurate French (1). Model: *J'aime le sport parce que c'est bon pour la santé. Je joue au foot depuis trois ans, et l'année dernière, mon équipe a gagné un match important. À l'avenir, je voudrais essayer la natation.*
:::

::: test
What are the recommended timings at Higher for (a) the role-play, (b) the photo description, and (c) the unprepared conversation? (3)
---
(a) 1 to 1.5 minutes (1). (b) About 1.5 minutes (1). (c) 4.5 to 5.5 minutes (1).
:::

::: test
List three things the top band for the unprepared conversation communication mark requires. (3)
---
A lot of information conveyed (1); consistent good development with regular extended responses (1); information always or nearly always conveyed clearly (1).
:::

::: test
Write two sentences in French describing a photo of a family on a beach, using *il y a* and *ils ont l'air* + adjective. (2)
---
One sentence with *il y a* (1) and one with *ils ont l'air* + an agreeing adjective (1). For example: *Sur la photo, il y a une famille à la plage. Ils ont l'air contents parce qu'il fait beau.*
:::
