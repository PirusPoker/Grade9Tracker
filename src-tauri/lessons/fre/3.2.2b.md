## What it is

This **Higher** lesson adds three verb forms to your Foundation set.

- **The future tense**: **je jouerai** = I will play, I'm going to play.
- **The conditional**: **je jouerais** = I would play.
- **The imperfect in full**: the plural forms **nous jouions, vous jouiez, ils jouaient**, on top of the singular from 3.2.1e.

The AQA list here is deliberately narrow, so learn exactly what it names and no more.

- **Future**: regular **-er verbs** in all six persons (**je parlerai … ils parleront**), plus the **singular** forms of four irregulars: **aurai/auras/aura** (avoir), **ferai/feras/fera** (faire), **irai/iras/ira** (aller), **serai/seras/sera** (être).
- **Conditional**: regular **-er verbs** in all six persons (**je parlerais … ils parleraient**), plus **aurais/aurait, ferais/ferait, irais/irait, serais/serait** and, from Foundation, **voudrais/voudrait**.
- **Not required**: the future or conditional of any other verb (for example **je finirai, je prendrai, je pourrais, nous serons**). For those, use the near future (**je vais finir**), which is always correct.
- **Imperfect**: plural forms of -er verbs, the nine anchor-verb patterns, and **avoir, être, faire** (**nous avions, vous étiez, ils faisaient**).

In the exam, the future and conditional are how Higher candidates show a **future time frame** with variety. The 150-word task often has a bullet such as "your plans for the future" or "what you would like to change". The speaking conversation asks **Qu'est-ce que tu feras l'année prochaine ?** or **Quel serait ton métier idéal ?** In **Listening** and **Reading**, the difference between **je travaillerai** (will), **je travaillerais** (would) and **je travaillais** (used to) is one or two letters, and questions test it.

A grade 9 answer uses all three time frames with more than one future form: **l'année prochaine, j'irai en France et je travaillerai dans un hôtel ; plus tard, j'aimerais habiter à l'étranger**. It uses the conditional for wishes and opinions (**ce serait génial**, **je préférerais**) and the plural imperfect for shared past habits (**quand nous étions petits, nous jouions ensemble**).

::: terms
- **le futur simple**: will + verb: -er infinitive + -ai, -as, -a, -ons, -ez, -ont: je visiterai, ils visiteront
- **aurai, ferai, irai, serai**: I will have, do, go, be (singular forms listed: aura, fera, ira, sera for il/elle)
- **il y aura**: there will be: il y aura moins de voitures
- **le conditionnel**: would + verb: -er infinitive + -ais, -ais, -ait, -ions, -iez, -aient: j'aimerais, nous aimerions
- **aurais, ferais, irais, serais**: I would have, do, go, be; aurait, ferait, irait, serait for il/elle: ce serait génial
- **je voudrais / j'aimerais**: I would like: the two most useful conditionals for plans and wishes
- **l'imparfait au pluriel**: -ions, -iez, -aient on the nous stem: nous habitions, vous finissiez, ils prenaient
- **nous avions, nous étions, nous faisions**: the plural imperfects of avoir, être, faire: ils étaient contents
- **futur ou conditionnel ?**: -rai (will) versus -rais (would): je jouerai / je jouerais sound almost alike, so read the spelling carefully
:::

::: method
**Future (-er verbs)**
1. Keep the **whole infinitive**: travailler.
2. Add **-ai, -as, -a, -ons, -ez, -ont**: **je travaillerai, nous travaillerons**.
3. For avoir, faire, aller, être, use the listed singular forms: **j'aurai, tu feras, il ira, elle sera**.
4. Any other verb, or a plural of those four? Use **aller + infinitive**: **nous allons être, je vais prendre**.

**Conditional (-er verbs)**
1. Keep the whole infinitive: aimer.
2. Add the imperfect endings **-ais, -ais, -ait, -ions, -iez, -aient**: **j'aimerais, ils aimeraient**.
3. Irregular singular: **j'aurais, je ferais, j'irais, je serais**, and **je voudrais**.

**Imperfect plural**
1. Take the **nous** form of the present and remove **-ons**: nous prenons → pren-. For être the stem is **ét-**.
2. Add **-ions, -iez, -aient**: **nous prenions, vous preniez, ils prenaient**.
:::

::: example The future, step by step
| | visiter (future) | aller (future, singular only) | être (future, singular only) |
|---|---|---|---|
| je/j' | visiterai | irai | serai |
| tu | visiteras | iras | seras |
| il/elle/on | visitera | ira | sera |
| nous | visiterons | *not required* | *not required* |
| vous | visiterez | *not required* | *not required* |
| ils/elles | visiteront | *not required* | *not required* |

**English:** Next summer I will go to France. I will visit Paris with my brother and we will stay in a hotel. It will be great.

1. "I will go": aller, singular → **j'irai**.
2. "I will visit": -er → **je visiterai**.
3. "we will stay": -er, plural → **nous resterons**.
4. "It will be great": être, singular → **ce sera génial**.

**French:** **L'été prochain, j'irai en France. Je visiterai Paris avec mon frère et nous resterons dans un hôtel. Ce sera génial.**
:::

::: example The conditional for wishes and opinions
**English:** In an ideal world, I would work abroad. I would like to live in Canada because it would be interesting, and my parents would spend the summer with me.

1. "I would work": -er → **je travaillerais**.
2. "I would like to live" → **j'aimerais habiter** (or **je voudrais habiter**).
3. "it would be" → **ce serait**.
4. "my parents would spend": passer is -er, plural → **mes parents passeraient**.

**French:** **Dans un monde idéal, je travaillerais à l'étranger. J'aimerais habiter au Canada parce que ce serait intéressant, et mes parents passeraient l'été avec moi.**

If the English uses a verb whose conditional is not on the list (for example "would come", "would be able to"), **rephrase with a listed -er verb** or with **j'aimerais / je voudrais + infinitive** rather than guess a form.
:::

::: example The imperfect in full
| | jouer | finir | prendre | avoir | être | faire |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| nous | jouions | finissions | prenions | avions | étions | faisions |
| vous | jouiez | finissiez | preniez | aviez | étiez | faisiez |
| ils/elles | jouaient | finissaient | prenaient | avaient | étaient | faisaient |

**English:** When we were little, my brother and I used to go to the beach every summer. We used to play in the sea while our parents watched us.

1. "we were little" → **nous étions petits**.
2. "we used to go" → **nous allions**.
3. "We used to play" → **nous jouions**.
4. "our parents watched us" (ongoing) → **nos parents nous regardaient** (plural direct object pronoun, see 3.2.2a).

**French:** **Quand nous étions petits, mon frère et moi, nous allions à la plage tous les étés. Nous jouions dans la mer pendant que nos parents nous regardaient.**

**Grade-9 flourish (not required by AQA, but credited if correct):** si + imperfect, then conditional: **Si j'avais plus d'argent, je voyagerais partout.** Never needed for a mark; use it only if you are sure of both verbs.
:::

:::

Rewrite in the near future, then in the future tense: (a) Je joue au foot. (b) Nous restons à la maison. (c) Elle va en ville.
---

:::

Will, would or used to? Translate: (a) je mangerai (b) je mangerais (c) je mangeais (d) ils parleraient (e) ils parlaient
---

Spot and correct the error: (a) Demain, je jouerais au foot. (meaning "I will play") (b) L'année prochaine, j'allerai en France. (c) Quand ils étaient jeunes, ils jouaient au foot et ils étais contents. (d) Plus tard, je voudrai habiter à l'étranger. (meaning "I would like")
---
(a) je jouerai; (b) j'irai; (c) ils étaient contents; (d) je voudrais (the listed conditional of vouloir; the future of vouloir is not required).
:::

::: q
Read: *À l'avenir, ma ville sera différente. Il y aura moins de voitures et les gens utiliseront plus les transports en commun. Moi, j'aimerais habiter à la campagne, mais mes amis préféreraient rester en ville. Quand nous étions petits, nous jouions dans la rue, mais aujourd'hui il y a trop de circulation.*
(a) What will there be less of? (b) What will people use more? (c) Where would the writer like to live? (d) What did they use to do when they were little?
---
(a) Cars. (b) Public transport. (c) In the countryside. (d) Play in the street.
:::

::: watch
- Future **-rai** (will) and conditional **-rais** (would) differ by one letter. In writing, check each one.
- Only **-er verbs** have a regular future and conditional on the list. For other verbs, use **aller + infinitive**, or a listed form such as **j'irai, je ferai, je serai, j'aurai**.
- **Singular only** for the four irregular futures: *nous serons* and *ils iront* are not required. Say **nous allons être**, **ils vont aller**.
- The future keeps the whole infinitive: **je mangerai**, not *je mangrai*.
- **ce sera** (it will be) and **ce serait** (it would be) are two of the most useful phrases in writing.
- Imperfect plural endings **-ions, -iez, -aient**. Do not mix them with the present (**nous jouons** is the present, **nous jouions** the imperfect).
- A future bullet can be answered with the near future, but using both it and the future tense shows range for the complexity mark.
:::
