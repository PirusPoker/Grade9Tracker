## What it is

**My personal world** is the first of Edexcel's six thematic contexts: **family**, **friends**, **relationships** and **equality** (how people are treated whatever their gender, identity, disability or background). Edexcel has one shared vocabulary list for all six themes, so this lesson gathers the words and structures this theme keeps calling on.

Where it turns up in the four papers:
- **Paper 1 Speaking.** A read-aloud card can be on this theme, followed by two unprepared present-tense questions. If you choose "My personal world" for the picture task, you describe a picture (a family meal, friends in a park), answer two set questions (the second in the past) and hold a five-minute conversation. Role plays are transactional, so this theme rarely appears there.
- **Papers 2 and 3 Listening and Reading.** Short accounts of family change, best friends, arguments, role models or a campaign for equal rights, with questions in English.
- **Paper 4 Writing.** Question 1 is an informal email of 80–90 words to a Spanish-speaking friend, and "tell your friend about your family" is the most natural version of it. The translation into Spanish nearly always has a sentence about a relative or a friend.

### Describing people

| Structure | Example | English |
|---|---|---|
| ser + personality | Mi hermana es muy trabajadora y responsable. | My sister is very hard-working and responsible. |
| tener + age, hair, eyes | Tiene catorce años, el pelo corto y los ojos azules. | He is fourteen, with short hair and blue eyes. |
| llevar + glasses, clothes | Mi abuelo lleva gafas. | My grandfather wears glasses. |
| estar + mood or state | Hoy mi madre está cansada; mis tíos están separados. | Today my mum is tired; my aunt and uncle are separated. |

Listed personality adjectives: **simpático, divertido, alegre, serio, tranquilo, trabajador, responsable, independiente, estricto**; Higher adds **fiel** (loyal), **pesado** (annoying, boring), **irritante, optimista, nervioso** and **listo** (clever, ready). Trabajador is irregular in the feminine: **trabajadora**. Adjectives ending in -e (alegre, independiente) or a consonant (fiel) have one form for both genders: mi tía es fiel, mis amigos son fieles.

### Getting on with people

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Me llevo bien / mal con mi padrastro. | I get on well / badly with my stepfather. |
| Nos llevamos muy bien. | We get on very well (with each other). |
| Discuto con mi hermano por la tele. | I argue with my brother about the TV. |
| Cuando éramos pequeños, nos peleábamos. | When we were little, we used to fight. |
| Mi madre siempre me apoya. | My mum always supports me. |
| Puedo confiar en mi mejor amiga. | I can trust my best friend. |
| Conozco a Jorge desde hace cinco años. | I have known Jorge for five years. (Higher: desde hace + present) |
| Lo que más me gusta de él es que es fiel. | What I like most about him is that he is loyal. |
| Mi abuela es mi modelo de conducta. | My grandmother is my role model. (a listed short phrase) |

**Relationships and the future:** casarse (to get married), separarse (to separate, split up), enamorarse (to fall in love), vivir juntos (to live together). Weddings and divorce are not on the list (boda and divorciarse are not there), so say mis padres se separaron and me gustaría casarme. At Higher, the future after **cuando** takes the present subjunctive, and only five verbs are required: **cuando sea mayor** (when I am older), **cuando tenga treinta años**, **cuando vaya a la universidad**.

### Equality

Equality is a named subject in Edexcel's themes, so expect a reading text or conversation question on it. Listed words: **la igualdad** (equality), **el derecho** (right: los derechos de las mujeres), **la discapacidad** and **con discapacidad** (disability, disabled: una persona con discapacidad), **el racismo**, **pobre** (poor), **la manifestación** (protest, demonstration) and **manifestarse** (to protest), **luchar por** (to fight for), **respetar** and **el respeto**, **el género** (gender), and the identity adjectives **gay, lesbiana, bisexual, heterosexual, transgénero, no binario**.

Useful sentences: Todos tenemos los mismos derechos (we all have the same rights). Hay que respetar a todo el mundo (you have to respect everyone). Creo que las mujeres y los hombres deberían tener el mismo salario (I think women and men should have the same salary).

**Inclusive language.** Pearson credits non-binary pronouns and agreements, such as **elle** and **-e** endings (elle es mi amigue), when they are used correctly and consistently. You never need them, and the standard forms always score full marks.

::: terms
- **llevarse bien / mal con**: to get on well or badly with someone; reflexive, so me llevo, te llevas, nos llevamos.
- **discutir**: to argue (also to discuss); discuto con mi padre por los deberes.
- **pelearse**: to fight, quarrel; cuando éramos pequeños nos peleábamos mucho.
- **apoyar**: to support; mis padres siempre me apoyan.
- **la pareja**: partner or couple, always feminine whoever it refers to.
- **-astro, -astra**: the listed ending for step-relations: hermanastro, hermanastra.
- **separarse**: to separate, split up; mis padres se separaron hace dos años.
- **fiel**: loyal (Higher); one form for both genders, plural fieles.
- **modelo de conducta**: role model, a listed short phrase; mi madre es mi modelo de conducta.
- **la igualdad**: equality; luchar por la igualdad = to fight for equality.
- **con discapacidad**: disabled; una persona con discapacidad, not "una persona discapacidad".
- **desde hace**: for (a length of time) with the present tense: la conozco desde hace un año.
:::

::: method
1. **Pick the right verb to describe a person.** Ser for personality and nationality, tener for age, hair and eyes, llevar for glasses, estar for moods and states (está cansado, están separados).
2. **Make every adjective agree with the person**, not with you: mi hermana es trabajadora, mis primos son simpáticos.
3. **Say how you get on and give a reason**: me llevo bien con… porque…; add a contrast with pero or aunque.
4. **Use one Higher structure per answer**: lo que más me gusta es que…, conozco a… desde hace…, cuando sea mayor…
5. **Move through the time frames**: imperfect for childhood (cuando era pequeño), preterite for one event (el verano pasado fuimos…), future or conditional for plans.
6. **In reading and listening, check who and when.** Questions often hinge on which relative did something, or whether it is now, used to be, or will be.
7. **On equality, give an opinion with a reason** using the listed words: creo que todos deberían tener los mismos derechos porque…
:::

::: example Paper 4 Question 1 (18): an email about your family
**Task (set in English, as on the real paper).** Write an email to your Spanish friend about your family. You must include the following points:
- who is in your family
- your opinion of one person in your family, with reasons
- something you did with your family recently
- a family celebration in the future.

Write your answer in Spanish. You should aim to write between 80 and 90 words.

**Model (88 words).**
¡Hola, Diego! En mi familia somos cinco: mis padres, mi hermana gemela, mi medio hermano y yo. Me llevo muy bien con mi madre porque es alegre y siempre me apoya, aunque a veces es un poco estricta. Lo que más me gusta de ella es que nunca se enfada. El domingo pasado fuimos todos al campo y comimos en un restaurante pequeño. ¡Fue un día estupendo! El año que viene mis abuelos celebrarán su aniversario y vamos a organizar una fiesta enorme en el jardín. ¡Escríbeme pronto!

**Why it scores.** All four bullets are covered and each has an extra detail, so AO2 reaches 11–13. AO3 gets 5: present, preterite and future, an opinion with porque, a contrast with aunque, a Higher structure (lo que más me gusta) and agreements made (estricta, enorme). Words beyond the list (aniversario, estupendo) earn the same credit as listed words, no more.
:::

::: example Paper 1 picture task: "My personal world"
**The picture you chose (described).** A family of three generations is eating at a long table in a garden. The grandmother is cutting a cake, two children are laughing and a man is taking a photo with his phone.

**Description, covering people, location and activity (8).**
En la foto hay una familia grande: hay unos abuelos, unos padres y dos niños. Están en un jardín y hace sol. Creo que están celebrando el cumpleaños de la abuela, porque ella está cortando un pastel. Los niños se están riendo y el padre está sacando una foto con el móvil. Todos parecen muy contentos.

**Compulsory question 1 (present).** ¿Cómo celebras tu cumpleaños normalmente?
Normalmente salgo a cenar con mi familia y después invito a mis amigos a casa para ver una película.

**Compulsory question 2 (past).** ¿Adónde fuiste con tu familia recientemente?
El mes pasado fuimos a la costa para visitar a mis tíos. Pasamos el día en la playa y por la noche cenamos juntos.

**Why it works.** The description names who (people), where (location) and what they are doing (activity, with the present continuous está cortando, se están riendo). Each answer is a full sentence with a detail, so it earns 2; "a la costa" alone would earn 1.
:::

::: example Reading: two correct statements and an inference word
> Desde hace un año, mi prima Natalia vive con nosotros porque sus padres trabajan en Ecuador. Al principio estaba triste y no hablaba mucho, pero ahora es como una hermana para mí. Mi hermano pequeño, Hugo, está un poco celoso, porque dice que mis padres pasan más tiempo con ella que con él.

**(a) Write the letters of the two correct statements.** A Natalia has lived with the family for a year. B Natalia's parents live in Spain. C At first Natalia was very chatty. D Hugo thinks Natalia gets more attention than him. (2)

**(b) Which is the best translation of celoso?** A jealous B proud C ill (1)

**Answer.** (a) A (1), from desde hace un año + vive. D (1): "pasan más tiempo con ella que con él". B is wrong (Ecuador) and C is the opposite (no hablaba mucho). (b) A (1). Celoso is not on the list, so use the context: Hugo complains that his parents spend more time with Natalia than with him.
:::

::: q
Complete with the correct form of ser or estar: "Mi padrastro ___ muy tranquilo, pero hoy ___ enfadado." (2)
---
es (1), a personality trait; está (1), a temporary state today.
:::

::: q
Make the adjectives agree: "Mis tías son (trabajador) y (alegre), y mi prima es muy (fiel)." (3)
---
trabajadoras (1), with the -a ending added before the plural -s; alegres (1); fiel (1), no change in the feminine.
:::

::: q
Translate into Spanish: "We get on well, but sometimes we argue." (2)
---
Nos llevamos bien (1), pero a veces discutimos (1). The reflexive pronoun nos is needed with llevarse.
:::

::: q
Translate into Spanish: "I have known my best friend for eight years." (2)
---
Conozco a mi mejor amigo / amiga (1) desde hace ocho años (1). Present tense with desde hace; personal a before a person. Reject "he conocido".
:::

::: q
Higher. Complete with the present subjunctive: "Cuando (ser) mayor, quiero vivir en el extranjero." (1)
---
sea (1). Future meaning after cuando, so the subjunctive; ser is one of the five listed verbs.
:::

::: q
Translate into Spanish: "My half-sister is disabled and she fights for the rights of disabled people." (3)
---
Mi media hermana tiene una discapacidad / es una persona con discapacidad (1); y lucha por (1); los derechos de las personas con discapacidad (1). Media agrees with hermana.
:::

::: q
Translate into English: "Cuando era pequeño, me peleaba con mi hermanastro, pero ahora nos llevamos muy bien." (3)
---
When I was little / young (1), I used to fight / argue with my stepbrother (1), but now we get on very well (1).
:::

::: watch
- **Tener, not ser, for age**: tengo quince años. Soy quince años is the classic error in Pearson's own list of minor errors; it costs accuracy every time.
- **Ser for character, estar for states**: es simpática (her nature), está triste (now), están separados (a state).
- **Agreement with the person described**: mi abuela es trabajadora, mis primas son altas. Missed endings add up in AO3.
- **Possessives agree with the thing owned**: mis padres, nuestra casa, su hermano (his, her or their brother).
- **Read the whole sentence in reading questions.** "Mis padres pasan más tiempo con ella que con él" tells you who gets the attention; an answer that names the wrong person scores 0.
- **Cover every bullet in Paper 4.** A strong email that skips one bullet is capped in the 8–10 band for AO2, however good the Spanish.
:::

::: test
Translate into English (Paper 3 Section B style).

> Mi abuela tiene ochenta años y vive con nosotros desde hace un año. Es una persona muy alegre y siempre tiene tiempo para escucharme. Cuando era joven, trabajaba en un hospital en Madrid. El verano pasado celebramos su cumpleaños con una fiesta enorme en el jardín. Para mí, es un verdadero modelo de conducta y quiero ser como ella. (10)
---
Marked as a whole. **9–10**: the meaning is fully and naturally transferred, with at most a slip that does not affect it. **6–8**: most of the meaning is transferred; occasional errors blur a detail. **3–5**: parts are transferred, but some errors lose the message. **1–2**: only isolated parts make sense.

**Model.** My grandmother is eighty and has been living with us for a year. She is a very cheerful person and always has time to listen to me. When she was young, she worked in a hospital in Madrid. **Key points:** desde hace + present (has been living … for a year, not "lives with us since a year"); escucharme (to listen to me); trabajaba (worked / used to work); celebramos is past here because of el verano pasado; modelo de conducta (role model).
