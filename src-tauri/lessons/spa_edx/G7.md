## What it is

**Derivational morphology** means building a new word from a word you already know by adding an **affix**: a small piece at the end of the word. **Libro** (book) becomes **librito** (little book); **rápida** (quick) becomes **rápidamente** (quickly).

Edexcel lists five suffixes. Pearson may use them on **any base word in the vocabulary list**, mainly in **Paper 3 (Reading)**. A derived word counts as known vocabulary, even if it is not printed in the list. The reverse also applies: if a derived word is listed, its base word can appear. So **one listed word gives you several free words**, provided you can recognise the pattern.

Pearson sets three limits:
- **Only one suffix** from this list is added to a base word. You may see **recomendable** (recomendar + -able), but never recomendablemente with two suffixes stacked.
- **Ordinary grammar endings can still be added** to the derived word: **recomendables** (plural), **carísimas** (feminine plural), **casitas**.
- **The English meaning must match the suffix**: "little", "very", "-ly", "-ity" or "-able". Words where the suffix has drifted to another meaning are not tested this way.

These suffixes are on the Foundation + Higher list. The grammar list does not ask you to produce them, so in Paper 4 **muy caro** is as good as **carísimo**. A correct derived form still adds variety.

### 1. -ito / -ita: "little" or affection

Add **-ito / -ita** to a noun. If the noun ends in -o or -a, drop that vowel first:

| base | derived | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| libro | **librito** | little book |
| casa | **casita** | little house, cottage |
| perro | **perrito** | little dog, puppy |
| gato | **gatito** | kitten |
| hermana | **hermanita** | little sister (or said with affection) |
| abuela | **abuelita** | granny |
| árbol | **arbolito** | little tree |

- **The gender stays the same**: la casa → la casita.
- **Plurals work as normal**: dos gatitos.
- **Spelling adjusts to keep the sound** before i: **c → qu** (chico → **chiquito**), **g → gu** (amiga → **amiguita**). This is the same SSC rule you need for the dictation (see G8).
- **The accent can disappear** because the stress moves: árbol → arbolito.
- **Endearment** is often the point. **Mi abuelita** is not a small grandmother; it shows affection. In a translation, "granny" or "my grandma" works better than "my little grandmother".
- **Not every word ending in -ito is a diminutive**: **bonito** (pretty), **favorito** (favourite) and **éxito** (success) are ordinary words.

### 2. -ísimo / -ísima: "very"

Add **-ísimo** to an adjective. If it ends in -o or -a, drop that vowel first. The result agrees like any adjective (-ísimo, -ísima, -ísimos, -ísimas):

| base | derived | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| caro | **carísimo** | very expensive |
| barato | **baratísimo** | very cheap |
| bueno | **buenísimo** | very good |
| interesante | **interesantísimo** | very interesting |
| fácil | **facilísimo** | very easy |
| difícil | **dificilísimo** | very difficult |
| rápido | **rapidísimo** | very fast |
| rico | **riquísimo** | very tasty / very rich |
| largo | **larguísimo** | very long |

- **The new accent is always on the í** of -ísimo, so any old accent goes: **fácil → facilísimo**, **rápido → rapidísimo**, **cómodo → comodísimo**.
- **Spelling keeps the sound**: **rico → riquísimo** (c → qu), **largo → larguísimo** (g → gu).
- **-ísimo is stronger than muy.** Las entradas eran carísimas means "the tickets were really expensive".

### 3. -mente: "-ly"

Add **-mente** to the **feminine singular** form of the adjective. Adjectives with one form for both genders (ending in -e or a consonant) just take -mente:

| adjective | form used | adverb | meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| rápido | rápida | **rápidamente** | quickly |
| tranquilo | tranquila | **tranquilamente** | calmly, quietly |
| cómodo | cómoda | **cómodamente** | comfortably |
| lento | lenta | **lentamente** | slowly |
| perfecto | perfecta | **perfectamente** | perfectly |
| alegre | alegre | **alegremente** | cheerfully |
| triste | triste | **tristemente** | sadly |
| fácil | fácil | **fácilmente** | easily |
| feliz | feliz | **felizmente** | happily |
| normal | normal | **normalmente** | normally |

- **The adjective keeps its accent**: **rápidamente, fácilmente, cómodamente**.
- **Rápidomente is wrong**: always build on the feminine form.
- Many -mente words are already on the list (**normalmente, finalmente, generalmente, rápidamente**), so learn them as words too.

### 4. -idad: "-ity"

Add **-idad** to an adjective. If it ends in a consonant, add it straight on. If it ends in -o, drop the vowel first. Every -idad noun is **feminine**: la, una:

| adjective | noun | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| nacional | **la nacionalidad** | nationality |
| personal | **la personalidad** | personality |
| popular | **la popularidad** | popularity |
| original | **la originalidad** | originality |
| real | **la realidad** | reality |
| público | **la publicidad** | publicity, advertising |
| activo | **la actividad** | activity |
| diverso | **la diversidad** | diversity |
| tranquilo | **la tranquilidad** | tranquillity, peace and quiet |

**En realidad** means "actually" or "in fact", not "really". **La publicidad** is usually "advertising".

### 5. -able: "-able"

Add **-able** to the stem of an **-ar verb**. The new adjective is the same for both genders and adds -s in the plural:

| verb | adjective | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| comparar | **comparable** | comparable |
| recomendar | **recomendable** | recommendable, advisable |
| aceptar | **aceptable** | acceptable |
| reciclar | **reciclable** | recyclable |
| lavar | **lavable** | washable |
| respetar | **respetable** | respectable |
| evitar | **evitable** | avoidable |

**Es recomendable** + infinitive is a natural way to say "it is advisable to": **es recomendable llevar agua**.

### Working out a word you have never seen

In Paper 3, Pearson also sets **two inference questions**: "Which of these is the best translation of the word …?" The word is **not** on the list, but the context makes it clear. Derived words are a separate thing, because they count as known. The same detective work cracks both:

1. **Look for a word you know inside it.** Reciclable → reciclar. Hermanita → hermana.
2. **Identify the ending**: -ito (little), -ísimo (very), -mente (-ly), -idad (-ity), -able (-able).
3. **Put them together, then make it natural English.** Lentamente → "slowly". Abuelita → "granny".
4. **Check the context.** Is it positive or negative? Is it a person, a thing or a place? Does the next sentence explain it?
5. **Use cognates, but check for false friends.** Many -idad and -able words are near-identical in English. Actual means "current", not "actual".

::: terms
- **derivational morphology**: making a new word by adding a suffix to a listed base word: libro → librito.
- **base word**: the listed word inside a derived word: reciclar inside reciclable, rápida inside rápidamente.
- **suffix**: an ending added to change a word's meaning or type: -ito, -ísimo, -mente, -idad, -able.
- **-ito / -ita**: noun ending meaning little or showing affection: casita, gatito, abuelita.
- **-ísimo / -ísima**: adjective ending meaning very; it agrees and carries its own accent: carísimas, facilísimo.
- **-mente**: adverb ending meaning -ly, added to the feminine form: rápidamente, alegremente.
- **-idad**: noun ending meaning -ity; the noun is always feminine: la publicidad, la nacionalidad.
- **-able**: adjective ending on an -ar verb stem meaning -able: recomendable, reciclable, lavable.
- **one-affix rule**: Pearson adds only one of these suffixes to a word, though gender and plural endings may follow.
- **inference word**: an off-list word in Paper 3 whose meaning you must deduce from context (1 mark each).
:::

::: method
1. **Spot the ending**: -ito/-ita, -ísimo/-ísima, -mente, -idad or -able (plus any -s or -a for agreement).
2. **Strip it off and rebuild the base**: put back the -o or -a, undo qu → c and gu → g, and put back a lost accent (facilísimo → fácil).
3. **Translate the base word**, then add the suffix's meaning: little, very, -ly, -ity, -able.
4. **Make it natural English**: abuelita → granny; carísimo → really expensive; en realidad → actually.
5. **Check it fits the context** of the sentence and the question being asked.
6. **When writing**, build -mente on the feminine form, put the accent on the í of -ísimo, and make -ísimo agree.
:::

::: example Breaking a word down
For each word, give the base word and the meaning: (a) gatito (b) carísimas (c) tranquilamente. (6)

**Answer.**
- (a) **gato** (1); kitten / little cat (1).
- (b) **caro** (1); very expensive (1). Feminine plural, so it describes something like las entradas.
- (c) **tranquilo / tranquila** (1); calmly / quietly (1).
:::

::: example Building adverbs
Form the adverb ending in -mente from: (a) lento (b) fácil (c) alegre. (3)

**Answer.** (a) **lentamente** (1): the feminine form lenta + -mente. (b) **fácilmente** (1): one form for both genders; the accent stays. (c) **alegremente** (1).
:::

::: example -ísimo with agreement
Complete with the -ísimo form of the adjective in brackets: (a) La paella estaba ___ (rico). (b) Las vacaciones fueron ___ (largo). (2)

**Answer.** (a) **riquísima** (1): c → qu, feminine to agree with la paella. (b) **larguísimas** (1): g → gu, feminine plural to agree with las vacaciones.
:::

::: example An inference word in context
Read the sentence. Which is the best translation of the word **lavable**?

"Esta chaqueta es lavable, así que puedes limpiarla en casa sin problemas."

A — expensive; B — washable; C — waterproof. (1)

**Answer.** **B** (1). Lavar (to wash) + -able; the rest of the sentence says you can clean it at home.
:::

::: example The one-affix rule
Explain why Pearson could print **recomendables** but would not print a word combining -able and -mente. (2)

**Answer.** Only one derivational suffix is added to a base word (1). Recomendables is recomendar + -able, plus an ordinary plural -s, which is allowed (1).
:::

Correct the error: "Mi hermano conduce rápidomente." (1)
---
rápidamente (1): -mente goes on the feminine form.

::: q
Why is "facilísimo" written with no accent on the a? (1)
---
The stress moves to the í of -ísimo, so the old accent of fácil is dropped (1).

::: q
Translate into English: "En realidad, la casita es comodísima." (2)
---
Actually / In fact (1), the cottage / little house is very comfortable (1). Accept "in reality"; reject "really".
:::

::: watch
- **Use the feminine form before -mente**: rápidamente, tranquilamente; rápidomente is wrong.
- **Keep the adjective's accent with -mente** (fácilmente) but **drop it with -ísimo** (facilísimo).
- **Make -ísimo agree**: las entradas carísimas, la comida riquísima.
- **Remember the spelling changes that keep the sound**: rico → riquísimo, largo → larguísimo, chico → chiquito.
- **-idad nouns are feminine**: la publicidad, una actividad.
- **Translate the derived word naturally**: abuelita is "granny", not "small grandmother"; en realidad is "actually".
- **Don't treat every -ito word as "little"**: bonito, favorito and éxito are ordinary words.
- **In the inference questions, test each option against the context** before you choose.
:::

::: test
Translate this passage into English.

> Mi abuelita vive sola en un pueblo pequeñísimo en la montaña. Cada domingo la visito con mi hermano y hablamos tranquilamente durante horas. Su casa es antigua, pero es comodísima y tiene un jardín precioso. Para ella, la tranquilidad es lo más importante, aunque en realidad allí no pasa nada. Es un sitio muy recomendable para descansar. (10)
---
Marked as a whole. **9–10**: the full meaning is transferred in natural English. **6–8**: most of the meaning comes across; occasional errors blur a detail. **3–5**: parts are transferred, but some errors lose the message. **1–2**: only isolated parts are clear.

**Model.** My granny lives alone in a tiny village in the mountains. Every Sunday I visit her with my brother and we chat calmly for hours. Her house is old, but it is really comfortable and has a beautiful garden. For her, peace and quiet is the most important thing, although actually nothing happens there. It is a highly recommended place to relax.

**Key points:** abuelita (granny); pequeñísimo (tiny / very small); la visito (I visit her); tranquilamente (calmly); comodísima (very comfortable); la tranquilidad (peace and quiet); lo más importante (the most important thing); en realidad (actually); recomendable (recommended / advisable).
:::
