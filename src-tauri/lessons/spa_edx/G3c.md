## What it is

The **imperfect** is the past tense for things that **used to happen**, things that **were happening**, and **description in the past** (what something was like, the weather, how people felt). The preterite tells you **what happened**; the imperfect sets the scene around it. You need both in any past narrative: in the Paper 1 conversation, in the past bullet of the Paper 4 writing tasks, and in reading and listening texts about childhood, holidays and changes.

Edexcel's grammar list requires:
- the imperfect of **regular -ar, -er and -ir verbs** and of **ser, ir and ver**, in **all six persons at Higher** (Foundation has the singular only);
- its two uses: **habitual** (I used to walk) and **continuous** (I was walking);
- the **imperfect continuous**: the imperfect of **estar + present participle** (estaba comiendo), in the **singular persons** (estaba, estabas, estaba). The plural (estábamos, estabais, estaban) is not required, but it is credited if correct.

The list also prints **había** (there was, there were, there used to be), and the vocabulary list gives **tenía**, **estaba** and **hacía** forms you will use all the time.

### Forming the imperfect

Take off -ar, -er or -ir and add these endings. **-er and -ir verbs share one set.**

| | hablar | comer | vivir |
|---|---|---|---|
| yo | hablaba | comía | vivía |
| tú | hablabas | comías | vivías |
| él / ella / usted | hablaba | comía | vivía |
| nosotros/as | hablábamos | comíamos | vivíamos |
| vosotros/as | hablabais | comíais | vivíais |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | hablaban | comían | vivían |

- **Accents:** every -er and -ir form has **í**. The -ar forms have an accent only in nosotros: **hablábamos**.
- **Yo and él are the same form** (hablaba, comía). Context or a name tells you who: **Mi madre trabajaba en un banco.**
- **No stem changes.** Verbs that change in the present are regular here: **podía, quería, jugaba, dormía, pedía, tenía, hacía, estaba**.

### Only three irregular verbs

| | ser | ir | ver |
|---|---|---|---|
| yo | era | iba | veía |
| tú | eras | ibas | veías |
| él / ella / usted | era | iba | veía |
| nosotros/as | éramos | íbamos | veíamos |
| vosotros/as | erais | ibais | veíais |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | eran | iban | veían |

Note the accents on **éramos** and **íbamos**, and **veía** keeps the e of ver.

### Use 1: habits, "used to"

The imperfect describes what happened **regularly** in the past:
- **Cuando era pequeña, iba al colegio a pie.** (When I was little, I used to walk to school.)
- **Todos los veranos veíamos a nuestros primos.** (Every summer we used to see our cousins.)
- **Mi abuelo trabajaba en una fábrica.** (My grandad worked / used to work in a factory.)

English often uses the simple past for a habit ("I walked to school"), so look at the meaning, not the English form. Signal phrases: **cuando era pequeño/a, de pequeño/a, de niño/a, antes, siempre, todos los días, cada semana, a menudo, normalmente**. At Higher, **soler** in the imperfect gives the same meaning: **solía ir al parque** (I used to go to the park).

### Use 2: description and background

Anything that describes how things **were**, rather than what **happened**, takes the imperfect:
- **weather:** **hacía sol / frío / calor**, **llovía**, **nevaba**;
- **time and age:** **eran las ocho**, **era la una**, **tenía diez años**;
- **appearance, character and places:** **mi profesora era muy simpática**, **la casa tenía un jardín**, **el hotel estaba cerca del mar**;
- **feelings and states:** **estaba cansado**, **tenía miedo**, **me sentía triste**;
- **what there was:** **había mucha gente**, **no había nada que hacer**.

### Use 3: "was …ing": the imperfect and the imperfect continuous

An action **in progress** in the past can be expressed in two ways:
- the plain imperfect: **Leía en el jardín.** (I was reading in the garden.)
- the imperfect continuous, **estaba + gerund**, which stresses that it was going on at that moment: **Estaba leyendo en el jardín.**

| estar (imperfect) | + gerund |
|---|---|
| estaba | Estaba haciendo los deberes. (I was doing my homework.) |
| estabas | ¿Qué estabas viendo? (What were you watching?) |
| estaba | Mi hermano estaba jugando con el móvil. (My brother was playing on his phone.) |

Gerunds are formed as in G3a: **-ando**, **-iendo**, with **leyendo**, **pidiendo** and **durmiendo**. The plural forms (**estábamos hablando**, **estaban esperando**) are not required, but they are credited if correct.

### Imperfect or preterite?

Think of a film: the imperfect is the **scenery and the background action**; the preterite is **each event that moves the story on**.

| imperfect | preterite |
|---|---|
| repeated or habitual: **íbamos a la playa todos los días** | one completed event: **un día fuimos a la montaña** |
| background, description: **hacía sol, había mucha gente** | an event at a specific time: **a las tres empezó a llover** |
| an action in progress: **estaba durmiendo** | the action that interrupts it: **cuando sonó el teléfono** |
| how something was: **el hotel era bonito** | a verdict on the whole event: **las vacaciones fueron geniales** |

**The interrupted action** is the classic exam structure. The longer action is in the imperfect, and the short one that cuts in is in the preterite:
- **Estaba comiendo cuando sonó el teléfono.** (I was eating when the phone rang.)
- **Mientras mi madre preparaba la comida, llegaron mis tíos.** (While my mum was making lunch, my aunt and uncle arrived.)

**Two common traps.**
- A **fixed, finished period** takes the preterite, however long it is: **Viví en Madrid tres años** (I lived in Madrid for three years, and that period is over). Compare **Cuando era pequeño, vivía en Madrid** (background to my childhood).
- **"Was" in a description is era or estaba, not fue**: **la playa era preciosa**. **Fue** sums up a whole event: **el concierto fue increíble**.

::: terms
- **imperfect**: the past tense for habits, description and actions in progress: jugaba, comía, vivía.
- **imperfect endings**: -aba, -abas, -aba, -ábamos, -abais, -aban; -ía, -ías, -ía, -íamos, -íais, -ían.
- **ser, ir, ver**: the only three irregular imperfects: era, iba, veía.
- **había**: there was, there were, there used to be: había un cine en mi pueblo.
- **habitual action**: something that happened regularly: "used to" in English: siempre iba en bici.
- **background description**: weather, time, age, appearance and feelings in the past: hacía frío, tenía doce años.
- **imperfect continuous**: estaba + gerund for "was …ing" at that moment: estaba durmiendo.
- **interrupted action**: an imperfect action cut into by a preterite event: estaba leyendo cuando llegó.
- **mientras**: while, usually followed by the imperfect: mientras esperaba, leí el periódico.
- **soler**: Higher verb meaning "usually"; in the imperfect, solía + infinitive means "used to".
:::

::: method
1. **Ask: is this an event or the scene?** An event that happens and is over takes the preterite. A habit, a description, or something in progress takes the imperfect.
2. **Look at the signal words.** Cuando era pequeño, antes, siempre, todos los días and mientras point to the imperfect. Un día, ayer, el año pasado and a las tres point to the preterite.
3. **For "was …ing", use the imperfect or estaba + gerund.** Put the interrupting event in the preterite.
4. **Form the imperfect**: -aba for -ar verbs, -ía for -er and -ir verbs, and only era, iba and veía are irregular.
5. **Check the accents**: -ábamos, every -ía form, éramos, íbamos.
6. **When translating into English**, choose "used to", "was …ing" or a simple past to give natural English.
:::

::: example Forming the imperfect
Complete with the imperfect: (a) Nosotros ___ (vivir) cerca del mar. (b) Mi abuela ___ (cocinar) muy bien. (c) Mis amigos y yo ___ (ir) al parque. (d) ¿Qué ___ (ver, tú) en la tele de pequeño? (4)

**Answer.** (a) **vivíamos** (1). (b) **cocinaba** (1). (c) **íbamos** (1): irregular, with the accent. (d) **veías** (1).
:::

::: example Habit or single event?
Translate into Spanish: (a) When I was little, I used to go to the beach every summer. (b) One summer I went to the mountains. (2)

**Answer.**
- (a) **Cuando era pequeño/a, iba a la playa todos los veranos.** (1) Both verbs are background and habit, so both are imperfect.
- (b) **Un verano fui a la montaña.** (1) This is one completed event, so it takes the preterite.
:::

::: example Setting the scene
Rewrite this description in the past: "Es sábado. Hace sol y hay mucha gente en el parque. Estoy muy contenta." (4)

**Answer.** **Era sábado** (1). **Hacía sol** (1) **y había mucha gente en el parque** (1). **Estaba muy contenta** (1). All four verbs are description, so all take the imperfect. Fue sábado or hizo sol would be wrong here.
:::

::: example The interrupted action
Translate into Spanish: "I was doing my homework when my friend phoned me." (2)

**Answer.** **Estaba haciendo los deberes** / **Hacía los deberes** (1) **cuando mi amigo me llamó** (1). The action in progress is imperfect; the interruption is preterite.
:::

::: example Translation into English: three meanings of the imperfect
Translate into English: "De niño, Iván jugaba al fútbol todos los días. Era muy rápido. Un día, mientras jugaba, se cayó." (3)

**Answer.** As a child, Iván used to play / played football every day (1). He was very fast (1). One day, while he was playing, he fell (over) (1). Jugaba is a habit the first time and an action in progress the second; se cayó is the event.
:::

::: watch
- **Description takes the imperfect**: hacía sol, había mucha gente, era divertido, estaba cansado. Hizo sol and fue divertido describe a whole, finished event, not the scene.
- **"Used to" is always imperfect**, but a habit can appear in English as a simple past ("I walked to school every day"). Go by the meaning.
- **Only ser, ir and ver are irregular**: era, iba, veía. Stem-changing verbs are regular: podía, quería, jugaba.
- **Accents**: hablábamos, every -ía form, éramos, íbamos.
- **Había never becomes plural**: había dos cines, not habían.
- **In an interrupted action**, the long action is imperfect and the interruption is preterite: estaba leyendo cuando llegó.
- **A fixed, finished period takes the preterite**: viví en Madrid dos años.
:::
