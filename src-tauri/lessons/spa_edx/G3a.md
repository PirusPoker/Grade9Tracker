## What it is

The present tense is the backbone of every paper. You use it to answer the two unprepared read-aloud questions in Paper 1, for the "present" bullet in both Paper 4 writing tasks, and in nearly every sentence of the reading and listening texts. Edexcel's grammar list says exactly what you must know:
- **regular -ar, -er and -ir verbs** in all six persons;
- verbs that follow one of **five anchor patterns**: **encontrar** (o → ue), **pensar** (e → ie), **pedir** (e → i), **conocer** (c → zc in the yo form) and **poner** (add -g in the yo form);
- the irregular present of **ser, estar, ir, hacer** and **tener**, including **tener + noun** where English says "be + adjective" (tengo frío, Alejandro tiene hambre);
- the **present continuous**: estar + present participle (estoy comiendo), including **leyendo** and **pidiendo**;
- **Higher only:** the spelling change **g → j** in the yo form (coger → cojo), and the present with **desde hace** for "have been …ing for" (taught in G2c).

One Spanish present covers two English ones: **como** = I eat **and** I am eating. The present continuous exists too, but Spanish uses it only to stress that something is happening right now.

Other irregular present forms are tested only if the vocabulary list prints them. The ones it prints are **oigo, oyes, oye** (oír), **sé** (saber), **doy** (dar), **digo** (decir), **veo** (ver), **juego, juegas, juega, juegan** (jugar) and **voy** (ir).

### Regular verbs

Take off -ar, -er or -ir and add the endings. The -er and -ir endings differ only in nosotros and vosotros.

| | hablar (to speak) | comer (to eat) | vivir (to live) |
|---|---|---|---|
| yo | hablo | como | vivo |
| tú | hablas | comes | vives |
| él / ella / usted | habla | come | vive |
| nosotros/as | hablamos | comemos | vivimos |
| vosotros/as | habláis | coméis | vivís |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | hablan | comen | viven |

Subject pronouns are normally left out, because the ending already shows the person: **Vivo en Madrid**, not Yo vivo en Madrid, unless you are contrasting people (**Yo estudio, pero él no hace nada**). **Usted** and **ustedes** take the third-person endings: **¿Usted habla inglés?**

### The five anchor patterns

Edexcel groups most irregular verbs under five models. Learn the model and you can conjugate every verb that follows it.

**Stem-changing ("boot") verbs.** The vowel in the stem changes in four persons, yo, tú, él and ellos, but **never in nosotros and vosotros**. Draw the table, and the changed forms make the shape of a boot.

| | encontrar (o → ue) | pensar (e → ie) | pedir (e → i) |
|---|---|---|---|
| yo | encuentro | pienso | pido |
| tú | encuentras | piensas | pides |
| él / ella / usted | encuentra | piensa | pide |
| nosotros/as | encontramos | pensamos | pedimos |
| vosotros/as | encontráis | pensáis | pedís |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | encuentran | piensan | piden |

Verbs on the vocabulary list that follow each one:
- **like encontrar (o → ue):** poder (puedo), volver (vuelvo), devolver, dormir (duermo), costar (cuesta), recordar (recuerdo), contar, mostrar, mover, doler (me duele), llover (llueve), and at Higher soler (suelo), acostarse (me acuesto) and probar (pruebo). **Jugar** changes u → ue in the same four persons: juego, juegas, juega, jugamos, jugáis, juegan.
- **like pensar (e → ie):** querer (quiero), preferir (prefiero), cerrar, comenzar, perder, recomendar, despertarse (me despierto), divertirse (me divierto), nevar (nieva), and at Higher empezar (empiezo), entender (entiendo) and sentirse (me siento).
- **like pedir (e → i):** repetir (repito), servir (sirvo), vestirse (me visto), seguir (sigo), and at Higher conseguir (consigo) and elegir (elijo).

**Verbs that change only in the yo form.**

| | conocer (c → zc) | poner (add -g) |
|---|---|---|
| yo | **conozco** | **pongo** |
| tú | conoces | pones |
| él / ella / usted | conoce | pone |
| nosotros/as | conocemos | ponemos |
| vosotros/as | conocéis | ponéis |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | conocen | ponen |

- **Like conocer:** parecer (parezco), reconocer, merecer, and at Higher ofrecer (ofrezco), agradecer, crecer, pertenecer, traducir (traduzco) and producir (produzco).
- **Like poner:** salir (salgo), and at Higher proponer (propongo).

Conocer means to know a person or a place, or to meet someone: **Conozco a Sara** (personal a), **No conozco Madrid**. Saber means to know a fact, or to know how to: **No sé la respuesta. ¿Sabes cocinar?**

### The five irregular verbs

| | ser | estar | ir | hacer | tener |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| yo | soy | estoy | voy | hago | tengo |
| tú | eres | estás | vas | haces | tienes |
| él / ella / usted | es | está | va | hace | tiene |
| nosotros/as | somos | estamos | vamos | hacemos | tenemos |
| vosotros/as | sois | estáis | vais | hacéis | tenéis |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | son | están | van | hacen | tienen |

Watch the accents on **estás, está, están**: **esta** with no accent means "this".

**Ser or estar?**
- **Ser** is for what something is: identity, nationality, job, character, what something is like, origin, the time. **Mi padre es médico. Somos de Madrid. Es la una. El libro es interesante.**
- **Estar** is for where something is, and for states and feelings at the moment. It is also the verb used in the present continuous. **Madrid está en el centro de España. Estoy cansado. ¿Cómo estás?**
- Some adjectives change meaning: **es aburrido** (he is boring) / **está aburrido** (he is bored); **es lista** (she is clever) / **está lista** (she is ready).

**Tener + noun.** English says "I am cold"; Spanish says "I have cold":

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| tengo frío / calor | I am cold / hot |
| tienes hambre / sed | you are hungry / thirsty |
| tiene miedo | he is scared |
| tenemos sueño | we are tired, sleepy |
| tenéis razón | you are right |
| tienen suerte | they are lucky |
| tengo quince años | I am fifteen |

Because these are nouns, "very" is **mucho / mucha**: **tengo mucho frío**, **tengo mucha hambre** (hambre is feminine).

### Higher: the spelling change g → j

In verbs ending in -ger or -gir, the g would sound hard before o, so the yo form writes **j** to keep the soft sound. Only the yo form changes:
- **coger → cojo**, coges, coge… (to take, catch: **Cojo el autobús a las ocho.**)
- **escoger → escojo**; **recoger → recojo**; **proteger → protejo**;
- **elegir → elijo**, which is also a pedir-type verb: eliges, elige, elegimos, elegís, eligen.

### The present continuous

**Estar + present participle (gerund)** = "be …ing", for something in progress at this moment:
- **-ar** → stem + **-ando**: hablar → **hablando**, jugar → **jugando**;
- **-er / -ir** → stem + **-iendo**: comer → **comiendo**, vivir → **viviendo**, escribir → **escribiendo**;
- a stem ending in a **vowel** takes **-yendo**: leer → **leyendo**, creer → **creyendo**, oír → **oyendo**, construir → **construyendo**;
- **pedir-type** verbs change e → i: pedir → **pidiendo**, repetir → **repitiendo**, servir → **sirviendo**, seguir → **siguiendo**, vestirse → **vistiéndose**. You will also meet **durmiendo** (dormir) and **diciendo** (decir).

| estar | example |
|---|---|
| estoy | Estoy haciendo los deberes. (I am doing my homework.) |
| estás | ¿Qué estás leyendo? (What are you reading?) |
| está | Mi madre está trabajando. (My mum is working.) |
| estamos | Estamos esperando el autobús. (We are waiting for the bus.) |
| estáis | ¿Estáis viendo la tele? (Are you watching TV?) |
| están | Los niños están jugando en el parque. (The children are playing in the park.) |

The participle never changes for gender or number. With a reflexive verb, the pronoun goes before estar or is joined to the end of the participle, which then needs an accent: **me estoy vistiendo** = **estoy vistiéndome**.

**When not to use it.** English uses "-ing" for plans ("I'm going to Spain next week"), but Spanish does not. Use the present or ir a: **Voy a España la semana que viene.** Habits also take the simple present: **Los sábados trabajo en una tienda**, not estoy trabajando.

::: terms
- **present tense**: one form covering "I play" and "I am playing": juego.
- **anchor pattern**: one of Edexcel's five model verbs (encontrar, pensar, pedir, conocer, poner) that other verbs copy.
- **stem-changing verb**: a verb whose stem vowel changes (o → ue, e → ie, e → i) in four persons, never in nosotros and vosotros.
- **boot verb**: another name for a stem-changing verb; the changed forms make a boot shape in the table.
- **yo-form change**: an irregular first person only, as in conozco, pongo, salgo, hago, tengo.
- **ser**: to be, for identity, character, job, origin and the time: es simpática.
- **estar**: to be, for location, temporary states and the present continuous: está cansada.
- **tener + noun**: Spanish for "be + adjective": tengo frío, tiene hambre, tenemos suerte.
- **g → j change**: Higher spelling change keeping the soft sound in the yo form: cojo, escojo, elijo.
- **present continuous**: estar + gerund for an action in progress right now: estoy leyendo.
- **gerund (present participle)**: the -ando / -iendo form; leyendo and pidiendo are the listed spelling changes.
:::

::: method
1. **Find the infinitive and its group.** Is it regular, one of the five irregulars (ser, estar, ir, hacer, tener), or does it follow an anchor pattern?
2. **Work out the person** from the subject: one person (él, ella, Sara), several (ellos, mis amigos), or yourself plus others (nosotros).
3. **Apply the stem change only where it belongs**: yo, tú, él and ellos change; nosotros and vosotros keep the infinitive's vowel (podemos, pensáis).
4. **Check the yo form separately**: conozco, pongo, salgo, hago, tengo, sé, doy, veo, digo, oigo, and at Higher cojo and elijo.
5. **Choose ser or estar**: what something is (ser) or where and how it is right now (estar).
6. **Use the continuous only for "right now"**: estar + -ando / -iendo. Use the simple present for habits and the present or ir a for plans.
7. **Re-read for accents**: estás, está, están; habláis, coméis, vivís; and an accent when a pronoun is joined to a gerund.
:::

::: example Anchor patterns in action
Complete with the present tense: (a) Nosotros ___ (poder) salir esta noche. (b) Mi hermana ___ (preferir) el té. (c) Yo ___ (conocer) a tu primo. (d) ¿Qué ___ (pedir) tus padres en el restaurante? (4)

**Answer.**
- (a) **podemos** (1): nosotros never changes the stem, so not puedemos.
- (b) **prefiere** (1): e → ie, like pensar.
- (c) **conozco** (1): c → zc in the yo form.
- (d) **piden** (1): e → i in the ellos form.
:::

::: example Ser, estar or tener?
Translate into Spanish: (a) My aunt is a nurse. (b) The hospital is near the station. (c) We are very hungry. (3)

**Answer.**
- (a) **Mi tía es enfermera.** (1) Ser for a job, with no article.
- (b) **El hospital está cerca de la estación.** (1) Estar for location.
- (c) **Tenemos mucha hambre.** (1) Tener + noun; mucha agrees with hambre.
:::

::: example Present continuous with the spelling changes
Write in the present continuous: (a) Paula lee una revista. (b) Los camareros sirven la comida. (c) Me visto. (3)

**Answer.**
- (a) **Paula está leyendo una revista.** (1) The vowel stem takes -yendo.
- (b) **Los camareros están sirviendo la comida.** (1) A pedir-type verb: e → i.
- (c) **Me estoy vistiendo / Estoy vistiéndome.** (1) The accent is needed when the pronoun is joined on.
:::

::: example Higher: the yo form of -ger verbs
Translate into Spanish: "I take the bus every day and I always choose the same seat." (2)

**Answer.** **Cojo el autobús todos los días** (1) **y siempre escojo el mismo sitio** (1). The g → j change keeps the soft sound of the g; cogo and escogo are wrong.
:::

::: example Translation into English: one present, two meanings
Translate into English: "Mis padres trabajan en Madrid, pero esta semana están trabajando en casa." (2)

**Answer.** My parents work in Madrid (1), but this week they are working at home (1). The simple present is a habit, so "work"; the continuous is "right now", so "are working".
:::

::: watch
- **Stem changes never reach nosotros and vosotros**: podemos, pensáis, pedimos. Puedemos and piensamos are major errors (wrong verb formation).
- **Learn the yo forms separately**: conozco, pongo, salgo, hago, tengo, sé, doy, veo, digo, oigo, juego, and at Higher cojo and elijo. Conoco and pono score nothing.
- **Do not add yo to every sentence.** The ending shows the person. Use the pronoun only for contrast or emphasis.
- **Ser for what something is, estar for where and how it is now.** Es aburrido and está aburrido mean different things.
- **"I am cold / hungry / scared" uses tener**, not ser or estar: tengo frío, tengo hambre, tengo miedo.
- **Do not overuse the continuous.** Habits take the simple present (los lunes juego al tenis), and plans take the present or ir a.
- **Accents**: estás, está, están; the vosotros endings -áis, -éis, -ís; and estoy vistiéndome once a pronoun is joined on.
:::
