## What it is

**Paper 3 Reading (1SP1/3H)** lasts **60 minutes**. It is worth **50 marks, 25%** of the GCSE. There is no dictionary.

| section | what you do | marks | suggested time |
|---|---|---|---|
| A Reading comprehension | eight questions on Spanish texts, set and answered in English | **40** | about 45 min |
| B Translation | translate one Spanish paragraph into English | **10** | about 10 min |

Leave about 5 minutes to check.

### Section A: how it is built

The texts get longer and harder through the paper: blog posts, forum entries, reviews, adverts, brochures, emails, articles and reports, across all six thematic contexts. In Pearson's sample Higher paper the eight questions are worth **3, 5, 7, 3, 7, 5, 5 and 5** marks. Most questions mix two or three of these types:

| type | instruction | how to get it right |
|---|---|---|
| **Gap-fill from a box** | "Complete the gap in each sentence using a word from the box. There are more words than gaps." | Work out what kind of word fits (a person, a place, an activity), find the line in the text, then match the **meaning**, not a look-alike word. |
| **Sentence completion** | "Put a cross in the correct box" (A, B or C) | Usually all three options appear in the text. Check the person, the time frame and any negative. |
| **Multiple response** | "Put a cross next to each one of the three (or two) correct statements." | Prove each statement true or false against a specific phrase. Exactly the number asked for. |
| **Short answers / tables** | "Answer the following questions in English. You do not need to write in full sentences." | Give the precise detail; one detail is enough when the table says "1 detail". |
| **Inference** | "Which of these is the best translation of the word …?" | The word is deliberately **not** on the vocabulary list. Use the context. 1 mark each, two per paper. |

### Reading the questions first

1. **Read the English questions before the text.** They follow the order of the text and tell you what to look for.
2. **Underline the question's key words**: who, when, why, how often, the problem, the advantage.
3. **Find the matching part of the text**, then read the sentence before and after. The answer is often just after **pero, sin embargo, aunque** or **lo malo es que**.
4. **Check the time frame and the person.** "What does she do now?" is not answered by **antes…** or by **mi hermano…**
5. **Check negatives**: **no, nunca, ya no, todavía no, tampoco, ni… ni…, nadie, nada**.

### Answering in English

- **Short is fine; vague is not.** "Because the bus is expensive" scores. "Bus" is an **isolated word**: it names the topic but doesn't answer "why?", so it scores 0.
- **Don't copy Spanish words** unless they are names: an answer in Spanish shows nothing.
- **Don't add a second, contradictory detail.** A wrong extra detail can cost the mark.
- **No marks for English spelling or grammar**, as long as the meaning is clear.

### The two inference words

Pearson may **gloss** up to 2% of a text (give the meaning in English), and up to 2% may be **cognates** that are not on the list. The two inference words are different: they are off-list, unglossed and tested directly. To crack one:
1. **Use the context.** What is the sentence about? Does it sound positive or negative? Is the word a person, a thing, an action or a description?
2. **Look for a word family.** You may know a related word, or a suffix from G7 (-ito, -ísimo, -mente, -idad, -able).
3. **Test each option** by putting it into the sentence. Only one makes sense with the rest of the text.

**Beware false friends.** These look like English words but mean something else:
- **actual** = current; **actualmente** = currently;
- **éxito** = success;
- **largo** = long;
- **asistir a** = to attend;
- **recordar** = to remember or remind;
- **en realidad** = actually.

### Section B: translation into English

You translate **one paragraph** of about five sentences (55–65 words). It moves through several tenses and usually contains a Higher structure. It is marked **as a whole** for how fully and naturally the meaning is transferred:
- **9–10:** the meaning is fully and appropriately transferred; any errors don't affect the clarity of the message.
- **6–8:** the meaning is mostly transferred; occasional errors (a wrong or missing word, awkward word order) affect clarity.
- **3–5:** parts are transferred; some errors (missed phrases, wrong tenses) stop the message coming across.
- **1–2:** only intermittent parts are transferred.

**Natural English, every detail.**
- **Keep every small word**: **ya** (already), **todavía** (still / yet), **también**, **nunca**, **cada** (each / every), **casi** (almost), **solo** (only), **muy, bastante, demasiado**.
- **Get every tense right**: **comí** (I ate), **comía** (I used to eat / was eating), **he comido** (I have eaten), **comeré** (I will eat), **comería** (I would eat), **voy a comer** (I'm going to eat).
- **Translate the structures, not the words:**

| Spanish | not | but |
|---|---|---|
| Vivo aquí desde hace dos años. | I live here since two years. | I have been living here for two years. |
| Llevo tres meses trabajando. | I carry three months working. | I have been working for three months. |
| Acabo de llegar. | I finish of arriving. | I have just arrived. |
| A mi hermano le gusta leer. | To my brother it pleases to read. | My brother likes reading. |
| Lo que más me gusta es… | The that most pleases me is… | What I like most is… |
| Suelo ir… | I am used to go… | I usually go… |
| Se puede… / Hay que… | One can… / There is to… | You can… / You have to… |
| Lo pasamos bien. | We passed it well. | We had a good time. |

- **Possessives and reflexives**: **me lavo el pelo** is "I wash my hair"; **su** can be his, her, their or your, so let the context decide.
- **Read your English aloud in your head.** If it sounds like a word-for-word copy, fix it.

::: terms
- **Section A**: eight comprehension questions in English (40 marks), on texts that get longer through the paper.
- **gap-fill from a box**: English sentences to complete with box words; there are more words than gaps.
- **multiple response**: choose exactly the two or three correct statements out of a list.
- **inference word**: an off-list, unglossed word whose meaning you deduce from context; two per paper, 1 mark each.
- **gloss**: an English meaning printed under a text for a word not on the list (up to 2% of a text).
- **cognate**: a word that looks and means the same in both languages: hospital, música, importante.
- **false friend**: a word that looks English but means something else: actual (current), éxito (success).
- **isolated word**: a one-word answer that names the topic but not the detail asked for; it scores 0.
- **Section B**: one paragraph translated into English, 10 marks, marked as a whole in four bands.
- **natural English**: translating structures by meaning: desde hace dos años = for two years.
:::

::: method
1. **Read the question and underline who, when and what** before reading the text.
2. **Find the matching sentence**, and read the sentences around it for pero, sin embargo, negatives and time markers.
3. **Check the person and the time frame** of your answer against the question.
4. **Write a precise detail in English**, never an isolated word, and never two contradictory answers.
5. **For an inference word**, decide its type and tone from the context, then test each option in the sentence.
6. **For the translation**, go sentence by sentence: tense, person, every small word, then rewrite as natural English.
7. **Check**: blanks filled, exact number of crosses, and the translation re-read for missing words.
:::

::: example Finding the detail
Read Diego's post. Why did Diego stop playing tennis?

> Antes jugaba al tenis todos los fines de semana, pero lo dejé el año pasado porque el club estaba demasiado lejos de mi casa. Ahora juego al baloncesto en el instituto. (1)

**Answer.** **The club was too far from his house** (1). "Far" alone is an isolated word and scores 0. "He plays basketball now" answers a different question.
:::

::: example An inference word
Read the sentence. Which is the best translation of the word **chiste**?

> Mi tío siempre cuenta el mismo chiste en las fiestas, pero todos nos reímos porque es muy divertido.

A — joke; B — song; C — dance. (1)

**Answer.** **A** (1). You tell it (cuenta), everyone laughs (nos reímos), and it is funny (divertido). A song or a dance is not told.
:::

::: example Statements: proving true or false
Read the text. Which **two** statements are correct?

> Mi hermana Pilar estudia en la universidad de Madrid. No le gusta vivir en la ciudad porque hay mucho ruido, pero tiene muchos amigos allí. Vuelve a casa una vez al mes.

A — Pilar likes city life. B — Pilar has lots of friends in Madrid. C — She comes home every week. D — She studies at university. (2)

**Answer.** **B** (1) and **D** (1). A is negated (no le gusta). C is wrong: she comes home once a month.
:::

::: example Translating a tricky sentence
Translate into English: "Desde hace tres años, mi hermana trabaja en un hospital y le encanta lo que hace." (3)

**Answer.** **My sister has been working in a hospital for three years** (1: desde hace + present = has been …ing for), **and she loves** (1: le encanta, not "to her it enchants") **what she does** (1: lo que = what).
:::

::: example Choosing the box word
Read the sentence: "Lo malo de mi barrio es que no hay nada para los jóvenes." Complete: "The problem with the area is that there is nothing for ......" Box: children · teenagers · old people · tourists. (1)

**Answer.** **teenagers** (1). Los jóvenes means young people; the box offers a synonym, not "young".
:::

::: q
How many inference words are there in the paper, and how many marks is each worth? (2)
---
Two (1); 1 mark each (1).
:::

::: q
Give the meaning of these false friends: (a) actual (b) éxito (c) largo (d) asistir a. (4)
---
(a) current / present (1) (b) success (1) (c) long (1) (d) to attend (1)
:::

::: q
Translate into English: (a) Llevo dos años estudiando español. (b) Acabamos de llegar. (2)
---
(a) I have been studying Spanish for two years (1). (b) We have just arrived (1).
:::

::: q
Translate into English: "Lo que más me gusta de mi pueblo es la tranquilidad." (2)
---
What I like most about my village / town (1) is the peace and quiet / tranquillity (1).
:::

::: q
Why would "money" score 0 as an answer to "Why doesn't Sara go to the cinema any more?" if the text says "ya no voy al cine porque las entradas son carísimas"? (1)
---
It is an isolated word; the detail is that the tickets are very expensive (1).
:::

::: q
How long should you spend on the Section B translation, and how is it marked? (2)
---
About 10 minutes (1); as a whole, in bands from 9–10 down to 1–2, for how fully and naturally the meaning is transferred (1).
:::

::: watch
- **Isolated words score 0**: answer the exact question with the detail, not just the topic.
- **The word you spot is often the trap**: check for negatives, a different person, or a different time frame.
- **Choose exactly the number of statements asked for.**
- **The box word is a synonym**: los jóvenes may be "teenagers", es caro may be "expensive".
- **Test every option for the inference word** in the sentence, not just the one that looks like English.
- **False friends**: actual, éxito, largo, asistir.
- **In the translation, don't drop the small words**: ya, todavía, también, nunca, cada, casi, solo.
- **Translate structures by meaning**: desde hace, llevar + gerund, acabar de, le gusta, lo que.
- **Keep every tense**: one wrong tense in each sentence pulls a translation down a band.
:::

::: test
Read Alejandro's forum post. Write the letters of the three correct statements.

::: test
Read the article. Complete the table in English. You do not need to write in full sentences.

> Hace cinco años mi pueblo estaba muy sucio y había basura en todas las calles. Ahora es muy diferente: hay sitios para reciclar en cada esquina y los vecinos limpian el río una vez al mes. Sin embargo, todavía hay un problema: mucha gente va al trabajo en coche y el aire no está limpio.

::: test
Read the hotel review. (a) Write the letters of the two correct statements. (b) Answer the question in English.

> Pasamos tres noches en un hotel en Madrid. La habitación era pequeña pero muy limpia, y desde la ventana se veía la Plaza Mayor. El desayuno era buenísimo, aunque bastante caro. Lo peor fue el ruido de la calle por la noche: no pudimos dormir bien.

(a) A — They stayed for three nights. B — The room was big. C — The room was clean. D — Breakfast was cheap. (b) What was the worst thing about the stay? (3)

::: test
Translate this passage into English.

> Desde hace dos años vivo con mi madre y mi hermano pequeño. Normalmente nos llevamos bien, aunque a veces discutimos por las tareas de casa. El fin de semana pasado fuimos al campo y comimos al aire libre. Lo que más me gusta de mi familia es que siempre nos apoyamos. Cuando tenga dieciocho años, me gustaría compartir un piso con mis amigos. (10)
---
Marked as a whole. **9–10**: the full meaning is transferred in natural English. **6–8**: most of the meaning comes across; occasional errors blur a detail. **3–5**: parts are transferred, but some errors (wrong tenses, missed phrases) lose the message. **1–2**: only isolated parts are clear.

**Model.** I have been living with my mum and my little brother for two years. We normally get on well, although sometimes we argue about the housework. Last weekend we went to the countryside and ate outdoors. What I like most about my family is that we always support each other. When I am eighteen, I would like to share a flat with my friends.

**Key points:** desde hace (have been living … for); nos llevamos bien (get on well); discutimos por (argue about); al aire libre (outdoors); lo que más me gusta (what I like most); nos apoyamos (support each other); cuando tenga (when I am, not "when I have").
:::

Marked as a whole. **9–10**: the full meaning is transferred in natural English. **6–8**: most of the meaning comes across; occasional errors blur a detail. **3–5**: parts are transferred, but some errors lose the message. **1–2**: only isolated parts are clear.

:::
