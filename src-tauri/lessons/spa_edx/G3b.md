## What it is

Spanish has two everyday past tenses for things that happened, and this lesson covers both:
- the **preterite** (compré, fui, hice) for a **completed event at a specific time** in the past: **Ayer compré un vestido nuevo.**
- the **perfect** (he comido, has visto) = haber + past participle, the equivalent of the English "have done": **He comido.** (I have eaten.)

Edexcel's grammar list requires:
- the preterite of **regular -ar, -er and -ir verbs** in all six persons;
- the irregular preterite of **ir, ser, dar, tener, poder, hacer, venir, estar, poner, querer, decir and traer**, and of verbs built on them with a prefix (mantener → mantuve, proponer → propuse);
- **Higher only:** the yo spelling changes **c → qu** (signifiqué), **z → c** (utilicé) and **g → gu** (jugué); the third-person **y** (leyó, creyeron); and the third-person stem changes of -ir verbs, **o → u** (durmió, durmieron) and **e → i** (prefirió, prefirieron);
- the perfect with **regular participles** (-ado, -ido) and **irregular participles** such as visto and hecho.

Where it turns up:
- **Paper 1.** The second compulsory picture-task question is always in the **past**, and the conversation needs a past time frame for AO3.
- **Paper 4.** Both writing tasks have a past bullet. The translation usually includes a preterite and a perfect, and a wrong tense for the time frame (el sábado pasado iré) is a major error.
- **Papers 2 and 3.** Questions turn on what someone **did**, **has done** or **has not done yet**.

### The regular preterite

| | comprar | comer | vivir |
|---|---|---|---|
| yo | compré | comí | viví |
| tú | compraste | comiste | viviste |
| él / ella / usted | compró | comió | vivió |
| nosotros/as | compramos | comimos | vivimos |
| vosotros/as | comprasteis | comisteis | vivisteis |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | compraron | comieron | vivieron |

- **The accents matter.** **Hablo** = I speak; **habló** = she spoke. **Compre** without the accent is not the preterite at all. In the Higher dictation, a missing stress accent loses the word.
- **-er and -ir verbs share one set of endings.**
- **Nosotros -ar and -ir forms look like the present** (hablamos, vivimos). The time phrase tells you which: **ayer hablamos** (we spoke), **normalmente hablamos** (we speak).
- **Stem-changing -ar and -er verbs do not change in the preterite**: encontré, pensó, volvieron, perdimos.

**Time phrases for the preterite:** ayer, el sábado, el fin de semana pasado, la semana pasada, el año pasado, el verano pasado, en 2024, **hace dos años** (two years ago), un día, de repente, luego, después, por fin.

### The irregular preterite

These have special stems and **no accents** at all.

| | ir / ser | dar | hacer | tener | estar |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| yo | fui | di | hice | tuve | estuve |
| tú | fuiste | diste | hiciste | tuviste | estuviste |
| él / ella / usted | fue | dio | **hizo** | tuvo | estuvo |
| nosotros/as | fuimos | dimos | hicimos | tuvimos | estuvimos |
| vosotros/as | fuisteis | disteis | hicisteis | tuvisteis | estuvisteis |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | fueron | dieron | hicieron | tuvieron | estuvieron |

The rest follow the same pattern. Take the stem, then add **-e, -iste, -o, -imos, -isteis, -ieron**:

| infinitive | stem | yo, él, ellos |
|---|---|---|
| poder | pud- | pude, pudo, pudieron |
| poner | pus- | puse, puso, pusieron |
| querer | quis- | quise, quiso, quisieron |
| venir | vin- | vine, vino, vinieron |
| decir | dij- | dije, dijo, **dijeron** |
| traer | traj- | traje, trajo, **trajeron** |

- **Ir and ser share the same forms.** Context decides: **Fue al cine** (he went to the cinema); **Fue divertido** (it was fun).
- **Hizo** is spelt with z to keep the "th" sound before o.
- **Decir and traer** drop the i in the ellos form: dijeron, trajeron.
- **Prefixed verbs copy their base verb**: mantener → mantuve, proponer → propuse, atraer → atrajo.

### Higher: spelling changes in the preterite

**Yo-form changes.** These keep the sound of the consonant before the ending -é. They affect the yo form only: busqué, but buscó, buscaste.

| ending | change | examples |
|---|---|---|
| -car | c → qu | buscar → **busqué**, tocar → **toqué**, practicar → **practiqué**, significar → **signifiqué**, explicar → **expliqué** |
| -zar | z → c | utilizar → **utilicé**, empezar → **empecé**, organizar → **organicé**, cruzar → **crucé** |
| -gar | g → gu | jugar → **jugué**, llegar → **llegué**, pagar → **pagué**, apagar → **apagué** |

**Third-person y.** When the stem ends in a vowel, the i of -ió and -ieron becomes y:
- leer: leí, leíste, **leyó**, leímos, leísteis, **leyeron**;
- creer → **creyó, creyeron**; oír → **oyó, oyeron**; construir → **construyó, construyeron**; caer → **se cayó** (she fell).

**-ir stem changes in the third person.** -ir verbs that change their stem in the present also change in the él and ellos forms of the preterite:
- **o → u:** dormir → **durmió, durmieron** (but dormí, dormiste, dormimos);
- **e → i:** preferir → **prefirió, prefirieron**; pedir → **pidió, pidieron**; repetir → **repitió**; servir → **sirvió**; seguir → **siguió**; divertirse → **se divirtió, se divirtieron**; vestirse → **se vistió**; sentirse → **se sintió**; elegir → **eligió**.

### The perfect tense

**Haber + past participle** = "have done":

| haber | + participle |
|---|---|
| he | He terminado los deberes. (I have finished my homework.) |
| has | ¿Has visto mis llaves? (Have you seen my keys?) |
| ha | Mi hermano ha perdido el móvil. (My brother has lost his phone.) |
| hemos | Hemos comprado los billetes. (We have bought the tickets.) |
| habéis | ¿Habéis comido? (Have you eaten?) |
| han | Mis primos han llegado. (My cousins have arrived.) |

**Regular participles:** -ar → **-ado** (trabajado), -er and -ir → **-ido** (perdido, preferido, vivido). A vowel before -ido takes an accent: **leído, creído, oído, traído**.

**Irregular participles** on the vocabulary list: **visto** (ver), **hecho** (hacer), **escrito** (escribir), **abierto** (abrir), **puesto** (poner), **vuelto** (volver), **dicho** (decir), **roto** (romper), **descubierto** (descubrir, Higher). Prefixed verbs copy them: devolver → **devuelto**.

Three rules:
- **The participle never changes** in the perfect: **ella ha llegado**, **las chicas han comido**. Only the passive with ser makes it agree (see G2c).
- **Nothing goes between haber and the participle.** Pronouns go before haber: **lo he visto**, **me he levantado**. Negatives go before too: **no he terminado**, **nunca he estado en España**.
- **Haber is not tener.** "I have finished" is **he terminado**, never tengo terminado.

### Preterite or perfect?

| preterite: a finished time | perfect: a time still going on, or no time given |
|---|---|
| ayer, el lunes, la semana pasada, el año pasado, hace un mes, en 2023 | hoy, esta mañana, esta semana, este año, ya, todavía no, nunca, alguna vez, recientemente |
| **Ayer comí paella.** (Yesterday I ate paella.) | **Hoy he comido paella.** (Today I have eaten paella.) |
| **El verano pasado fui a Madrid.** | **Este verano he ido a Madrid dos veces.** |

- English and Spanish usually agree: if English says "have done", Spanish uses the perfect. One difference: English often says "I ate" with "today" (I ate well today), where Spain uses **hoy he comido bien**.
- In much of Latin America, speakers use the preterite where Spain uses the perfect. You will understand both. In your own writing, follow the table.
- **The perfect is never used for "have been …ing for"**. That needs the present with desde hace (G2c).

::: terms
- **preterite**: the past tense for a completed action at a specific time: ayer visité el museo.
- **regular preterite endings**: -é, -aste, -ó, -amos, -asteis, -aron; -í, -iste, -ió, -imos, -isteis, -ieron.
- **irregular stem**: a special preterite stem with no accents: hic-, tuv-, estuv-, pud-, pus-, quis-, vin-, dij-, traj-.
- **ir / ser preterite**: fui, fuiste, fue, fuimos, fuisteis, fueron, shared by both verbs.
- **yo spelling change**: Higher c → qu, z → c, g → gu before -é: busqué, utilicé, jugué.
- **third-person y**: Higher change after a vowel stem: leyó, creyeron, construyó.
- **-ir stem change**: Higher o → u or e → i in the él and ellos forms only: durmió, prefirieron.
- **perfect tense**: haber (he, has, ha, hemos, habéis, han) + past participle: he visto.
- **past participle**: the -ado / -ido form; irregulars include visto, hecho, escrito, puesto, vuelto, dicho.
- **hace + time**: "ago" with the preterite: fui a Barcelona hace tres años.
:::

::: method
1. **Read the time phrase.** A finished time (ayer, el año pasado, hace…) means the preterite. Hoy, esta semana, ya, nunca or no time at all means the perfect.
2. **For the preterite, check whether the verb is irregular.** The twelve listed verbs and their prefixed forms use special stems and take no accents.
3. **For a regular preterite, put the accent on yo and él**: -é / -í and -ó / -ió.
4. **At Higher, check the yo form of -car, -gar and -zar verbs** (qu, gu, c), **the él and ellos forms of vowel-stem verbs** (y) **and of -ir stem-changers** (u or i).
5. **For the perfect, conjugate haber, then add the participle.** Make sure it is not one of the irregulars (visto, hecho, escrito, puesto, vuelto, dicho, abierto, roto).
6. **When translating into English**, give a natural tense: fuimos = we went; hemos ido = we have been / we went (today).
:::

::: example Regular preterite and accents
Translate into Spanish: (a) I bought a T-shirt. (b) She bought a T-shirt. (c) They ate in a restaurant. (3)

**Answer.**
- (a) **Compré una camiseta.** (1) The accent is needed: compre is a different form.
- (b) **Compró una camiseta.** (1) Without the accent, compro means I buy.
- (c) **Comieron en un restaurante.** (1)
:::

**Answer.** (a) **hice** (1). (b) **vinieron** (1). (c) **dijo** (1). (d) **tuvimos** (1). None of them takes an accent.

::: example The perfect with an irregular participle
Translate into Spanish: (a) We have seen the film. (b) Have you (tú) done your homework? (c) My sister has written a letter. (3)

**Answer.** (a) **Hemos visto la película.** (1) (b) **¿Has hecho los deberes?** (1) (c) **Mi hermana ha escrito una carta.** (1) Reject veído, hacido, escribido.

::: example Choosing the tense
Translate into Spanish: "This morning I have cleaned my room, but yesterday I did nothing." (3)

**Answer.** **Esta mañana he limpiado mi habitación** (1): the perfect, because the time is not over. **pero ayer** (1) **no hice nada** (1): the preterite after ayer, with the negative no … nada.
:::

::: watch
- **Regular preterite accents carry meaning**: compré, compró, comí, comió. Hablo (I speak) is not habló (he spoke).
- **Irregular preterites have no accents**: fui, fue, di, dio, hice, hizo, tuve, tuvo, vine, vino. Writing fué or hizó is wrong.
- **Hizo has a z.** Dijeron and trajeron have no i.
- **At Higher, the spelling changes are in the yo form only** (busqué, but buscó), and the -ir stem changes are in the él and ellos forms only (durmió, but dormí).
- **The perfect uses haber, never tener**, and the participle never agrees: ella ha llegado.
- **Irregular participles**: visto, hecho, escrito, puesto, vuelto, dicho, abierto, roto. Hacido and escribido are major errors.
- **Match the tense to the time phrase**: ayer and hace + time take the preterite; hoy, esta semana, ya and nunca take the perfect.
:::
