## What it is

A **linear equation** has the unknown to the power 1 only — no squares, no $x$ in a denominator — so it has exactly one solution. $3x + 7 = 22$, $5(2x - 1) = 3x + 9$, $\dfrac{x + 4}{3} = 2x - 6$.

Solving means undoing what has been done to $x$ until $x$ stands alone, doing the same to both sides at each step so the balance holds. The skill is not the algebra — it is reading a **worded** problem and turning it into an equation in the first place: perimeters, ages, angles in a shape, "I think of a number".

::: method
1. **Clear fractions** first: multiply every term by the denominator (or the LCM of the denominators).
2. **Expand brackets.**
3. **Collect** $x$ terms on one side, numbers on the other — move the smaller $x$ term so the coefficient stays positive.
4. **Divide** by the coefficient of $x$. Leave a fraction as a fraction unless told to give a decimal.
5. **Check** by substituting back into the *original* equation.
6. **From words:** let $x$ be the thing you are asked for (or the smallest quantity). Write every other quantity in terms of $x$. Write the sentence that links them as an equation (perimeter $=$, sum of angles $= 180$, total cost $=$). Solve, then answer the actual question — sometimes it is not $x$ itself.
:::

::: example Brackets on both sides
Solve $5(2x - 1) = 3(x + 4) + 4$.

$10x - 5 = 3x + 12 + 4 = 3x + 16$.

$7x = 21 \Rightarrow x = 3$. Check: $5 \times 5 = 25$; $3 \times 7 + 4 = 25$ ✓.
:::

::: example Fractions
Solve $\dfrac{x + 4}{3} = 2x - 6$.

Multiply by 3: $x + 4 = 6x - 18 \Rightarrow 22 = 5x \Rightarrow x = \dfrac{22}{5} = 4.4$.
:::

::: example Two fractions
Solve $\dfrac{2x - 1}{4} - \dfrac{x + 2}{3} = 1$.

Multiply every term by 12: $3(2x - 1) - 4(x + 2) = 12 \Rightarrow 6x - 3 - 4x - 8 = 12 \Rightarrow 2x = 23 \Rightarrow x = 11.5$.
:::

::: example A perimeter problem
A rectangle has length $(3x + 2)$ cm and width $(x - 1)$ cm. Its perimeter is 42 cm. Find its area.

$2(3x + 2) + 2(x - 1) = 42 \Rightarrow 8x + 2 = 42 \Rightarrow x = 5$.

Length 17, width 4: area $= 68$ cm$^2$.
:::

::: example Angles
The angles of a triangle are $x$, $2x + 10$ and $3x - 40$ degrees. Find the largest angle.

$x + 2x + 10 + 3x - 40 = 180 \Rightarrow 6x = 210 \Rightarrow x = 35$.

Angles: $35°, 80°, 65°$. Largest $80°$.
:::

::: example Ages
Amir is 4 years older than Ben. In 6 years Amir will be twice as old as Ben was 3 years ago. How old is Ben now?

Let Ben be $b$; Amir is $b + 4$. In 6 years Amir is $b + 10$; 3 years ago Ben was $b - 3$.

$b + 10 = 2(b - 3) \Rightarrow b + 10 = 2b - 6 \Rightarrow b = 16$.
:::

::: q
Solve $7x - 9 = 3x + 15$.
---
$4x = 24 \Rightarrow x = 6$.
:::

::: q
Solve $4(x - 3) = 2(x + 5)$.
---
$4x - 12 = 2x + 10 \Rightarrow x = 11$.
:::

::: q
Solve $\dfrac{3x - 2}{5} = 4$.
---
$3x - 2 = 20 \Rightarrow x = \dfrac{22}{3}$.
:::

::: q
Solve $\dfrac{x}{2} + \dfrac{x}{3} = 10$.
---
$3x + 2x = 60 \Rightarrow x = 12$.
:::

::: q
Solve $\dfrac{x + 1}{2} - \dfrac{x - 2}{5} = 3$.
---
$5(x + 1) - 2(x - 2) = 30 \Rightarrow 3x + 9 = 30 \Rightarrow x = 7$.
:::

::: q
Solve $2 - 3(x - 4) = 5x - 2$.
---
$2 - 3x + 12 = 5x - 2 \Rightarrow 16 = 8x \Rightarrow x = 2$.
:::

::: q
The perimeter of an isosceles triangle with equal sides $(2x + 3)$ cm and base $(x - 1)$ cm is 40 cm. Find $x$.
---
$2(2x + 3) + x - 1 = 40 \Rightarrow 5x + 5 = 40 \Rightarrow x = 7$.
:::

::: q
Three consecutive integers add to 96. Find them.
---
$n + (n + 1) + (n + 2) = 96 \Rightarrow n = 31$: $31, 32, 33$.
:::

::: q
A number is doubled, 7 is added, and the result is divided by 3 to give 9. Find the number.
---
$\dfrac{2x + 7}{3} = 9 \Rightarrow 2x + 7 = 27 \Rightarrow x = 10$.
:::

::: q
Tickets cost £8 for adults and £5 for children. 60 tickets were sold for £399. How many were children's tickets?
---
$c$ children, $60 - c$ adults: $5c + 8(60 - c) = 399 \Rightarrow 480 - 3c = 399 \Rightarrow c = 27$.
:::

::: q
The angles of a quadrilateral are $x + 10$, $2x$, $3x - 20$ and $x + 20$ degrees. Find the smallest angle.
---
$7x + 10 = 360 \Rightarrow x = 50$. Angles: $60°, 100°, 130°, 70°$. Smallest $60°$.
:::

::: watch
- **Multiply every term** when clearing a fraction — including the ones that were not fractions.
- A minus outside a bracket flips every sign inside: $-3(x - 4) = -3x + 12$.
- Check by substituting into the **original** equation, not into a line halfway down.
- In a word problem, answer the question asked: the area, the largest angle, the number of children — not just "$x = 5$".
- Define your letter in a sentence ("let Ben's age be $b$"). It is a mark, and it stops you mixing up quantities.
- Fractions are fine as answers: $x = \frac{22}{3}$ unless decimals were asked for.
:::

::: test
Solve $7 - 2(x - 3) = 3x + 1$. (2)
---
$7 - 2x + 6 = 3x + 1$, so $13 - 2x = 3x + 1$ (1); $12 = 5x$, $x = 2.4$ (1).
:::

::: test
A rectangle has sides $(2x + 3)$ cm and $(x - 1)$ cm and perimeter 40 cm. Find $x$ and the area of the rectangle. (3)
---
$2(2x + 3 + x - 1) = 40$, so $3x + 2 = 20$ (1); $x = 6$ (1). Sides 15 cm and 5 cm, area 75 cm$^2$ (1).
:::

::: test
Solve $\dfrac{4}{x - 1} = 3$. (2)
---
$4 = 3(x - 1) = 3x - 3$ (1); $x = \dfrac{7}{3}$ (1).
:::

::: test
I think of a number, double it, subtract 5, then multiply the result by 3. The answer is 33. Find the number. (2)
---
$3(2n - 5) = 33$ (1); $2n - 5 = 11$, $n = 8$ (1).
:::
