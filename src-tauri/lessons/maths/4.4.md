## What it is

Measuring, and the quantities built from measurements.

**Compound measures** combine two units. The three you need, each with its triangle:

$$\text{speed} = \frac{\text{distance}}{\text{time}} \qquad \text{density} = \frac{\text{mass}}{\text{volume}} \qquad \text{pressure} = \frac{\text{force}}{\text{area}}$$

Cover the one you want and the triangle shows the calculation: distance $=$ speed $\times$ time, time $=$ distance $\div$ speed. Units follow: km/h, g/cm$^3$, N/m$^2$.

**Average speed** over a whole journey is *total* distance over *total* time — not the average of the speeds.

**Bearings** describe direction: measured **from north**, **clockwise**, written with **three figures**. Due east is $090°$; south-west is $225°$. A bearing question is usually an angle-facts question in disguise (north lines are parallel, so alternate and co-interior angles apply), sometimes with a scale drawing or trigonometry attached.

Also here: reading scales on instruments, time on the 12- and 24-hour clock, and sensible estimates (a door is about 2 m, an apple about 150 g).

::: terms
- **Bearing**: a direction measured clockwise from north, written as three figures, e.g. 045°.
- **Back bearing**: the bearing of the return journey: add or subtract 180°.
- **24-hour clock**: times from 00:00 to 23:59, with no am or pm.
- **Speed**: distance travelled ÷ time taken.
- **Density**: mass ÷ volume, e.g. in g/cm³.
- **Pressure**: force ÷ area, e.g. in N/m².
- **Compound measure**: a measure made from two others, such as speed (m/s) or density (g/cm³).
:::

::: method
1. **Compound measure:** write the formula, convert units to match what the answer needs, substitute, state units. Speed in km/h needs distance in km and time in **hours** (minutes $\div 60$).
2. **Average speed of a whole journey:** add up all the distances, add up all the times (each part's time from $d \div v$), then divide.
3. **Density:** find the volume from the shape's formula if it is not given; mass $=$ density $\times$ volume.
4. **Pressure:** area in m$^2$ for N/m$^2$ (pascals); a smaller area gives a bigger pressure.
5. **Bearing of B from A:** stand at A, face north, turn clockwise until you face B. Three figures: $045°$, not $45°$.
6. **Back bearing** (of A from B): add $180°$ if the bearing is under $180°$, subtract $180°$ if over.
7. **Bearings with angle facts:** draw north lines at every point (they are parallel); use co-interior angles ($180° -$) and angles round a point ($360° -$) to move between them.
8. **Reading a scale:** value of one small division $=$ gap between labelled marks $\div$ number of spaces.
:::

::: example Speed with unit conversion
A car travels 68 km in 51 minutes. Find its average speed in km/h.

$51$ min $= 0.85$ h. Speed $= 68 \div 0.85 = 80$ km/h.
:::

::: example Average speed of a two-part journey
Ana cycles 24 km at 16 km/h and then 10 km at 20 km/h. Find her average speed.

Times: $24 \div 16 = 1.5$ h; $10 \div 20 = 0.5$ h. Total $34$ km in $2$ h: $17$ km/h.

(Not $18$ — the average of the speeds is wrong because she spends longer at the slower speed.)
:::

::: example Density
A gold bar is a cuboid $12$ cm by $6$ cm by $4$ cm. Gold has density $19.3$ g/cm$^3$. Find its mass in kg.

Volume $= 288$ cm$^3$. Mass $= 288 \times 19.3 = 5558.4$ g $= 5.56$ kg (3 s.f.).
:::

::: example Pressure
A box weighs $420$ N and stands on a base $0.5$ m by $0.6$ m. Find the pressure on the floor.

Area $= 0.3$ m$^2$. Pressure $= 420 \div 0.3 = 1400$ N/m$^2$.
:::

::: example Bearings and back bearings
The bearing of B from A is $062°$. Find the bearing of A from B.

Under $180°$, so add: $062 + 180 = 242°$.
:::

::: example A bearing from angle facts
From port P, ship S is on a bearing of $130°$. From S, lighthouse L is on a bearing of $040°$. Find angle PSL.

At S, the north line makes $180° - 130° = 50°$ with SP (co-interior angles between the parallel north lines). Angle PSL $= 50° + 40° = 90°$.
:::

::: example Convert — 12- and 24-hour times
A train leaves at 10:47 pm and arrives at 01:25 the next morning. Write the departure time on the 24-hour clock and find the journey time. (3)

10:47 pm is 22:47 (1).

From 22:47 to midnight is 1 hour 13 minutes; from midnight to 01:25 is 1 hour 25 minutes (1).

Journey time = 1 h 13 min + 1 h 25 min = 2 hours 38 minutes (1).
:::

::: q
A train covers 210 km in 1 h 45 min. Find its average speed in km/h.
---
$210 \div 1.75 = 120$ km/h.
:::

::: q
How long does it take to travel 150 miles at 40 mph? Give the answer in hours and minutes.
---
$3.75$ h $= 3$ h $45$ min.
:::

::: q
A runner does 5 km at 12 km/h then 3 km at 8 km/h. Find the average speed for the whole run.
---
Times $\dfrac{5}{12} + \dfrac38 = \dfrac{19}{24}$ h; $8 \div \dfrac{19}{24} = 10.1$ km/h (3 s.f.).
:::

::: q
A metal cylinder has radius 3 cm, height 10 cm and mass 2260 g. Find its density.
---
$V = \pi \times 9 \times 10 = 282.7$ cm$^3$; density $= 2260 \div 282.7 = 7.99$ g/cm$^3$.
:::

::: q
Two liquids are mixed: 200 cm$^3$ of density 0.8 g/cm$^3$ and 300 cm$^3$ of density 1.2 g/cm$^3$. Find the density of the mixture.
---
Mass $160 + 360 = 520$ g; volume $500$; density $1.04$ g/cm$^3$.
:::

::: q
A force of 60 N acts on an area of 0.004 m$^2$. Find the pressure.
---
$15\,000$ N/m$^2$.
:::

::: q
Write the bearing of due west, of south-east, and of a direction $25°$ west of north.
---
$270°$; $135°$; $335°$.
:::

::: q
The bearing of Q from P is $295°$. Find the bearing of P from Q.
---
$295 - 180 = 115°$.
:::

::: q
A ship sails from A on a bearing of $070°$ to B, then turns to a bearing of $160°$. Through what angle does it turn, and what is angle ABC if it then reaches C?
---
Turn $= 160 - 70 = 90°$ clockwise; angle ABC $= 180 - 90 = 90°$.
:::

::: q
From O, point A is 8 km due east and point B is 6 km due north. Find the bearing of A from B, to the nearest degree.
---
At B, the angle between south and BA: $\tan\theta = \dfrac{8}{6}$, $\theta = 53°$; bearing $= 180 - 53 = 127°$.
:::

::: q
A scale reads from 0 to 1 kg with 10 small divisions between 0.4 and 0.5. The pointer is 7 divisions past 0.4. What is the reading?
---
Each division $0.01$ kg: $0.47$ kg.
:::

::: q
A meeting starts at 13:40 and lasts 2 hours 35 minutes. When does it end, in 12-hour clock?
---
16:15, i.e. 4:15 pm.
:::

::: watch
- **Minutes must become hours** before speed in km/h: 45 min is $0.75$ h, not $0.45$.
- Average speed $= \dfrac{\text{total distance}}{\text{total time}}$. Averaging two speeds is wrong unless the times are equal.
- Bearings: **from north, clockwise, three figures**. $062°$, never $62°$ and never anticlockwise.
- "Bearing of B **from** A": you are standing at A. Read which is which.
- North lines are parallel — the tools are co-interior angles and angles round a point.
- Units on every compound answer: km/h, g/cm$^3$, N/m$^2$. A number alone is incomplete.
:::

::: test
A ship sails 12 km on a bearing of 040°, then 9 km on a bearing of 130°. Find its distance from the start and the bearing of its final position from the start. (6)
---
The change of direction from 040° to 130° is 90°, so the path makes a right angle (1). Distance $= \sqrt{12^2 + 9^2}$ (1) $= 15$ km (1). Angle at start $= \tan^{-1}\left(\frac{9}{12}\right)$ (1) $= 36.9°$ (1). Bearing $= 40 + 36.9 = 077°$ (1).
:::
