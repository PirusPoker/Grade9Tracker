## What it is

**Standard form** writes any number as a number between 1 and 10, times a power of ten:

$$A \times 10^n, \qquad 1 \le A < 10, \quad n \text{ an integer}$$

$3\,400\,000 = 3.4 \times 10^6$; $0.00052 = 5.2 \times 10^{-4}$. Big numbers get positive powers, small ones negative. It exists so that the mass of the Earth ($5.97 \times 10^{24}$ kg) and the mass of an electron ($9.11 \times 10^{-31}$ kg) can be written, compared and multiplied without counting zeros.

Calculating in standard form is the index laws applied to the tens: multiply the $A$s, add the powers; divide the $A$s, subtract the powers. Adding and subtracting need the **same power** first — or just do it on the calculator with the $\times 10^x$ button.

::: terms
- **Standard form**: a number written as A × 10ⁿ, where 1 ≤ A < 10 and n is an integer.
- **Large numbers in standard form**: have a positive power of 10, e.g. 3 400 000 = 3.4 × 10⁶.
- **Small numbers in standard form**: have a negative power of 10, e.g. 0.00052 = 5.2 × 10⁻⁴.
- **Multiplying in standard form**: multiply the numbers and add the powers, then adjust so 1 ≤ A < 10.
- **Adding in standard form**: convert to ordinary numbers, or to the same power of 10, before adding.
:::

::: method
1. **Into standard form:** move the point until there is one non-zero digit in front of it; the number of places moved is the power — positive if the original number was 10 or more, negative if it was less than 1.
2. **Out of standard form:** move the point $n$ places right (positive $n$) or left (negative $n$), filling with zeros.
3. **Multiply:** $(a \times 10^m)(b \times 10^n) = ab \times 10^{m+n}$. If $ab$ is 10 or more, adjust: $24 \times 10^7 = 2.4 \times 10^8$.
4. **Divide:** $\dfrac{a}{b} \times 10^{m-n}$. If $\dfrac ab < 1$, adjust: $0.5 \times 10^3 = 5 \times 10^2$.
5. **Add / subtract:** write both with the same power of ten (use the larger power), add the $A$s, then re-adjust.
6. **Calculator:** type $3.4$, press the $\times 10^x$ key (sometimes EXP or EE), type $6$. Never type $\times 10 \wedge 6$ inside a division — it goes wrong with the order of operations.
7. **Ordering:** compare powers first; equal powers, compare $A$.
:::

::: example Writing numbers in standard form
Write (a) $67\,200\,000$, (b) $0.000\,000\,308$, (c) $45.6 \times 10^4$ in standard form.

(a) $6.72 \times 10^7$. (b) $3.08 \times 10^{-7}$. (c) $45.6 = 4.56 \times 10$, so $4.56 \times 10^5$.
:::

::: example Multiplying and dividing
Work out (a) $(3 \times 10^5) \times (4 \times 10^{-2})$, (b) $(8.4 \times 10^9) \div (2.1 \times 10^{3})$, (c) $(2 \times 10^{-3})^3$.

(a) $12 \times 10^3 = 1.2 \times 10^4$.

(b) $4 \times 10^6$.

(c) $8 \times 10^{-9}$.
:::

::: example Adding
Work out $5.3 \times 10^6 + 8 \times 10^5$.

$8 \times 10^5 = 0.8 \times 10^6$, so $5.3 \times 10^6 + 0.8 \times 10^6 = 6.1 \times 10^6$.
:::

::: example A problem in context
Light travels at $3 \times 10^8$ m/s. The Sun is $1.5 \times 10^{11}$ m away. How long does sunlight take to reach Earth?

$t = \dfrac{1.5 \times 10^{11}}{3 \times 10^8} = 0.5 \times 10^3 = 500$ s (about 8.3 minutes).
:::

::: example Mass of a set of atoms
One atom of gold has mass $3.27 \times 10^{-22}$ g. How many atoms are in 5 g of gold? Give your answer in standard form to 3 s.f.

$\dfrac{5}{3.27 \times 10^{-22}} = 1.529 \times 10^{22} \approx 1.53 \times 10^{22}$ atoms.
:::

::: example Ordering
Put $2.9 \times 10^{-3}$, $3.1 \times 10^{-4}$, $9 \times 10^{-4}$ and $0.0028$ in order, smallest first.

$0.0028 = 2.8 \times 10^{-3}$. Powers $-4$ first: $3.1 \times 10^{-4}$, $9 \times 10^{-4}$; then $-3$: $2.8 \times 10^{-3}$, $2.9 \times 10^{-3}$.
:::

::: q
Write $0.000\,415$ and $920\,000\,000$ in standard form.
---
$4.15 \times 10^{-4}$; $9.2 \times 10^8$.
:::

::: q
Write $7.03 \times 10^{-5}$ and $6.2 \times 10^{4}$ as ordinary numbers.
---
$0.0000703$; $62\,000$.
:::

::: q
Work out $(6 \times 10^4) \times (7 \times 10^5)$, giving your answer in standard form.
---
$42 \times 10^9 = 4.2 \times 10^{10}$.
:::

::: q
Work out $(3 \times 10^{-2}) \div (6 \times 10^{4})$ in standard form.
---
$0.5 \times 10^{-6} = 5 \times 10^{-7}$.
:::

::: q
Work out $4.7 \times 10^8 - 3.9 \times 10^7$.
---
$47 \times 10^7 - 3.9 \times 10^7 = 43.1 \times 10^7 = 4.31 \times 10^8$.
:::

::: q
Work out $(5 \times 10^3)^2 \div (2.5 \times 10^{-2})$.
---
$25 \times 10^6 \div (2.5 \times 10^{-2}) = 10 \times 10^8 = 1 \times 10^9$.
:::

::: q
A rectangle has length $3.2 \times 10^4$ cm and width $2.5 \times 10^3$ cm. Find its area in standard form.
---
$8 \times 10^7$ cm$^2$.
:::

::: q
The population of a country is $6.8 \times 10^7$ and its area $2.4 \times 10^5$ km$^2$. Find the population density in people per km$^2$, to 3 s.f.
---
$\dfrac{6.8 \times 10^7}{2.4 \times 10^5} = 2.83 \times 10^2 = 283$ people/km$^2$.
:::

::: q
A virus is $1.2 \times 10^{-7}$ m long. How many, laid end to end, make 1 mm? Give your answer in standard form.
---
$\dfrac{10^{-3}}{1.2 \times 10^{-7}} = 8.33 \times 10^3$ (3 s.f.).
:::

::: q
Which is larger, $8.9 \times 10^{-6}$ or $1.1 \times 10^{-5}$, and by how much?
---
$1.1 \times 10^{-5} = 11 \times 10^{-6}$ is larger, by $2.1 \times 10^{-6}$.
:::

::: watch
- **$A$ must be between 1 and 10.** $42 \times 10^9$ and $0.5 \times 10^3$ are not standard form; adjust the power.
- Moving the point left makes the power **bigger** by one ($24 \times 10^7 = 2.4 \times 10^8$); right makes it smaller.
- Add and subtract only after matching the powers.
- On the calculator, use the $\times 10^x$ key and put a standard-form number in **brackets** when it is a denominator.
- Negative power means small, not negative: $5.2 \times 10^{-4} = 0.00052$.
- "Give your answer in standard form" — the ordinary number, even if correct, loses the final mark.
:::

Work out $(8.4 \times 10^7) \div (2.1 \times 10^{-3})$, giving the answer in standard form. (2)

Work out $5.6 \times 10^5 + 8 \times 10^4$, giving the answer in standard form. (2)
---
$560\,000 + 80\,000 = 640\,000$ (1) $= 6.4 \times 10^5$ (1).
:::

::: test
Light travels at $3 \times 10^8$ m/s. There are $3.15 \times 10^7$ seconds in a year. How far does light travel in a year? Give the answer in standard form to 2 significant figures. (2)
---
$3 \times 10^8 \times 3.15 \times 10^7 = 9.45 \times 10^{15}$ (1) $\approx 9.5 \times 10^{15}$ m (1).
:::

::: test
A hydrogen atom has mass $1.67 \times 10^{-27}$ kg. Find the mass of $5 \times 10^{23}$ hydrogen atoms in standard form. (2)
---
$1.67 \times 5 = 8.35$ and $10^{-27 + 23} = 10^{-4}$ (1): $8.35 \times 10^{-4}$ kg (1).
:::

Write these in order of size, smallest first: $2.1 \times 10^{-3}$, $9.8 \times 10^{-4}$, $1.05 \times 10^{-3}$, $0.0015$. (2)
---
As ordinary numbers: 0.0021, 0.00098, 0.00105, 0.0015 (1). Order: $9.8 \times 10^{-4}$, $1.05 \times 10^{-3}$, $0.0015$, $2.1 \times 10^{-3}$ (1).
