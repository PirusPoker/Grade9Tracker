## What it is

"Per cent" means per hundred: $35\% = \dfrac{35}{100} = 0.35$. The powerful idea at Higher tier is the **multiplier**: a percentage change is a single multiplication.

- Increase by $12\%$: multiply by $1.12$.
- Decrease by $12\%$: multiply by $0.88$.
- Find $12\%$ of something: multiply by $0.12$.

Because a change is a multiplication, **repeated** changes are repeated multiplications — powers. $\pounds 500$ at $3\%$ compound interest for 5 years is $500 \times 1.03^5$. And a **reverse** percentage — "after a 20% increase the price is £96, what was it before?" — is a division: $96 \div 1.2 = 80$.

Two things to keep straight: percentage **of** (a quantity) versus percentage **change** (a comparison), and **simple** interest (the same amount added each year) versus **compound** (interest on the interest).

::: method
1. **Write the multiplier first.** $+15\% \to 1.15$; $-15\% \to 0.85$; $+2.5\% \to 1.025$; $-0.5\% \to 0.995$.
2. **Percentage change** $= \dfrac{\text{change}}{\text{original}} \times 100\%$. The bottom is always the *original* (the earlier value).
3. **One number as a percentage of another:** $\dfrac{\text{part}}{\text{whole}} \times 100$. Same units first.
4. **Reverse percentage:** new value $\div$ multiplier $=$ original. Never subtract the percentage from the new value.
5. **Compound change:** original $\times$ multiplier$^{\,n}$. For a target ("how many years until it exceeds…"), try values of $n$ on the calculator or use logs.
6. **Depreciation** is compound decrease: $\times 0.8^n$ for 20% a year.
7. **Two successive changes** multiply: $+20\%$ then $-20\%$ is $\times 1.2 \times 0.8 = \times 0.96$, an overall $4\%$ decrease — not zero.
:::

::: example Percentage of, and percentage change
(a) A jacket costing £68 is reduced by 35%. Find the sale price. (b) A town's population rose from 12,400 to 13,020. Find the percentage increase.

(a) $68 \times 0.65 = \pounds 44.20$.

(b) Change $= 620$. $\dfrac{620}{12400} \times 100 = 5\%$.
:::

::: example Reverse percentage
After a 15% price rise a phone costs £299. What did it cost before?

Original $\times 1.15 = 299$, so original $= 299 \div 1.15 = \pounds 260$.
:::

::: example Reverse with a decrease
In a sale everything is 30% off. A coat's sale price is £84. Find the original price.

$84 \div 0.7 = \pounds 120$.
:::

::: example Compound interest
£2000 is invested at 4% per year compound interest. Find the value after 6 years, and the interest earned.

$2000 \times 1.04^6 = 2000 \times 1.26532 = \pounds 2530.64$.

Interest $= 2530.64 - 2000 = \pounds 530.64$.
:::

::: example Depreciation over time
A car worth £18,000 loses 15% of its value each year. After how many whole years is it first worth less than half its original value?

Need $0.85^n < 0.5$. $0.85^4 = 0.522$, $0.85^5 = 0.444$. After **5** years.
:::

::: example Working back through compound change
An investment grew by 6% a year compound for 3 years and is now worth £5955.08. What was invested, to the nearest pound?

Original $\times 1.06^3 = 5955.08$, so original $= 5955.08 \div 1.191016 = 5000.00$. £5000 was invested.
:::

::: q
Find 17.5% of £460, and increase £460 by 17.5%.
---
$460 \times 0.175 = \pounds 80.50$; $460 \times 1.175 = \pounds 540.50$.
:::

::: q
A laptop's price falls from £750 to £630. Find the percentage decrease.
---
$\dfrac{120}{750} \times 100 = 16\%$.
:::

::: q
A student scored 54 out of 80 in a test. Write this as a percentage.
---
$\dfrac{54}{80} \times 100 = 67.5\%$.
:::

::: q
After a 12% increase, a salary is £30,240. Find the salary before the increase.
---
$30240 \div 1.12 = \pounds 27{,}000$.
:::

::: q
A TV is reduced by 40% to £354. Find the original price.
---
$354 \div 0.6 = \pounds 590$.
:::

::: q
£3500 is invested at 2.5% per year compound. Find the total after 4 years, to the nearest penny.
---
$3500 \times 1.025^4 = 3500 \times 1.10381 = \pounds 3863.35$.
:::

::: q
A house bought for £240,000 rises in value by 5% one year and falls by 5% the next. What is it worth, and what is the overall percentage change?
---
$240000 \times 1.05 \times 0.95 = 240000 \times 0.9975 = \pounds 239{,}400$. Overall change $-0.25\%$.
:::

::: q
A machine costing £40,000 depreciates by 20% each year. After how many years is its value first below £15,000?
---
$0.8^n < 0.375$: $0.8^4 = 0.4096$, $0.8^5 = 0.328$. After 5 years.
:::

::: q
The price of a ticket including 20% VAT is £54. Find the VAT charged.
---
Pre-VAT price $= 54 \div 1.2 = 45$; VAT $= \pounds 9$.
:::

::: q
£P is invested at $r\%$ compound interest. After 2 years it is worth $1.1025P$. Find $r$.
---
$\left(1 + \dfrac{r}{100}\right)^2 = 1.1025 \Rightarrow 1 + \dfrac{r}{100} = 1.05 \Rightarrow r = 5$.
:::

::: q
Simple interest of 3% a year is paid on £800 for 4 years. How much more would compound interest at 3% have paid over the same time?
---
Simple: $800 \times 0.03 \times 4 = 96$. Compound: $800(1.03^4 - 1) = 800 \times 0.12551 = 100.41$. Difference $\pounds 4.41$.
:::

::: watch
- **Reverse percentage: divide by the multiplier.** Taking 15% *off* £299 gives £254.15, which is wrong — the 15% was of the *original*, not of £299.
- Percentage change is over the **original** value, always.
- Compound means **powers**: $1.04^6$, not $1.04 \times 6$ and not $1.24$.
- Write the multiplier down as a line of working: "$\times 1.04^6$" earns the method mark even if the arithmetic slips.
- Successive percentages multiply; they do not add. $+50\%$ then $-50\%$ leaves $75\%$.
- Money answers to 2 decimal places, and "to the nearest penny" means don't round earlier than the last step.
:::

::: test
A town's population of 52,000 grows by 3% each year. Find the population after 5 years, to the nearest hundred. (2)
---
$52000 \times 1.03^5$ (1) $= 60,282\ldots \approx 60,300$ (1).
:::

::: test
A price is increased by 25% and then decreased by 20%. Find the overall percentage change. (2)
---
Multiplier $1.25 \times 0.8 = 1$ (1), so the overall change is 0% — the price is back where it started (1).
:::

::: test
The value of a bike falls from £320 to £272. Find the percentage decrease. (2)
---
Decrease $= £48$ (1); $\frac{48}{320} \times 100 = 15\%$ (1).
:::

::: test
£5000 invested at compound interest grows to £5618 after 2 years. Find the annual interest rate. (3)
---
$5000 \times m^2 = 5618$, so $m^2 = 1.1236$ (1); $m = \sqrt{1.1236} = 1.06$ (1); rate $= 6\%$ (1).
:::

::: test
A price including 20% VAT is £91.20. Find the price before VAT was added. (2)
---
£91.20 is 120% (1); $91.20 \div 1.2 = £76$ (1).
:::
