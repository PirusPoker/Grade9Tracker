**1 (a)** Which one of the following is a metamorphic rock? (1)
A. Chalk
B. Granite
C. Slate
D. Clay

**(b)** Explain one way that farming has changed lowland landscapes in the UK. (2)

**(c)** Explain one reason why upland landscapes in the north and west of the UK are made of different rocks from lowland landscapes in the south and east. (2)

(5)
---
(a) C (1).

(b) 1 for a way, 1 for development. E.g. hedgerows were removed to make larger fields for machinery (1), creating open, patchwork-free arable landscapes such as parts of East Anglia (1). Or: wetlands such as the Fens were drained (1), turning marsh into flat, intensively farmed land (1).

(c) 1 for a reason, 1 for development. E.g. the north and west were shaped by past tectonic activity, with volcanic eruptions and intrusions (1), leaving hard igneous and metamorphic rocks such as granite and slate that resist erosion and form uplands (1). Accept that the south and east are younger sedimentary rocks such as chalk and clay laid down in seas (1), which are softer and form lowlands (1).
