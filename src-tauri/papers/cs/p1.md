`total = total + i * i`
`endif`

::: q 8-9 2.1.3
**12.** (a) Show the steps of a binary search for 27 in the list 3, 8, 12, 19, 27, 34, 41, 50. (3)

(b) Explain why merge sort is preferred to bubble sort for a list of a million records. (2)

(5)
---
(a) Middle of 8 is the 4th item, 19; 27 is larger, so search 27, 34, 41, 50 (1); middle is 34 (or 27 depending on convention); 27 is smaller, so search 27 (1); found — three comparisons at most (1).

(b) Bubble sort makes many passes comparing neighbours, so its work grows roughly with the square of the list size (1); merge sort halves the list repeatedly and merges, so it is far faster on large lists (1).
