**5.** A developer is designing a cinema ticket booking program. Explain, with an example from this program, what is meant by decomposition and by abstraction. (2)
---

::: q 8-9 1.3.1 1.4.2
**13.** (a) Give one advantage of a mesh topology over a star topology, and one advantage of a star over a mesh. (2)

(b) Explain how a firewall and encryption each protect a company's data. (2)

(4)
---
(a) Mesh: no single point of failure, data can take another route if a link fails (1); star: cheaper and simpler to set up, and a fault in one cable affects only one device (1).

(b) A firewall examines incoming and outgoing traffic and blocks anything that does not meet its rules, stopping unauthorised access (1); encryption scrambles stored or transmitted data with a key so that anyone who intercepts or steals it cannot read it (1).
:::
