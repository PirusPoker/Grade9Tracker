**1.** (a) Write 360 as a product of its prime factors. (2)

(b) $84 = 2^2 \times 3 \times 7$. Find the lowest common multiple (LCM) of 360 and 84. (2)

::: q 4-5 R3-8
**2.** Amir, Beth and Cara share some money in the ratio $2 : 3 : 7$. Cara gets £60 more than Beth.

How much money does Amir get? (3)
---
The difference is $7 - 3 = 4$ parts (P1); one part is $60 \div 4 = £15$ (P1); Amir gets $2 \times 15 = £30$ (A1).

::: q 4-5 A17
**3.** Solve $\dfrac{3x - 1}{4} = 2x - 9$. (3)
---
Multiply both sides by 4: $3x - 1 = 8x - 36$ (M1); collect terms: $35 = 5x$ (M1); $x = 7$ (A1).

::: q 4-5 G3-4
**4.** Each interior angle of a regular polygon is 7 times as big as each exterior angle.

How many sides does the polygon have? (3)
---
Interior and exterior angles add to $180^\circ$, so $e + 7e = 180$ (P1); exterior angle $e = 22.5^\circ$ (P1); $360 \div 22.5 = 16$ sides (A1).
:::

::: q 4-5 R11
**5.** A train travels 135 km in 1 hour 30 minutes.

Work out its average speed in metres per second. (3)
---
$135 \div 1.5 = 90$ km/h (M1); $90 \times 1000 \div 3600$, or $135\,000 \div 5400$ (M1); $25$ m/s (A1). (Using 1.3 hours for 1 hour 30 minutes scores no marks for the division.)
:::

::: q 4-5 P6-8
**6.** There are 30 students in a class. 18 study French, 14 study Spanish and 5 study neither language.

(a) How many students study both French and Spanish? (2)

(b) A student is chosen at random from the class. Find the probability that this student studies exactly one of the two languages. (2)

(4)
---
(a) $30 - 5 = 25$ study at least one language (M1); $18 + 14 - 25 = 7$ (A1).

(b) French only $18 - 7 = 11$, Spanish only $14 - 7 = 7$, so $11 + 7 = 18$ students (M1); $\dfrac{18}{30} = \dfrac{3}{5}$ (A1) oe.
:::

**7.** Here are the times, in minutes, taken by 11 runners in Group A to finish a race.

12, 14, 15, 15, 17, 18, 20, 21, 23, 26, 30

(a) Find the median and the interquartile range of the times. (3)

(b) The runners in Group B had a median time of 16 minutes and an interquartile range of 11 minutes. Make two comparisons between the times of Group A and Group B. (2)

(5)
---
(a) Median $= 18$ minutes (B1); lower quartile $15$ and upper quartile $23$ (the 3rd and 9th values) (M1); interquartile range $= 8$ minutes (A1).

(b) One comparison of averages in context, e.g. Group A's median is higher, so on average Group A took longer (C1). One comparison of spread in context, e.g. Group A's IQR is smaller, so Group A's times were more consistent (C1). (Comparisons must be in context; "the median is bigger" alone scores 0.)

::: q 6-7 N6-7
**10.** (a) Find the value of $27^{-\frac{2}{3}}$. (2)

(b) $2^n = \dfrac{8^5}{4^3}$. Find the value of $n$. (2)

(4)
---
(a) $27^{\frac{1}{3}} = 3$, or $27^{\frac{2}{3}} = 9$, or the reciprocal used correctly (M1); $\dfrac{1}{9}$ (A1).

(b) $8^5 = 2^{15}$ or $4^3 = 2^6$ (M1); $2^{15 - 6} = 2^9$, so $n = 9$ (A1).

**11.** In a sale, all prices are reduced by 20%. A jacket costs £64 in the sale.

(a) Work out the price of the jacket before the sale. (2)

(b) After the sale the shop increases the sale price of the jacket by 20%. Sam says, "The jacket is now back to its price before the sale." Is Sam correct? Show how you decide. (1)

(3)
---
(a) £64 is 80% of the original price, so $64 \div 0.8$ (M1); £80 (A1). (Adding 20% of £64 to get £76.80 scores 0.)

(b) No: $64 \times 1.2 = £76.80$, which is less than £80, because the 20% is now taken of a smaller amount (C1).

(a) Write down the exact value of $\tan 60^\circ$. (1)

(b) Find the exact length of $QR$. (2)

**14.** Line $L$ passes through the points $(2, 5)$ and $(6, -3)$.

Find an equation of the line that is perpendicular to $L$ and passes through the point $(4, 1)$. (4)
---
Gradient of $L = \dfrac{-3 - 5}{6 - 2} = -2$ (M1); perpendicular gradient $= \dfrac{1}{2}$, the negative reciprocal (M1); $1 = \dfrac{1}{2} \times 4 + c$, or $y - 1 = \dfrac{1}{2}(x - 4)$ (M1); $y = \dfrac{1}{2}x - 1$ (A1) oe, e.g. $x - 2y = 2$.

**16.** $y$ is inversely proportional to the square of $x$. When $x = 4$, $y = 5$.

(a) Find $y$ when $x = 10$. (2)

(b) Find the positive value of $x$ when $y = 20$. (1)

(3)
---
(a) $y = \dfrac{k}{x^2}$ and $5 = \dfrac{k}{16}$, so $k = 80$ (M1); $y = \dfrac{80}{100} = \dfrac{4}{5}$ or $0.8$ (A1).

(b) $20 = \dfrac{80}{x^2}$, so $x^2 = 4$ and $x = 2$ (B1).

**18.** The table gives information about the times, $t$ seconds, taken by 85 people to solve a puzzle. The information is to be shown in a histogram.

| Time ($t$ seconds) | $0 < t \le 10$ | $10 < t \le 15$ | $15 < t \le 20$ | $20 < t \le 40$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Frequency | 12 | 18 | 25 | 30 |

(a) Work out the frequency density of the $20 < t \le 40$ class. (1)

(b) Work out an estimate for the number of people who took more than 17 seconds. (2)

(3)
---
(a) $30 \div 20 = 1.5$ (B1).

(b) Assume the 25 times in $15 < t \le 20$ are spread evenly, so $\dfrac{3}{5} \times 25 = 15$ took between 17 and 20 seconds (M1); $15 + 30 = 45$ people (A1).

::: q 8-9 G9-10
**19.** $A$, $B$ and $C$ are points on a circle with centre $O$. The straight line $PAT$ is the tangent to the circle at $A$. Angle $TAC = 58^\circ$, and $B$ lies on the major arc $AC$.

(a) Find the size of angle $ABC$. Give a reason for your answer. (2)

(b) Find the size of angle $OCA$. Give reasons for each stage of your working. (2)

(4)
---
(a) $58^\circ$ (B1); the angle between a tangent and a chord is equal to the angle in the alternate segment (C1).

(b) Angle $OAT = 90^\circ$ because a tangent is perpendicular to the radius at the point of contact, so angle $OAC = 90 - 58 = 32^\circ$ (M1); triangle $OAC$ is isosceles because $OA = OC$ are radii, so angle $OCA = 32^\circ$ (A1, with both reasons). (Alternative: angle $AOC = 116^\circ$, as the angle at the centre is twice the angle at the circumference, then $(180 - 116) \div 2 = 32^\circ$.)
:::

**20.** Solve $\dfrac{3}{x + 1} + \dfrac{2}{x - 2} = 1$.

Give your answers in the form $a \pm \sqrt{b}$, where $a$ and $b$ are integers. (4)
---
Multiply through by $(x + 1)(x - 2)$: $3(x - 2) + 2(x + 1) = (x + 1)(x - 2)$ (M1); $5x - 4 = x^2 - x - 2$, so $x^2 - 6x + 2 = 0$ (A1); complete the square or use the formula: $(x - 3)^2 = 7$, or $x = \dfrac{6 \pm \sqrt{36 - 8}}{2}$ (M1); $x = 3 \pm \sqrt{7}$ (A1).

::: q 8-9 G24-25
**21.** $OAB$ is a triangle with $\overrightarrow{OA} = \mathbf{a}$ and $\overrightarrow{OB} = \mathbf{b}$.

$X$ is the point on $OA$ such that $OX : XA = 3 : 1$. $Y$ is the midpoint of $AB$.

(a) Find $\overrightarrow{XY}$ in terms of $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$. Give your answer in its simplest form. (2)

(b) $OB$ is extended to the point $Z$ so that $OB : BZ = 2 : 1$. Prove that $X$, $Y$ and $Z$ lie on a straight line. (3)

(5)
---
(a) $\overrightarrow{XY} = \overrightarrow{XA} + \overrightarrow{AY} = \dfrac{1}{4}\mathbf{a} + \dfrac{1}{2}(\mathbf{b} - \mathbf{a})$ (M1); $= -\dfrac{1}{4}\mathbf{a} + \dfrac{1}{2}\mathbf{b}$, or $\dfrac{1}{4}(2\mathbf{b} - \mathbf{a})$ (A1).

(b) $\overrightarrow{OZ} = \dfrac{3}{2}\mathbf{b}$ (P1); $\overrightarrow{XZ} = -\dfrac{3}{4}\mathbf{a} + \dfrac{3}{2}\mathbf{b}$ (P1); $\overrightarrow{XZ} = 3\overrightarrow{XY}$, so $XZ$ is parallel to $XY$, and they share the point $X$, so $X$, $Y$ and $Z$ are on a straight line (C1: both "parallel" and "common point" needed).
:::

::: q 8-9 A16
**22.** The point $P(2, 4)$ lies on the circle $x^2 + y^2 = 20$.

(a) Find an equation of the tangent to the circle at $P$. (3)

(b) The tangent meets the $x$-axis at $Q$. $O$ is the origin. Find the area of triangle $OPQ$. (2)

(5)
---
(a) Gradient of the radius $OP = \dfrac{4}{2} = 2$ (M1); gradient of the tangent $= -\dfrac{1}{2}$ (M1); $y - 4 = -\dfrac{1}{2}(x - 2)$, i.e. $y = -\dfrac{1}{2}x + 5$ or $x + 2y = 10$ (A1).

(b) $y = 0$ gives $x = 10$, so $Q$ is $(10, 0)$ (M1); area $= \dfrac{1}{2} \times 10 \times 4 = 20$ square units, using the height of $P$ above the $x$-axis (A1).
:::
