**19.** Two cylinders are mathematically similar. The surface area of the smaller cylinder is $45\text{ cm}^2$ and the surface area of the larger cylinder is $80\text{ cm}^2$. The volume of the smaller cylinder is $54\text{ cm}^3$.

Work out the volume of the larger cylinder. (3)
---
M1 area scale factor $\dfrac{80}{45} = \dfrac{16}{9}$, so length scale factor $\dfrac{4}{3}$.

A1 $54 \times \dfrac{64}{27} = 128\text{ cm}^3$.

**20.** $A$ and $B$ are points on a circle with centre $O$. $PA$ and $PB$ are tangents to the circle from the point $P$. $C$ is a point on the major arc $AB$. Angle $APB = 48^\circ$.

Work out the size of angle $ACB$. Give a reason for each step of your working. (3)
---
B1 angles $OAP$ and $OBP$ are $90^\circ$ because a tangent is perpendicular to the radius at the point of contact.

M1 angle $AOB = 360 - 90 - 90 - 48 = 132^\circ$ (angles in a quadrilateral add up to $360^\circ$).

A1 angle $ACB = 66^\circ$ because the angle at the centre is twice the angle at the circumference.

**26.** The point $P(3, 4)$ lies on the circle $x^2 + y^2 = 25$.

(a) Work out the equation of the tangent to the circle at $P$. Give your answer in the form $y = mx + c$. (3)

(b) The tangent meets the $x$-axis at the point $Q$. Work out the $x$-coordinate of $Q$. (1)

(4)
---
(a) M1 gradient of the radius $OP = \dfrac{4}{3}$.

M1 gradient of the tangent $= -\dfrac{3}{4}$ (negative reciprocal) and substitutes $(3, 4)$: $4 = -\dfrac{3}{4}(3) + c$.

A1 $y = -\dfrac{3}{4}x + \dfrac{25}{4}$ oe.

(b) B1ft $x = \dfrac{25}{3}$ (or $8\dfrac{1}{3}$).

::: q 8-9 G24-25
**27.** $OAB$ is a triangle. $\overrightarrow{OA} = \mathbf{a}$ and $\overrightarrow{OB} = \mathbf{b}$.

$C$ is the point on $AB$ such that $AC : CB = 1 : 2$.

(a) Show that $\overrightarrow{OC} = \dfrac{1}{3}(2\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b})$ (2)

(b) $E$ is the point such that $\overrightarrow{AE} = \mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b}$.

Prove that $O$, $C$ and $E$ lie on a straight line. (2)

(4)
---
(a) M1 $\overrightarrow{AB} = \mathbf{b} - \mathbf{a}$ and $\overrightarrow{OC} = \mathbf{a} + \dfrac{1}{3}(\mathbf{b} - \mathbf{a})$.

A1 $\dfrac{2}{3}\mathbf{a} + \dfrac{1}{3}\mathbf{b} = \dfrac{1}{3}(2\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b})$ with working shown.

(b) M1 $\overrightarrow{OE} = \overrightarrow{OA} + \overrightarrow{AE} = 2\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b}$.

A1 $\overrightarrow{OE} = 3\overrightarrow{OC}$, so $OC$ and $OE$ are parallel and share the point $O$; hence $O$, $C$ and $E$ are collinear.
