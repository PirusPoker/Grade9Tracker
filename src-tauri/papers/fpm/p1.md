**6.** $f(x) = 2x^3 + x^2 - 13x + 6$.

(a) Show that $(x - 2)$ is a factor of $f(x)$. (1)

(b) Factorise $f(x)$ completely. (3)

(c) Hence solve $f(x) = 0$. (1)

(5)
---
(a) $f(2) = 16 + 4 - 26 + 6 = 0$ (1).

(b) $f(x) = (x - 2)(2x^2 + 5x - 3)$ (2); $= (x - 2)(2x - 1)(x + 3)$ (1).

(c) $x = 2, \dfrac{1}{2}, -3$ (1).

**7.** An arithmetic series has first term 7. The sum of its first 10 terms is 250.

(a) Find the common difference. (3)

(b) Find the smallest value of $n$ for which the $n$th term exceeds 100. (2)

(5)
---
(a) $\dfrac{10}{2}[14 + 9d] = 250$ (1); $14 + 9d = 50$ (1); $d = 4$ (1).

(b) $7 + 4(n - 1) > 100$ gives $n - 1 > 23.25$ (1); $n = 25$ (1).

::: q 6-7 2
**8.** The roots of $2x^2 - 5x + 1 = 0$ are $\alpha$ and $\beta$.

(a) Find the value of $\alpha^2 + \beta^2$. (2)

(b) Find a quadratic equation, with integer coefficients, whose roots are $\dfrac{1}{\alpha}$ and $\dfrac{1}{\beta}$. (3)

(5)
---
(a) $\alpha + \beta = \dfrac{5}{2}$, $\alpha\beta = \dfrac{1}{2}$ (1); $\alpha^2 + \beta^2 = \dfrac{25}{4} - 1 = \dfrac{21}{4}$ (1).

(b) Sum $= \dfrac{\alpha + \beta}{\alpha\beta} = 5$ (1); product $= \dfrac{1}{\alpha\beta} = 2$ (1); $x^2 - 5x + 2 = 0$ (1).
:::

**9.** Solve $2\cos^2 x + 3\sin x = 3$ for $0^\circ \le x \le 360^\circ$. (5)
---
Use $\cos^2 x = 1 - \sin^2 x$: $2 - 2\sin^2 x + 3\sin x - 3 = 0$ (1); $2\sin^2 x - 3\sin x + 1 = 0$ (1); $(2\sin x - 1)(\sin x - 1) = 0$ (1); $\sin x = \dfrac{1}{2}$: $x = 30^\circ, 150^\circ$ (1); $\sin x = 1$: $x = 90^\circ$ (1).
:::

**11.** The curve $C$ has equation $y = x^3 - 3x^2 - 9x + 5$.

(a) Find the coordinates of the stationary points of $C$ and determine the nature of each. (5)

(b) Find the equation of the tangent to $C$ at the point where $x = 0$. (2)

(7)
---
(a) $\dfrac{dy}{dx} = 3x^2 - 6x - 9 = 3(x - 3)(x + 1)$ (1); $x = 3$ or $x = -1$ (1); $(3, -22)$ and $(-1, 10)$ (1); $\dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2} = 6x - 6$: positive at $x = 3$, minimum (1); negative at $x = -1$, maximum (1).

(b) Gradient at $x = 0$ is $-9$, point $(0, 5)$ (1); $y = -9x + 5$ (1).
:::

::: q 8-9 9c
**12.** (a) Find the area of the finite region enclosed between the curve $y = x^2 - 4x$ and the $x$-axis. (4)

(b) The region between the curve $y = \sqrt{x}$, the $x$-axis and the line $x = 4$ is rotated through $360^\circ$ about the $x$-axis. Find the exact volume of the solid formed. (3)

(7)
---
(a) The curve crosses the axis at $x = 0$ and $x = 4$ (1); $\int_0^4 (x^2 - 4x)\,dx = \left[\dfrac{x^3}{3} - 2x^2\right]_0^4$ (1) $= \dfrac{64}{3} - 32 = -\dfrac{32}{3}$ (1); area $= \dfrac{32}{3}$ (1).

(b) $V = \pi\int_0^4 x\,dx$ (1) $= \pi\left[\dfrac{x^2}{2}\right]_0^4$ (1) $= 8\pi$ (1).
:::

::: q 8-9 7
**13.** In triangle $OAB$, $\overrightarrow{OA} = \mathbf{a}$ and $\overrightarrow{OB} = \mathbf{b}$. $P$ lies on $OA$ with $OP : PA = 2 : 1$, and $Q$ lies on $AB$ with $AQ : QB = 1 : 2$.

Show that $PQ$ is parallel to $OB$, and find the ratio $PQ : OB$. (4)
---
$\overrightarrow{OP} = \dfrac{2}{3}\mathbf{a}$ (1); $\overrightarrow{OQ} = \mathbf{a} + \dfrac{1}{3}(\mathbf{b} - \mathbf{a}) = \dfrac{2}{3}\mathbf{a} + \dfrac{1}{3}\mathbf{b}$ (1); $\overrightarrow{PQ} = \overrightarrow{OQ} - \overrightarrow{OP} = \dfrac{1}{3}\mathbf{b}$ (1); a multiple of $\mathbf{b}$, so parallel to $OB$, and $PQ : OB = 1 : 3$ (1).
:::

::: q 8-9 9d
**14.** A particle moves along a straight line. At time $t$ seconds its velocity is $v = 3t^2 - 12t + 9$ m/s, and at $t = 0$ it is at the origin $O$.

(a) Find the times at which the particle is at rest. (2)

(b) Find the displacement of the particle from $O$ when $t = 3$. (2)

(c) Find the total distance travelled between $t = 0$ and $t = 3$. (3)

(7)
---
(a) $3(t - 1)(t - 3) = 0$ (1); $t = 1$ and $t = 3$ (1).

(b) $s = t^3 - 6t^2 + 9t$ (1); $s(3) = 27 - 54 + 27 = 0$ (1).

(c) It reverses at $t = 1$: $s(1) = 4$ (1); it travels 4 m out and 4 m back (1); total $8$ m (1).
:::
