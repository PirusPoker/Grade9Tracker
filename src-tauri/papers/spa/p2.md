intro: AQA GCSE Spanish 8692 Higher, Paper 4 style: 1 hour 15 minutes, no dictionary. Question 1 is a translation into Spanish of five sentences. AQA marks it as one 10-mark answer: 5 marks for how many of its 15 elements you convey, and 5 for your vocabulary and grammar overall. This app splits it into one block per sentence, worth 2 marks each (1 for meaning, 1 for accurate Spanish), so you can see which sentence cost you marks. Question 2 (about 90 words, 15 marks) and Question 3 (about 150 words, 25 marks) each offer a choice of two tasks: answer one task only, and write something about every bullet point. Suggested timing: 20 minutes for Question 1, 20 for Question 2, 30 for Question 3, and 5 for checking verbs, agreements and accents.

::: q 4-5 3.1.1a 3.2.1h 4.7
**Question 1.** Translate the following sentences into Spanish. (Sentences 1 to 5 appear one per block.)

**Sentence 1.** My sister is very nice, but she never helps at home.

(2)
---
Model: *Mi hermana es muy simpática, pero nunca ayuda en casa.* (Also accept *amable*, and *no ayuda nunca*.)

Meaning (1): all three elements conveyed, minor slips allowed: my sister is very nice | but she never helps | at home.

Accuracy (1): *hermana* and *simpática* both feminine; *es* (ser for a trait, not *está*); *nunca* before the verb, or *no ... nunca* around it; *ayuda* in the third person singular.
:::

::: q 4-5 3.1.3a 3.2.1d 4.7
**Sentence 2.** Last summer we went to the coast by train with our friends.

(2)
---
Model: *El verano pasado fuimos a la costa en tren con nuestros amigos.*

Meaning (1): all three elements conveyed: last summer we went | to the coast by train | with our friends.

Accuracy (1): the preterite *fuimos* (not *fui*, *íbamos* or *vamos*); *en tren* (not *por tren*); *nuestros* agreeing with *amigos*.
:::

::: q 6-7 3.1.2a 3.2.1e 4.7
**Sentence 3.** When I was little, I used to play football every day, but now I prefer to swim.

(2)
---
Model: *Cuando era pequeño (pequeña), jugaba al fútbol todos los días, pero ahora prefiero nadar.*

Meaning (1): all three elements conveyed: when I was little | I used to play football every day | but now I prefer to swim.

Accuracy (1): the imperfect *era* and *jugaba* for 'was' and 'used to'; *jugar a* + *el* becoming *al fútbol*; *todos los días*; the stem change *prefiero*; the infinitive *nadar*; the accent on *fútbol*.
:::

::: q 6-7 3.1.1a 3.2.1f 3.2.1i 4.7
**Sentence 4.** Tomorrow, if it is sunny, I am going to visit my grandparents, who live near the beach.

(2)
---
Model: *Mañana, si hace sol, voy a visitar a mis abuelos, que viven cerca de la playa.*

Meaning (1): all three elements conveyed: tomorrow, if it is sunny | I am going to visit my grandparents | who live near the beach.

Accuracy (1): *hace sol* (not *es soleado*); *voy a* + infinitive (or the future *visitaré*); the personal *a* before *mis abuelos*; the relative *que*; *cerca de la playa*.
:::

::: q 8-9 3.1.1c 3.2.2c 3.2.2d 4.7
**Sentence 5.** I have been learning Spanish for four years and, when I am older, I would like to work in Spain.

(2)
---
Model: *Aprendo español desde hace cuatro años y, cuando sea mayor, me gustaría trabajar en España.* (Also accept *Llevo cuatro años aprendiendo español* and *Estudio español desde hace cuatro años*.)

Meaning (1): all three elements conveyed: I have been learning Spanish for four years | when I am older | I would like to work in Spain.

Accuracy (1): the present tense with *desde hace* (not *he aprendido ... por*); the subjunctive *sea* after *cuando* referring to the future (not *soy* or *seré*); the conditional *me gustaría* + infinitive; *español* without a capital.
:::

::: q 8-9 3.1.2a 3.1.1c 3.2.1d 3.2.1f 4.7
**Question 2.** Answer **either** 2.1 **or** 2.2. Write approximately 90 words in Spanish. You must write something about each bullet point.

**2.1** You are writing a blog post about your free time. Mention:
- what you usually do at weekends
- a film or concert you saw recently
- your plans for next weekend.

**2.2** You are writing to your Spanish friend about work. Mention:
- the jobs you do to help at home
- how you earned some money recently
- the job you would like in the future.

(15)
---
Marked on the whole answer, with no need for the bullets to be covered equally. Content (AO2) is out of 10 and language (AO3) out of 5.

**Content (10).**
- **9 to 10:** all three bullets covered; clear throughout; ideas regularly developed, with a lot of relevant information.
- **7 to 8:** all three covered; mostly clear, with the odd lapse; ideas often developed, quite a lot of information.
- **5 to 6:** at least two bullets covered; generally clear but with lapses; a few ideas developed.
- **3 to 4:** at least one bullet covered; clear only some of the time; little relevant information.
- **1 to 2:** at least one bullet covered; often unclear; very little information.

**Language (5).**
- **5:** a good variety of vocabulary; regular attempts at complex language; past, present and future all used, mainly successfully; errors mainly minor.
- **4:** some variety; some complex language; at least two time frames, mainly successful; errors mainly minor.
- **3:** some variety; occasional complex language; two time frames attempted, not always successfully; more accurate than inaccurate.
- **2:** limited vocabulary; mainly simple sentences with some linking words; time frames may not be successful; generally inaccurate.
- **1:** narrow, repetitive vocabulary; short simple sentences; no successful time frames; highly inaccurate.

A mark of 0 for content means 0 for language.

**Model answer, 2.1 (about 90 words, top band):**

*Normalmente, los fines de semana quedo con mis amigos en el centro o juego al baloncesto en el parque. Me encanta porque me ayuda a olvidar el estrés del instituto.*

*El sábado pasado vi una película de acción en el cine con mi hermano. Fue muy emocionante, aunque el final fue un poco triste.*

*El fin de semana próximo voy a ir a la costa con mi familia. Si hace buen tiempo, nadaremos en el mar y comeremos en un restaurante. ¡Tengo muchas ganas!*

It covers each bullet in its own paragraph with a reason or detail. It uses the present, the preterite (*vi*, *fue*) and the future (*voy a ir*, *nadaremos*, *comeremos*), with a *si* clause and varied linking words.

For 2.2, the same pattern works. Present: *ayudo a mi madre a limpiar la cocina*. Past: *el verano pasado trabajé de camarero en un café y gané mucho dinero*. Future or conditional: *me gustaría ser periodista porque ...*.
:::

::: q 8-9 3.1.3c 3.1.2c 3.2.2c 3.2.2d 4.7
**Question 3.** Answer **either** 3.1 **or** 3.2. Write approximately 150 words in Spanish. You must write something about both bullet points.

**3.1** You are writing an article for a Spanish website about the environment where you live. Mention:
- what you and your family have done recently to protect the environment
- how your town or area should change in the future.

**3.2** You are writing an article about famous people. Mention:
- a famous person who has influenced you, and why
- whether you would like to be famous in the future.

(25)
---
Marked on the whole answer. Content (AO2) is out of 15. Language (AO3) is out of 10: 5 for range and 5 for accuracy. If only one bullet is covered, content is capped at 12. Everything written is judged for language, even any part that goes off the bullets.

**Content (15).**
- **13 to 15:** a lot of relevant information; ideas, accounts and descriptions regularly and successfully developed; clear, with very few or no lapses.
- **10 to 12:** quite a lot of information; regular attempts at development, mostly successful; mostly clear, with a few lapses.
- **7 to 9:** an adequate amount of information; some successful development; usually clear, with some lapses.
- **4 to 6:** some information; a little development; sometimes unclear.
- **1 to 3:** limited information; very little or no development; often unclear.

**Range of language (5).**
- **5:** a very good variety of vocabulary and structures; complex language regularly attempted and often successful.
- **4:** a good variety; complex language regularly attempted and generally successful.
- **3:** some variety; complex language occasionally attempted and sometimes successful.
- **2:** little variety; many short, simple sentences but also longer ones with linking words.
- **1:** very little variety; mainly short, simple sentences.

**Accuracy (5).**
- **5:** usually accurate; the odd major error and some minor ones, mostly in complex structures; verbs and tenses secure.
- **4:** generally accurate; several errors, mostly in complex structures; verbs generally correct.
- **3:** reasonably accurate; errors in both simple and complex language; verbs sometimes correct.
- **2:** more inaccurate than accurate; verbs often wrong.
- **1:** mostly inaccurate; errors in every sentence; verbs nearly always wrong.

A mark of 0 for content means 0 for language.

**Model paragraph, 3.1, first bullet (top band):**

*En mi casa intentamos proteger el medio ambiente todos los días. Reciclamos el plástico y el papel, y ya no compramos botellas de agua. El mes pasado, mi hermana y yo recogimos la basura de la playa con un grupo del instituto, y lo que más me sorprendió fue la cantidad de plástico que encontramos. Lo peor es que mucha gente todavía tira basura en la calle. Por eso, cuando sea mayor, quiero trabajar para que mi ciudad sea más limpia.*

It is developed (what the family does, a past account, an opinion, a consequence) and full of complex language that is on the list: *ya no*, *lo que*, *lo peor*, and the subjunctive after *cuando* and *para que*. A second paragraph on the future of the town would use the future (*habrá más zonas verdes*), the conditional (*sería mejor ...*) and *deberíamos* + infinitive.

For 3.2: name the person and say what they do (present), how they influenced you (preterite and imperfect: *cuando tenía doce años vi ...*), and then give a balanced view of fame, with *lo bueno ... lo malo ...*, the conditional and *cuando sea mayor*.
:::
