**20.** $f(x) = 3x - 2$ and $g(x) = x^2 + 1$.

(a) Find $fg(x)$, simplifying your answer. (2)

(b) Find $f^{-1}(x)$. (2)

(c) Solve $gf(x) = 10$. (3)

(7)
---
(a) $f(x^2 + 1) = 3(x^2 + 1) - 2$ (1) $= 3x^2 + 1$ (1).

(b) $y = 3x - 2 \Rightarrow x = \dfrac{y + 2}{3}$ (1); $f^{-1}(x) = \dfrac{x + 2}{3}$ (1).

(c) $(3x - 2)^2 + 1 = 10$, so $(3x - 2)^2 = 9$ (1); $3x - 2 = \pm 3$ (1); $x = \dfrac{5}{3}$ or $x = -\dfrac{1}{3}$ (1).
:::

::: q 8-9
**21.** Prove that the difference between the squares of any two consecutive odd numbers is a multiple of 8. (3)
---
Let the numbers be $2n + 1$ and $2n + 3$ (1). $(2n + 3)^2 - (2n + 1)^2 = 4n^2 + 12n + 9 - 4n^2 - 4n - 1 = 8n + 8$ (1) $= 8(n + 1)$, a multiple of 8 for any integer $n$ (1).
:::

::: q 8-9
**22.** The curve $y = x^3 - 6x^2 + 9x + 2$ has two stationary points. Find their coordinates and determine the nature of each. (5)
---
$\dfrac{dy}{dx} = 3x^2 - 12x + 9$ (1); $3(x - 1)(x - 3) = 0$, $x = 1$ or $3$ (1); points $(1, 6)$ and $(3, 2)$ (1); $\dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2} = 6x - 12$: negative at $x = 1$ so maximum (1); positive at $x = 3$ so minimum (1).
:::
