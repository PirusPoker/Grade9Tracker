**18.** In triangle $OAB$, $\overrightarrow{OA} = \mathbf{a}$ and $\overrightarrow{OB} = \mathbf{b}$. $M$ is the midpoint of $OA$ and $N$ is the midpoint of $AB$.

Prove that $MN$ is parallel to $OB$ and that $MN = \dfrac{1}{2}OB$. (4)
---
$\overrightarrow{OM} = \dfrac{1}{2}\mathbf{a}$ (1); $\overrightarrow{ON} = \mathbf{a} + \dfrac{1}{2}(\mathbf{b} - \mathbf{a}) = \dfrac{1}{2}\mathbf{a} + \dfrac{1}{2}\mathbf{b}$ (1); $\overrightarrow{MN} = \overrightarrow{ON} - \overrightarrow{OM} = \dfrac{1}{2}\mathbf{b}$ (1); this is a scalar multiple of $\mathbf{b}$, so $MN$ is parallel to $OB$, and its length is half of $OB$ (1).
:::
