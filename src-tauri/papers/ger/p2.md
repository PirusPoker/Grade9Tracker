::: q 4-5 3.1.1a 3.2.1g 4.7
**Question 1: translate the following sentences into German.**

**Sentence 1.** *My brother is three years younger than me and he often plays football.*

(2)
---
Model: **Mein Bruder ist drei Jahre jünger als ich, und er spielt oft Fußball.**

1 mark for meaning if all three elements are conveyed:
- my brother is three years younger (*Mein Bruder ist drei Jahre jünger*);
- than me (*als ich*);
- he often plays football (*er spielt oft Fußball*).

1 mark for accurate language:
- the comparative *jünger* with its umlaut;
- the nominative *als ich*, not *als mich*;
- *spielt* with a -t ending;
- capitals on *Bruder*, *Jahre* and *Fußball*.

*Er spielt Fußball oft* is understandable but unidiomatic, so it gets the meaning mark only.
:::

::: q 4-5 3.1.2a 3.2.1e 4.7
**Sentence 2.** *Last Saturday we went into town with our parents.*

(2)
---
Model: **Letzten Samstag sind wir mit unseren Eltern in die Stadt gefahren.** *gegangen* is equally correct.

1 mark for meaning if all three elements are conveyed:
- last Saturday (*Letzten Samstag*);
- we went into town (*sind wir ... in die Stadt gefahren / gegangen*);
- with our parents (*mit unseren Eltern*).

1 mark for accurate language:
- inversion after the time phrase: *Letzten Samstag sind wir*, not *Letzten Samstag wir sind*;
- *sein* as the auxiliary;
- the dative plural *mit unseren Eltern*;
- the accusative of movement *in die Stadt*.

The commonest error is *haben* with *gefahren*.
:::

::: q 6-7 3.1.1c 3.2.2b 4.7
**Sentence 3.** *I have been learning German for five years, because I would like to live in Germany.*

(2)
---
Model: **Ich lerne seit fünf Jahren Deutsch, weil ich in Deutschland wohnen / leben möchte.**

1 mark for meaning if all three elements are conveyed:
- I have been learning German (*Ich lerne ... Deutsch*);
- for five years (*seit fünf Jahren*);
- because I would like to live in Germany (*weil ich in Deutschland wohnen möchte*).

1 mark for accurate language:
- *seit* + **present** tense (*Ich habe seit fünf Jahren Deutsch gelernt* is wrong in meaning);
- the dative plural *Jahren*;
- *möchte* at the very end of the *weil* clause, after the infinitive.
:::

::: q 6-7 3.1.3c 3.2.2c 3.2.1d 4.7
**Sentence 4.** *We should use the car less, because the air in our town is dirty.*

(2)
---
Model: **Wir sollten das Auto weniger benutzen, weil die Luft in unserer Stadt schmutzig ist.**

1 mark for meaning if all three elements are conveyed:
- we should use the car less (*Wir sollten das Auto weniger benutzen*);
- because the air (*weil die Luft*);
- in our town is dirty (*in unserer Stadt schmutzig ist*).

1 mark for accurate language:
- *sollten* (plural, 'should') + an infinitive at the end;
- *ist* at the end of the *weil* clause;
- the dative feminine *in unserer Stadt*.

*Wir sollen* means 'we are supposed to'. It is acceptable for meaning, but it is not the Higher structure.
:::

::: q 8-9 3.1.3a 3.2.2c 4.7
**Sentence 5.** *If I were rich, I would buy a house by the sea for my whole family.*

(2)
---
Model: **Wenn ich reich wäre, würde ich für meine ganze Familie ein Haus am Meer kaufen.** *ein Haus am Meer für meine ganze Familie* is equally correct.

1 mark for meaning if all three elements are conveyed:
- if I were rich (*Wenn ich reich wäre*);
- I would buy a house by the sea (*würde ich ein Haus am Meer kaufen*);
- for my whole family (*für meine ganze Familie*).

1 mark for accurate language:
- *wäre* at the end of the *wenn* clause, with its umlaut;
- verb, comma, verb: *wäre, würde ich*;
- *kaufen* at the end;
- the accusative *für meine ganze Familie*;
- *am Meer*.

*Wenn ich reich war* ('when I was rich') changes the meaning and loses the language mark.
:::

**Question 2.** Answer **either** Question 2.1 **or** Question 2.2. Write approximately **90 words** in German. You must write something about each bullet point.

**2.1** You are writing an email to your German friend about healthy living. Mention:
- what you eat and drink in a normal week
- something healthy you did recently
- how you will stay healthy in the future.

**2.2** You are writing a blog post about celebrations. Mention:
- how your family usually celebrates birthdays
- a recent celebration you enjoyed
- a festival you would like to see in a German-speaking country.

(15)
---
**Content (10 marks).** Judge the whole answer and use all parts of each descriptor, not just the number of bullets covered.

| Marks | What earns it |
|---|---|
| 9-10 | All three bullets covered; communication clear; ideas regularly developed, with a lot of relevant information. |
| 7-8 | All three bullets covered; mostly clear with occasional lapses; ideas often developed, with quite a lot of information. |
| 5-6 | At least two bullets covered; generally clear with likely lapses; a few ideas developed and some relevant information. |
| 3-4 | At least one bullet covered; sometimes clear with regular lapses; little relevant information. |
| 1-2 | At least one bullet covered; often unclear; very little relevant information. |

**Language (5 marks).** A zero for content means zero here.

| Mark | What earns it |
|---|---|
| 5 | Good variety of vocabulary; regular attempts at complex language; all three time frames referenced and mainly successful; errors mainly minor, with some major ones possible in complex sentences. |
| 4 | A variety of vocabulary; some attempts at complexity; at least two time frames, mainly successful; errors mainly minor. |
| 3 | Some variety; occasional complexity; at least two time frames, not always successful; more accurate than inaccurate. |
| 2 | Limited variety; mainly simple sentences, perhaps some linked with connectives; time frames may not succeed; generally inaccurate. |
| 1 | Narrow, repetitive language; short or badly built sentences; no successful time frames; highly inaccurate. |

**Model answer (2.1, top band):** *Hallo Jonas! In einer normalen Woche esse ich viel Obst und Gemüse, aber am Wochenende esse ich leider oft Pommes. Ich trinke jeden Tag viel Wasser, weil ich mich dann besser konzentrieren kann. Letzte Woche bin ich mit meiner Schwester zehn Kilometer gelaufen. Das war anstrengend, aber danach habe ich mich super gefühlt. In Zukunft werde ich früher ins Bett gehen, weil ich morgens immer müde bin. Außerdem möchte ich weniger Zucker essen. Wenn ich mehr Zeit hätte, würde ich jeden Tag Sport treiben. Bis bald, Alex*

Why it scores top marks:
- It covers all three bullets and develops each one with a reason or detail.
- It uses the present, the perfect (with *sein* and a reflexive) and the future, plus a *wenn ... hätte, würde* conditional.
- It includes two-verb *weil* clauses and a modal.

For 2.2, the same structure works: a present-tense routine with a reason, a past-tense account with an opinion, and *möchte* / *würde gern* plus a *weil* clause for the future festival.

**Question 3.** Answer **either** Question 3.1 **or** Question 3.2. Write approximately **150 words** in German. You must write something about both bullet points.

**3.1** You are writing an article for a German website about school and the future. Describe:
- a day when something went wrong at school
- your plans for your future career, and why.

**3.2** You are writing an article for a German magazine about technology. Describe:
- a time when technology caused a problem for you
- whether young people spend too much time online, and what should change.

(25)
---
**Content (15 marks).** If only one bullet is covered, the maximum is 12.

| Marks | What earns it |
|---|---|
| 13-15 | A lot of information relevant to the task; regular successful development of ideas, accounts or description; clear, with very few or no lapses. |
| 10-12 | Quite a lot of information; regular attempts at development, mostly successful; mostly clear with a few lapses. |
| 7-9 | An adequate amount of information; some successful development; usually clear with some lapses. |
| 4-6 | Some information; a little development; sometimes unclear, with regular lapses. |
| 1-3 | Limited information; very little or no development; often unclear. |

**Range of language (5 marks).**

| Mark | What earns it |
|---|---|
| 5 | Very good variety of vocabulary and structures; complex language regularly attempted and often successful. |
| 4 | Good variety; complex language regularly attempted and generally successful. |
| 3 | Some variety; complex language occasionally attempted and sometimes successful. |
| 2 | Little variety; short simple structures often, but regular longer sentences with linking words. |
| 1 | Very little variety; mainly short, simple structures. |

**Accuracy (5 marks).** A zero for content means zero for range and accuracy.

| Mark | What earns it |
|---|---|
| 5 | Usually accurate, with occasional major and some minor errors, especially in complex structures; verbs and tenses secure. |
| 4 | Generally accurate, with several errors, usually in complex structures; verbs and tenses generally correct. |
| 3 | Reasonably accurate, with errors in both simple and complex language; verbs sometimes correct. |
| 2 | More inaccurate than accurate; verbs often wrong. |
| 1 | Mostly inaccurate, with errors in every sentence; verbs nearly always wrong. |

**Model paragraph (3.1, both bullets, top band):** *Letzten Montag hatte ich einen schrecklichen Tag. Zuerst kam der Bus zu spät, also musste ich zur Schule laufen. In der ersten Stunde schrieben wir eine wichtige Prüfung in Chemie, aber ich war so müde, dass ich viele Antworten nicht wusste. Meine Lehrerin war nicht zufrieden, und ich fühlte mich schrecklich. In Zukunft möchte ich Tierarzt werden, weil ich Tiere liebe und anderen helfen will. Deshalb werde ich nächstes Jahr in der Oberstufe Biologie und Chemie lernen. Wenn ich die Noten für das Studium nicht bekomme, werde ich trotzdem mit Tieren arbeiten.*

What the model shows:
- The account uses the simple past, which is allowed in Higher written narrative (*kam, musste, schrieben, wusste, fühlte*).
- It has *so ... dass*, a two-verb *weil* clause, the future with *werden* and a *wenn* clause.
- A full answer would add another 40 to 50 words developing the second bullet: why the career appeals, what it involves, and an opinion with *hätte/wäre/würde*.

For 3.2, narrate the problem in the past (e.g. a broken laptop before a deadline), then give a balanced opinion with *man sollte ...*, *seit* + present and a conditional.
