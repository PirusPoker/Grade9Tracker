**13.** $A$, $B$ and $C$ are points on a circle with centre $O$. The line $TA$ is the tangent to the circle at $A$. $C$ lies on the major arc $AB$, and angle $TAB = 64^\circ$, where $T$ is on the opposite side of $AB$ from $C$.

(4)

**22.** $OAB$ is a triangle with $\overrightarrow{OA} = \mathbf{a}$ and $\overrightarrow{OB} = \mathbf{b}$.

$M$ is the midpoint of $OA$. $P$ is the point on $AB$ such that $AP : PB = 2 : 1$. $R$ is the point such that $\overrightarrow{OR} = 2\mathbf{b}$.

(a) Write $\overrightarrow{AB}$ in terms of $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$. (1)

(b) Show that $\overrightarrow{OP} = \dfrac{1}{3}(\mathbf{a} + 2\mathbf{b})$. (2)

(c) Prove that $M$, $P$ and $R$ lie on a straight line. (3)

(6)
---
(a) $\mathbf{b} - \mathbf{a}$ (B1).

(b) $\overrightarrow{OP} = \overrightarrow{OA} + \dfrac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{AB} = \mathbf{a} + \dfrac{2}{3}(\mathbf{b} - \mathbf{a})$ (M1); $= \dfrac{1}{3}\mathbf{a} + \dfrac{2}{3}\mathbf{b} = \dfrac{1}{3}(\mathbf{a} + 2\mathbf{b})$ (A1).

(c) $\overrightarrow{MP} = -\dfrac{1}{2}\mathbf{a} + \dfrac{1}{3}\mathbf{a} + \dfrac{2}{3}\mathbf{b} = \dfrac{1}{6}(4\mathbf{b} - \mathbf{a})$ (M1); $\overrightarrow{MR} = -\dfrac{1}{2}\mathbf{a} + 2\mathbf{b} = \dfrac{1}{2}(4\mathbf{b} - \mathbf{a})$ (M1); $\overrightarrow{MR} = 3\overrightarrow{MP}$, so the vectors are parallel, and they share the point $M$, so $M$, $P$ and $R$ are collinear (A1). (The final mark needs both "parallel" (or a multiple) and the common point.)

**25.** A bag contains $n$ counters. 3 of the counters are red. Two counters are taken at random from the bag without replacement.

The probability that both counters are red is $\dfrac{1}{15}$.

(a) Show that $n^2 - n - 90 = 0$. (3)

(b) Find the value of $n$. (2)

(5)
---
(a) $\dfrac{3}{n} \times \dfrac{2}{n - 1}$ (M1); $\dfrac{6}{n(n - 1)} = \dfrac{1}{15}$ (M1); $n(n - 1) = 90$, so $n^2 - n - 90 = 0$ (A1), with no errors seen.

(b) $(n - 10)(n + 9) = 0$ (M1); $n = 10$, rejecting $n = -9$ because a number of counters cannot be negative (A1).
