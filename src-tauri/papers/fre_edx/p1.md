Ma mère vit en Belgique (*Belgium*), alors je la vois seulement pendant les vacances. Je m'entends bien avec mon père, car il est toujours prêt à m'écouter. (a) Write the correct letter for each question.

(i) Inès lives with: **A** her mother **B** her father and half-brothers **C** her mother and half-brothers. (1)

(ii) Inès sees her mother: **A** every weekend **B** only during the holidays **C** never. (1)

(iii) Inès gets on well with her father because he: **A** is always ready to listen **B** gives her money **C** makes her laugh. (1)

**Section B**

**9.** Translate this passage into **English**.

> Ma tante habite au Canada depuis trois ans. Elle travaille dans un hôtel où elle parle souvent anglais avec les clients. L'été dernier, je suis allé la voir et nous avons fait du bateau sur un lac. Avant de partir, j'avais peur de prendre l'avion, mais le voyage s'est très bien passé. L'année prochaine, je voudrais y retourner avec mes parents.

(10)
---
Pearson marks the translation as a whole on one grid (best fit), not word by word. In the spirit of its grid:

- **9–10:** the whole meaning comes across naturally; any slips do not blur the message.
- **6–8:** most of the meaning comes across; occasional wrong or missing words, or awkward word order, blur a few details.
- **3–5:** parts of the meaning come across, but some errors (wrong tenses, missing phrases, wrong words) stop sections being understood.
- **1–2:** only scattered parts come across; frequent errors.
- **0:** nothing creditable.

**Model:** *My aunt has been living in Canada for three years. She works in a hotel where she often speaks English with the customers. Last summer I went to see her and we went boating on a lake. Before leaving, I was scared of flying / taking the plane, but the journey went very well. Next year I would like to go back there with my parents.*

**The details that separate 9–10 from 6–8:**
- *habite... depuis trois ans*: has been living / has lived for three years. "Lives in Canada since three years" or "lived" is a tense error.
- *où*: where; *souvent*: often (must not be dropped); *les clients*: customers / clients / guests.
- *je suis allé la voir*: I went to see her (*la* is her, not "the").
- *nous avons fait du bateau*: we went boating / went on a boat. Accept "we took a boat out".
- *Avant de partir*: before leaving / before going (not "after").
- *j'avais peur de prendre l'avion*: I was afraid / scared of flying / of taking the plane (imperfect: "was", not "am" or "have been").
- *s'est très bien passé*: went very well. "Passed very well" is a literal error that blurs meaning.
- *je voudrais y retourner*: I would like to go back / return there. "I want" is a slight loss; "I will" is wrong.
:::
