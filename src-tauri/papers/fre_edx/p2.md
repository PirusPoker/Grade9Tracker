**Question 1.** Answer **either** 1(a) **or** 1(b). Write your answer in French. You should aim to write between **80 and 90 words**.

**1(a)** Write to your friend about your family. You must include the following points:
- who is in your family
- your opinion of family celebrations, with reasons
- something you did with your family recently
- how you will spend time with your family next weekend.

**1(b)** Write to your friend about technology. You must include the following points:
- how you use your phone
- your opinion of social media, with reasons
- a film or series you watched recently
- what technology you will buy in the future.

(18)
---
Two grids, applied separately (wording paraphrased from the spirit of Pearson's criteria). Language errors count only in the language grid.

**Response to the task (13 marks)**
- **11–13:** all four bullets answered; ideas often developed (an extra clause or sentence on most bullets); easy to follow, with at most an occasional unclear message.
- **8–10:** at least three bullets answered; some development; generally clear, but some messages take a moment to understand.
- **5–7:** at least two bullets; brief, occasional development; the message sometimes breaks down.
- **1–4:** at least one bullet; little or no extra detail; often hard to follow.
- Bullets can be answered in any order and need not be equal. A bullet answered in the wrong time frame (for example "next weekend" in the past) does not count as covered.

**Language (5 marks)**
- **5:** some variety of vocabulary and structures, some longer sentences and the odd complex structure; past, present and future generally successful; mostly accurate, with minor slips and perhaps one major error.
- **3–4:** occasional variety and longer sentences; at least two time frames partly successful; some accurate language, minor errors plus some major ones.
- **1–2:** a narrow range of short, simple sentences; time frames rarely work; frequent errors.
- A minor error does not stop meaning (a wrong gender, a missing accent, *dans le France*); a major one does (a wrong verb form, the wrong tense, an infinitive instead of a conjugated verb).

**Model for 1(a) (about 85 words):** *Dans ma famille, il y a cinq personnes : mes parents, mes deux sœurs et moi. J'adore les fêtes de famille parce qu'on mange bien et qu'on rit beaucoup, mais elles sont parfois un peu longues. Le week-end dernier, nous sommes allés chez mes grands-parents pour l'anniversaire de mon grand-père. Après avoir mangé, nous avons joué aux cartes, et c'était très amusant. Samedi prochain, je vais faire du vélo avec mon père, et le soir, on regardera un film ensemble.*

Every bullet is answered and developed; three time frames; *après avoir* + past participle, the imperfect *c'était* and the future *regardera* lift the language mark.

**Ideas for 1(b):** *J'utilise mon portable pour parler avec mes amis et écouter de la musique.* Opinion with a reason and a counter-point: *Les réseaux sociaux sont utiles, mais on y passe trop de temps.* Past: *Hier soir, j'ai regardé une série française qui m'a beaucoup plu.* Future: *L'année prochaine, j'achèterai un ordinateur portable pour mes études.*

::: q 8-9 T3 T5 G2b G3c G3d P4
**Question 2.** Answer **either** 2(a) **or** 2(b). Write your answer in French. You should aim to write between **130 and 150 words**.

**2(a)** Write about the environment for your school website. You must include the following points:
- what makes a town good for the environment
- the pros and cons of cars in towns
- what you did recently to help the environment
- how you will protect the environment in the future.

**2(b)** Write about work for an online magazine. You must include the following points:
- what makes a good job
- the pros and cons of having a part-time job while at school
- any work experience you have done
- your plans for after your exams.

(22)
---
Two grids, applied separately (wording paraphrased from the spirit of Pearson's criteria). Language errors count only in the language grid.

**Response to the task (17 marks)**
- **14–17:** all four bullets answered; ideas developed consistently, with detail, reasons and examples on every bullet; easy to follow throughout.
- **11–13:** all four bullets answered; frequent development; clear, with the occasional unclear message.
- **8–10:** at least three bullets answered; some development; generally clear.
- **5–7:** at least two bullets; brief development; the message sometimes breaks down.
- **1–4:** at least one bullet; very little development; often unclear.
- The "pros and cons" bullet needs both sides: an answer that gives only advantages has not fully answered it.

**Language (5 marks)**
- **4–5:** a wide variety of vocabulary and structures; frequent longer and complex sentences; past, present and future consistently successful; consistently accurate, any errors minor.
- **2–3:** some variety and some complex language; at least two time frames generally successful; mostly minor errors with the occasional major one.
- **1:** occasional variety; time frames occasionally successful; frequent errors, some of them major.

**Model for 2(b) (about 145 words):** *À mon avis, un bon métier doit être intéressant et utile aux autres. Le salaire est important, bien sûr, mais il vaut mieux faire un travail qu'on aime.*

*Avoir un petit travail quand on est au lycée présente des avantages : on gagne de l'argent et on apprend à être responsable. D'un autre côté, il est difficile de trouver le temps de faire ses devoirs, et on risque d'être fatigué en classe.*

*L'année dernière, j'ai fait un stage de deux semaines dans une école primaire. J'aidais les enfants à lire et je préparais les activités de l'après-midi. Au début, j'étais stressé, mais les professeurs m'ont beaucoup encouragé.*

*Après mes examens, je vais continuer mes études au lycée. J'étudierai les langues, parce que je voudrais travailler à l'étranger. Si c'est possible, je passerai une année au Canada.*

One paragraph per bullet, each developed with a reason or an example. Higher structures: *il vaut mieux* + infinitive, *il est difficile de*, an infinitive as the subject (*Avoir un petit travail...*), imperfect for repeated past actions, the future *étudierai* and *passerai*, and *si* + present + future.

**Ideas for 2(a):** a good town has *des pistes cyclables, des bus électriques et des espaces verts*; cars are *pratiques pour les personnes âgées* but *causent de la pollution et des embouteillages*; recently *j'ai ramassé des déchets sur la plage avec ma classe*; in future *je prendrai le train au lieu de l'avion et je recyclerai tout*.

**Question 3.** Translate the paragraph into **French**. (The real paper prints it as one paragraph; the six sentences are marked one per block here.)

> I love going to the beach. My brother prefers the mountains. Last summer, we stayed in a small hotel near the sea. Every morning, my parents used to have breakfast in the garden. I have been learning French for four years, so I was able to help them. Next year, I will go to Canada with my friends and it will be fantastic.

**Sentence 1.** I love going to the beach.

(1)
---
**Model:** *J'adore aller à la plage.* Accept *J'aime beaucoup aller à la plage*.

1 mark if the meaning is complete and there is no major error. The infinitive *aller* after *adorer* (not *allant*, not *à aller*) and *à la* are the points to check.
:::

::: q 8-9 T6 G3a P4
**Sentence 2.** My brother prefers the mountains.

(1)
---
**Model:** *Mon frère préfère la montagne.* Accept *les montagnes*.

1 mark for complete meaning with no major error: *préfère* (é in the infinitive, è in the stem before a silent ending) and the definite article *la* or *les*.
:::

::: q 8-9 T6 G3b G6 P4
**Sentence 3.** Last summer, we stayed in a small hotel near the sea.

(2)
---
**Model:** *L'été dernier, nous sommes restés dans un petit hôtel près de la mer.* Accept *on est restés / on est resté*.

**Meaning (1):** last summer + we stayed + small hotel + near the sea all conveyed.

**Accuracy (1):** *rester* takes **être**, and the participle agrees (*restés*); *petit* before the noun; *près de la* (not *près la* or *près du mer*).
:::

::: q 8-9 T6 T1 G3c P4
**Sentence 4.** Every morning, my parents used to have breakfast in the garden.

(2)
---
**Model:** *Tous les matins, mes parents prenaient le petit-déjeuner dans le jardin.* Accept *Chaque matin*.

**Meaning (1):** every morning + used to have breakfast + in the garden.

**Accuracy (1):** the **imperfect** plural *prenaient* ("used to" = imperfect; *ont pris* or *prennent* loses this mark); *tous les matins* with no preposition.
:::

::: q 8-9 T5 G3a G1c G3b P4
**Sentence 5.** I have been learning French for four years, so I was able to help them.

(2)
---
**Model:** *J'apprends le français depuis quatre ans, donc j'ai pu les aider.* Accept *alors* for *donc*, and *j'étudie* for *j'apprends*.

**Meaning (1):** have been learning + for four years + so + I was able to help them.

**Accuracy (1):** **present tense + depuis** (*j'ai appris... depuis* is a major error); *le français* with a small f and the article; the pronoun *les* in front of the infinitive *aider* (*j'ai pu aider eux* is a major error).
:::

::: q 8-9 T6 G3d G6 P4
**Sentence 6.** Next year, I will go to Canada with my friends and it will be fantastic.

(2)
---
**Model:** *L'année prochaine, j'irai au Canada avec mes amis, et ce sera fantastique.* Accept *je vais aller* and *ça va être*.

**Meaning (1):** next year + I will go + to Canada + with my friends + it will be fantastic.

**Accuracy (1):** a correct future (*irai*, not *allerai*; *sera*, not *serai*); **au** Canada because Canada is masculine. *En Canada* or *à Canada* is only a minor error, so the mark can still be given if the verbs are right.

**Whole-paragraph check (Pearson's grids):** meaning 5–6 when nearly every sentence is fully conveyed, 3–4 when most is, 1–2 when only parts are; accuracy 4 when errors are only minor, 3 when major errors are rare, 2 with some major errors, 1 with frequent errors. Your block total out of 10 maps closely onto that.
:::
