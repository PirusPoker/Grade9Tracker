intro: Pearson Edexcel GCSE Spanish 1SP1 Higher, Paper 3 style: 60 minutes, no dictionary. Section A (40 marks) is eight questions on Spanish texts, set in English and answered in English: choose a letter, complete a gap from a box, or write a short answer. Two words in the paper are not on the vocabulary list; work them out from the context. Section B (10 marks) is a paragraph to translate into English, marked as a whole: spend about 10 minutes on it. Give exactly the number of details asked for, and check the time frame and any negatives before you answer.

**Section A**

**1.** Read Hugo's description of his best friend.

> Conozco a Iván desde hace ocho años. Vive en el piso de arriba con su madre y su abuelo. Somos muy diferentes: a él le encanta leer, mientras que yo prefiero el deporte. Los domingos jugamos al baloncesto en el parque y después comemos en casa de su abuelo, que cocina muy bien.

Complete each sentence with a word from the box. There are more words than gaps.

> grandfather · uncle · cousin · reading · cooking · basketball · football

(a) Iván lives with his mother and his .......... (1)

(b) Iván loves .......... (1)

(c) On Sundays, Hugo and Iván play .......... (1)

(3)
---
(a) grandfather (1).

(b) reading (1). Not 'cooking': it is the grandfather who cooks well.

(c) basketball (1).

::: q 4-5 T2 G7 P3
**2.** Read Sara's post about her brother.

> Hace un año, mi hermano Daniel pasaba demasiadas horas delante de la pantalla y dormía muy poco. Siempre estaba cansado y de mal humor. Un día, el médico le dijo que debía apagar el móvil una hora antes de acostarse. Al principio le pareció imposible, pero ahora lee una novela en la cama y se duerme rápidamente. Además, ya no coge el autobús para ir al instituto: ahora va en bicicleta. Mi madre dice que es otra persona: más tranquilo, más sano y mucho menos gruñón.

(a) Choose the correct ending for each sentence. Write the letter.

(i) A year ago, Daniel … A slept too much. B was always tired. C never did his homework. (1)

(ii) The doctor told him to … A switch his phone off an hour before bed. B go to bed an hour earlier. C stop reading in bed. (1)

(iii) Now, Daniel … A cannot get to sleep. B watches series in bed. C reads before he goes to sleep. (1)

(iv) Daniel now goes to school … A by bus. B by bike. C on foot. (1)

(b) Which of these is the best translation of the word **gruñón**? A grumpy B hungry C sleepy (1)

(5)
---
(a)(i) B (1): *siempre estaba cansado*. A is the opposite: he slept very little.

(ii) A (1).

(iii) C (1): *lee una novela en la cama y se duerme rápidamente*.

(iv) B (1). Not A: *ya no coge el autobús* means he no longer takes the bus.

(b) A (1). Clues: earlier he was *de mal humor* (in a bad mood), and *menos gruñón* sits beside *más tranquilo*, so it is a bad-tempered quality that has improved.

**3.** Read this review of a new series and its main actress.

> La nueva serie *Calle Mayor* cuenta la historia de una familia que abre una tienda de ropa en un pueblo de Ecuador. Cada episodio dura veinte minutos y es muy divertido. Lo mejor es la música: todos los episodios terminan con una canción tradicional. Si te gustan las historias de familia, ¡tienes que verla!

(a) Complete each sentence with a word from the box. There are more words than gaps.

> shop · restaurant · funny · sad · song · dance · film

(i) The family in the series opens a .......... (1)

(ii) Each episode is very .......... (1)

(iii) Every episode ends with a .......... (1)

The review continues.

> La actriz principal, Natalia, tiene solo diecisiete años. Antes de esta serie actuaba en el teatro de su ciudad, pero no era famosa. Ahora tiene más de un millón de seguidores, aunque casi no usa las redes sociales porque prefiere pasar tiempo con sus amigos. En el futuro, quiere hacer su propia película.

(b) Choose the correct ending for each sentence. Write the letter.

(i) Before this series, Natalia … A acted in a theatre. B was already famous. C worked in a shop. (1)

(ii) Natalia … A posts on social media every day. B has more than a million followers. C has few friends. (1)

(c) Answer in English. You do not need to write in full sentences.

(i) Why does Natalia hardly use social media? (1)

(ii) What does she want to do in the future? (1)

(7)
---
(a)(i) shop (1). (ii) funny (1). (iii) song (1).

(b)(i) A (1): *actuaba en el teatro de su ciudad*. Not B: *no era famosa*.

(ii) B (1). Not A: *casi no usa las redes sociales* (she hardly uses them).

(c)(i) She prefers to spend time with her friends (1).

(ii) Make her own film (1). Accept 'make a film of her own'. Reject 'act in a film', which is not what she says.

**4.** Read this post on a holiday forum.

> El verano pasado, mi novio y yo alquilamos una casa en las montañas de Asturias* porque queríamos descansar lejos del ruido de la ciudad. Los primeros días fueron perfectos: paseamos por el bosque y nos bañamos en el río. Sin embargo, el último día hubo mucho viento y lluvia, y no había luz en toda la casa. Pasamos la noche entera jugando a juegos de mesa con una linterna.
>
> *Asturias: a region in the north of Spain

(a) Answer in English. You do not need to write in full sentences.

(i) Why did they rent a house in the mountains? (1)

(ii) What did they do on the first days? (1 detail) (1)

(b) Which of these is the best translation of the word **linterna**? A torch B blanket C radio (1)

(3)
---
(a)(i) To rest / relax away from the noise of the city (1). 'To rest' alone is enough; 'for a holiday' is not.

(ii) Any one (1): they walked in the forest / woods; they swam / bathed in the river.

(b) A (1). Clues: there was no light (*no había luz*) and they played games all night, so they needed something that gives light.

**5.** Read Paula's and Jorge's posts about their future plans.

(a) What does Paula say?

> Este año termino el instituto y todavía no sé qué hacer. Mis profesores dicen que debería ir a la universidad para estudiar Medicina, pero a mí me interesa más la cocina. Mi tía tiene un restaurante en Barcelona y me ha ofrecido un trabajo para el próximo verano. Antes de decidir, quiero hablar con mis padres, que siempre me dan buenos consejos.

Write the letters of the **three** correct statements.

A Paula is finishing school this year.
B Her teachers think she should go to university.
C Paula wants to study medicine.
D Her aunt has offered her a job.
E Paula has already accepted the job.
F Her parents never give her advice.

(3 marks)

(b) What does Jorge say?

> Nunca me ha gustado estudiar, así que el año que viene voy a hacer un curso de formación profesional para ser electricista. Lo bueno de este trabajo es que nunca es aburrido, porque cada día estás en un sitio diferente. Ahora mismo trabajo los sábados en un supermercado para ganar un poco de dinero. Algún día me gustaría tener mi propia empresa.

Complete the table in English. You do not need to write in full sentences.

(i) What he has never liked (1)

(ii) Why the job is never boring (1)

(iii) His current situation (1 detail) (1)

(iv) His plan for one day in the future (1)

(7)
---
(a) A, B, D, in any order (1 each, 3). C is wrong: medicine is the teachers' idea and she is more interested in cooking. E is wrong: she wants to talk to her parents before deciding. F is the opposite of what she says.

(b)(i) Studying (1).

(ii) Every day you are in a different place (1).

(iii) Any one (1): he works on Saturdays in a supermarket; he works to earn a bit of money.

**6.** Read Lola's article about her neighbourhood.

> Mi barrio está en las afueras de Valencia. Hace diez años era un lugar bastante sucio y peligroso, pero ahora es muy diferente. Han construido un parque enorme donde antes no había nada, y se puede ir en bicicleta por todas las calles principales.

(a) Write the letters of the **two** correct statements.

A Lola lives in the centre of Valencia.
B Her area used to be dangerous.
C The new park is quite small.
D You can now cycle along all the main streets.

(2 marks)

The article continues.

> Sin embargo, todavía hay problemas. Por la noche, los jóvenes hacen mucho ruido en la plaza y los vecinos no pueden dormir. Además, mucha gente sigue usando el coche para ir a sitios que están muy cerca. Yo creo que el transporte público debería ser gratuito para los menores de dieciocho años. Así habría menos tráfico y el aire estaría más limpio.

(b) Complete the table in English. You do not need to write in full sentences.

(i) The problem at night (1 detail) (1)

(ii) What many people still do (1)

(iii) What Lola suggests (1)

(5)
---
(a) B and D, in either order (1 each, 2). A is wrong: she lives on the outskirts (*las afueras*). C is wrong: the park is enormous.

(b)(i) Any one (1): young people make a lot of noise in the square; the neighbours cannot sleep.

(ii) They (still) use the car to go to places that are very close / for short journeys (1). 'They use the car' alone is not enough: the point is the short distances. *Sigue usando* = still uses.

(iii) Free public transport for under-18s (1). Both 'free' and 'under-18s' (or 'young people') are needed.

> **la ingeniería: engineering

(a) What did many girls do at the age of fourteen at that time? (1)

(b) What did everyone tell Dolores about engineering? (1)

**Section B**

**9.** Translate this paragraph into English.

> Me llamo Elena y vivo en Madrid desde hace tres años. Antes vivíamos en un pueblo pequeño donde no había mucho que hacer. Lo que más me gusta de la ciudad es que puedo ir a pie a todas partes. Ayer fui al cine con mi hermano menor y nos divertimos mucho. Cuando sea mayor, me gustaría vivir en el extranjero, aunque será difícil estar lejos de mi familia.

(10)
---
Marked as a whole, by best fit (the examiner's own grid works the same way):

- **9–10:** the full meaning comes across in natural English; any slips do not affect the message.
- **6–8:** most of the meaning comes across; the odd word is wrong or missing, or the English is a little awkward.
- **3–5:** some sentences are right, but wrong words, omissions or wrong tenses blur the message in places.
- **1–2:** only scattered phrases are right; frequent errors stop the message coming across.
- **0:** nothing creditworthy.

**Model translation:** My name is Elena and I have been living in Madrid for three years. Before, we used to live in a small village where there was not much to do. What I like most about the city is that I can walk everywhere. Yesterday I went to the cinema with my younger brother and we had a great time. When I am older, I would like to live abroad, although it will be difficult to be far from my family.

**The ten points to check** (each one missed or wrong costs about a mark):

1. *vivo … desde hace tres años* = I have been living … for three years (not 'I live … since three years').
2. *Antes vivíamos* = Before, we used to live / we lived (imperfect, and 'we', not 'I').
3. *donde no había mucho que hacer* = where there was not much to do.
4. *Lo que más me gusta* = What I like most.
5. *puedo ir a pie a todas partes* = I can walk / go on foot everywhere.
6. *Ayer fui* = Yesterday I went (preterite of *ir*, not 'I was').
7. *mi hermano menor* = my younger brother (not 'my little brother's' or 'my older brother').
8. *nos divertimos mucho* = we had a great time / we really enjoyed ourselves.
9. *Cuando sea mayor, me gustaría* = When I am older, I would like (the subjunctive *sea* is translated by an English present).
10. *aunque será difícil estar lejos de* = although it will be difficult to be far from.
:::
