intro: Pearson Edexcel GCSE Spanish 1SP1 Higher, Paper 4 style: 1 hour 20 minutes, no dictionary, answer all three questions. Question 1 (18 marks, about 80–90 words) and Question 2 (22 marks, about 130–150 words) each offer a choice of two tasks: answer one task only, write in full sentences, and cover all four bullet points in the past, present and future. Question 3 (10 marks) is a short paragraph to translate into Spanish. Pearson marks it as a whole (6 for meaning, 4 for accurate Spanish); this app splits it into one block per sentence, worth 2 marks each (1 for the meaning, 1 for accurate Spanish), so you can see which sentence cost you marks. Suggested timing: 20 minutes for Question 1, 35 for Question 2, 15 for Question 3, and 10 for checking verbs, agreements and accents.

**Question 1.** Answer **either** (a) **or** (b).

**(a)** Write an email to your Spanish friend about technology.

You must include the following points:
- how you use your mobile phone
- your opinion of social media, with reasons
- a film or series you watched recently
- what you are going to do online this weekend.

Write your answer in Spanish. You should aim to write between 80 and 90 words.

**(b)** Write an email to your Spanish friend about your family.

You must include the following points:
- who is in your family
- your opinion of one member of your family, with reasons
- what you did with your family last weekend
- a celebration you will have with your family in the future.

Write your answer in Spanish. You should aim to write between 80 and 90 words.

(18)
---
Two marks are added together.

**Response to the task (13 marks), best fit:**
- **11–13:** all four bullet points covered; ideas developed often (an extra clause or sentence on most points); easy to follow, with at most an occasional unclear message.
- **8–10:** at least three bullet points covered; some development; generally clear, though some messages are not immediately clear. *Missing one bullet point caps you here, however good the rest is.*
- **5–7:** at least two bullet points; occasional, brief development; parts are clear but the message sometimes breaks down.
- **1–4:** at least one bullet point; little or no extra detail; the message often breaks down.

**Language (5 marks), best fit:**
- **5:** some variety of vocabulary and structures, some longer sentences and occasional complex language (*porque, cuando, que, lo que*, two verbs in one sentence); three time frames used with general success; generally accurate, mainly minor errors.
- **3–4:** occasional variety and occasional longer sentences; at least two time frames with some success; some accurate language, mostly minor errors with some major ones.
- **1–2:** short simple sentences; limited success with time frames; frequent errors.

Minor errors (a missing accent, a wrong gender, *soy quince años*) do not stop the meaning; major errors do (a wrong tense or person, an unconjugated infinitive, an English word).

**What each bullet needs, option (a):** a present-tense use of the phone (*uso mi móvil para…*); an opinion with a reason (*creo que… porque…*); a preterite (*vi, me gustó, fue*); a future (*voy a jugar / jugaré*). Option (b): present (*somos cuatro, tengo…*); opinion with a reason (*me llevo bien con… porque…*); preterite (*fuimos, comimos*); future (*celebraremos, vamos a organizar*).

**Model answer (a), 90 words:**

¡Hola, Sofía! Uso mi móvil todos los días para hablar con mis amigos y escuchar música. Creo que las redes sociales son útiles porque puedes compartir fotos, pero a veces hay comentarios muy negativos, lo que me pone triste. Intento no pasar más de dos horas al día en línea. El sábado pasado vi una serie de misterio con mi hermana y nos encantó, aunque el final fue un poco raro. Este fin de semana voy a jugar en línea con mi primo, que vive en Escocia. ¡Escríbeme pronto! Alex

**Model answer (b), 87 words:**

¡Hola, Diego! En mi familia somos cuatro: mis padres, mi hermano menor y yo. Me llevo muy bien con mi hermano porque es muy gracioso y siempre me ayuda con los deberes, aunque a veces es un poco pesado. El fin de semana pasado fuimos todos juntos a la playa y comimos en un restaurante italiano. ¡Fue un día fantástico! El año que viene celebraremos el cumpleaños de mi abuela, que tendrá ochenta años, y vamos a organizar una fiesta enorme en el jardín. ¡Hasta pronto! Sam
:::

**Question 2.** Answer **either** (a) **or** (b).

**(a)** Write an article for your school's website about healthy living.

You must include the following points:
- what you do to stay healthy
- the advantages and disadvantages of doing sport in a team
- a time when you felt ill or stressed
- how you will improve your lifestyle in the future.

Write your answer in Spanish. You should aim to write between 130 and 150 words.

**(b)** Write a blog for a travel website about holidays.

You must include the following points:
- the type of holiday you prefer
- the advantages and disadvantages of staying in a hotel
- a trip you went on in the past
- a place you would like to visit in the future.

Write your answer in Spanish. You should aim to write between 130 and 150 words.

(22)
---
Two marks are added together.

**Response to the task (17 marks), best fit:**
- **14–17:** all four bullet points covered; ideas developed consistently; easy to understand throughout, with a message that is rarely anything but immediately clear.
- **11–13:** all four bullet points covered; frequent development; clear, with the occasional unclear message.
- **8–10:** at least three bullet points covered; some development; generally clear. *Missing a bullet point caps you here.*
- **5–7:** at least two bullet points; occasional, brief development; the message sometimes breaks down.
- **1–4:** at least one bullet point; very limited development; the message often breaks down.

**Language (5 marks), best fit:**
- **4–5:** a wide variety of vocabulary and structures, frequent longer sentences and frequent complex language; three time frames used consistently well; consistently accurate, with only minor errors.
- **2–3:** some variety, some longer sentences and some complex language; at least two time frames generally well used; generally accurate, mostly minor errors with occasional major ones.
- **1:** occasional variety; occasional success with time frames; frequent errors, some of them major.

**What lifts an answer to the top:** a real pros-and-cons paragraph (*por un lado… por otro lado…, lo bueno es que…, lo malo es que…*); a past paragraph that mixes the preterite for events with the imperfect for description (*me sentía, no dormía*); a future paragraph with more than *voy a* (*haré, me gustaría, cuando tenga…*); and Higher structures used naturally (*desde hace, ya no, vale la pena, hace falta, lo que*). Vocabulary from outside the list earns equal, not extra, credit.

**Model answer (a), 141 words:**

Para mí, es importante llevar una vida sana. Intento comer mucha fruta y verdura, bebo agua en lugar de refrescos y duermo ocho horas cada noche. Además, juego al fútbol en un equipo dos veces a la semana.

Hacer deporte en equipo tiene muchas ventajas. Por un lado, conoces a gente nueva y aprendes a trabajar con los demás. Por otro lado, si el equipo pierde, puede ser frustrante, y los entrenamientos ocupan mucho tiempo.

El año pasado, durante los exámenes, me sentía muy estresado. No dormía bien y no tenía ganas de salir. Por suerte, hablé con mi tutora, que me dio buenos consejos, y empecé a correr por las mañanas.

En el futuro, voy a pasar menos tiempo delante de la pantalla. Cuando tenga más tiempo libre, haré yoga para relajarme, porque vale la pena cuidar la salud mental.

**Model answer (b), 141 words:**

Prefiero las vacaciones en la costa porque me encanta bañarme en el mar y tomar el sol. Sin embargo, también me gusta visitar ciudades con mucha historia.

Quedarse en un hotel tiene ventajas: no tienes que cocinar ni limpiar, y muchas veces hay piscina. Lo malo es que puede ser bastante caro y, a veces, las habitaciones son pequeñas y ruidosas. Por eso, mi familia prefiere alquilar un piso.

Hace dos años fui a Madrid con mis padres. Visitamos el Parque del Retiro, donde alquilamos un barco, y comimos unas tapas deliciosas en la Plaza Mayor. Lo que más me gustó fue el museo del Prado, aunque había muchísima gente.

En el futuro, me gustaría visitar Buenos Aires porque tengo ganas de ver un partido de fútbol allí. Si tengo suficiente dinero, iré con mis mejores amigos después de los exámenes.
:::

**Question 3.** Translate this paragraph into Spanish. (Its five sentences appear one per block.)

**Sentence 1.** My town is quite small, but it has a modern library.

(2)
---
**Mi pueblo es bastante pequeño, pero tiene una biblioteca moderna.** Accept *ciudad* for town.

- Meaning (1): all of 'my town', 'quite small', 'but it has', 'a modern library' conveyed.
- Accuracy (1): *es* (not *está*) for a permanent description; *bastante* before the adjective; *una biblioteca moderna* with the adjective after the noun. *Librería* means bookshop and loses the meaning mark.
:::

::: q 6-7 T3 T1 G2b P4
**Sentence 2.** On Saturdays I like going to the market with my grandmother.

(2)
---
**Los sábados me gusta ir al mercado con mi abuela.**

- Meaning (1): 'on Saturdays', 'I like', 'going to the market', 'with my grandmother' all conveyed.
- Accuracy (1): *los sábados* with no *en* (*en sábado* is a minor error); *me gusta* + **infinitive** (*ir*, not *yendo*); *al* (= *a + el*), not *a el*.
:::

::: q 8-9 T3 G3b P4
**Sentence 3.** Last year they opened a new sports centre near my house.

(2)
---
**El año pasado abrieron un polideportivo nuevo cerca de mi casa.** Also accept *un nuevo polideportivo*, *un nuevo centro deportivo*, *el año anterior*.

- Meaning (1): 'last year', 'they opened', 'a new sports centre', 'near my house' all conveyed.
- Accuracy (1): preterite 'they' form *abrieron* (not *abrían*, *han abierto* or *abrimos*); *cerca de* with *de*.
:::

::: q 8-9 T3 G3c G4-5 P4
**Sentence 4.** When I was younger, there was more traffic and the air was dirtier.

(2)
---
**Cuando era más pequeño/a (más joven), había más tráfico y el aire estaba más sucio.** *El aire era más sucio* is accepted.

- Meaning (1): 'when I was younger', 'there was more traffic', 'the air was dirtier' all conveyed.
- Accuracy (1): **imperfect** throughout, because this is a description of how things used to be: *era*, *había* (not *hubo* or *hay*), *estaba*; *más sucio* agreeing with *el aire*; the accent on *tráfico*.
:::

::: q 8-9 T3 G3d G3e G6 P4
**Sentence 5.** When I have enough money, I will buy an electric bike in order to protect the environment.

(2)
---
**Cuando tenga suficiente dinero, compraré una bicicleta eléctrica para proteger el medio ambiente.** *Voy a comprar* is accepted for 'I will buy'.

- Meaning (1): 'when I have enough money', 'I will buy', 'an electric bike', 'to protect the environment' all conveyed.
- Accuracy (1): **cuando + subjunctive** *tenga* for a future 'when' (*cuando tengo* is a major error at Higher: it means 'whenever I have'); future *compraré* with its accent; *eléctrica* agreeing with *bicicleta*; *para* + infinitive (*para proteger*, not *por proteger* or *para protejo*).
:::
