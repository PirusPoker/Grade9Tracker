intro: AQA GCSE French 8652 Higher style, 1 hour 15 minutes, no dictionary, all answers in French. Questions 1 to 5 are the Question 1 translation into French. AQA marks those five sentences as one 10-mark answer: 15 elements of meaning ticked on grid one (13 to 15 ticks = 5 marks), plus 5 marks on grid two for vocabulary and grammar across all five sentences. The app splits them into five 2-mark blocks, so you can see which sentence cost you marks. Question 6 is the 90-word task (15 marks, three bullet points) and question 7 is the 150-word task (25 marks, two bullet points). Suggested timing: 15 minutes on the translation, 20 on question 6, 35 on question 7, 5 to check. The paper ramps from grade 4–5 to grade 8–9.

::: q 4-5 3.1.2a 3.2.1c 4.7
**Question 1: translate the following sentences into French.** (Sentences 1 to 5 together make the 10-mark AQA question.)

**Sentence 1.** My sister often goes to the cinema with her friends on Saturdays.

(2)
---
**Model:** *Ma sœur va souvent au cinéma avec ses amies le samedi.*

**Meaning (1 mark):** all three elements conveyed, even with minor slips:
- *Ma sœur va souvent* (my sister often goes);
- *au cinéma* (to the cinema);
- *avec ses amies / ses amis le samedi* (with her friends on Saturdays).

**Accurate language (1 mark):** no more than a minor slip. *Va* (not *allé*), *souvent* after the verb, *au* (not *à le*), *ses* (not *sa* or *son*), and *le samedi* with no preposition for "on Saturdays". Accept *le samedi* or *tous les samedis*.
:::

::: q 4-5 3.1.1a 3.2.1d 4.7
**Sentence 2.** Last weekend, we visited my grandparents, who live in the countryside.

(2)
---
**Model:** *Le week-end dernier, nous avons rendu visite à mes grands-parents, qui habitent à la campagne.*

**Meaning (1 mark):** all three elements conveyed:
- *Le week-end dernier, nous avons rendu visite* (last weekend we visited): accept *nous sommes allés voir*, *on a rendu visite* or *on est allés voir*;
- *à mes grands-parents* (my grandparents);
- *qui habitent / vivent à la campagne* (who live in the countryside).

**Accurate language (1 mark):** a correct perfect tense (*avons rendu* or *sommes allés*), and *qui* + *habitent* with the plural ending. *Nous avons visité mes grands-parents* conveys the meaning, but *visiter* is used for places, so treat it as a minor slip only; it does not by itself lose the language mark.
:::

::: q 6-7 3.1.3b 3.2.2c 3.2.2d 4.7
**Sentence 3.** I have been using social media for three years, but I no longer post photos.

(2)
---
**Model:** *J'utilise les réseaux sociaux depuis trois ans, mais je ne poste plus de photos.*

**Meaning (1 mark):** all three elements conveyed:
- *J'utilise les réseaux sociaux* (I have been using social media);
- *depuis trois ans* (for three years);
- *mais je ne poste plus de photos* (but I no longer post photos): accept *je ne partage plus de photos* or *je ne mets plus de photos en ligne*.

**Accurate language (1 mark):** the **present** tense with *depuis* (*j'ai utilisé... depuis* is a major error); *ne...plus* around the verb; *de* (not *des*) after the negative.
:::

::: q 6-7 3.1.1c 3.2.2b 4.7
**Sentence 4.** Next year, I will work in a shop because I would like to earn some money.

(2)
---
**Model:** *L'année prochaine, je travaillerai dans un magasin parce que je voudrais gagner de l'argent.*

**Meaning (1 mark):** all three elements conveyed:
- *L'année prochaine, je travaillerai* (next year I will work): accept *je vais travailler*;
- *dans un magasin* (in a shop);
- *parce que / car je voudrais gagner de l'argent* (because I would like to earn some money): accept *j'aimerais*.

**Accurate language (1 mark):** the future *travaillerai* (infinitive + *-ai*) or *vais travailler*; the conditional *voudrais* or *aimerais* (not *voudrai*); *de l'argent*.
:::

::: q 8-9 3.1.3c 3.2.2c 4.7
**Sentence 5.** After cleaning the beach with my class, we understood that it is important to protect the sea.

(2)
---
**Model:** *Après avoir nettoyé la plage avec ma classe, nous avons compris qu'il est important de protéger la mer.*

**Meaning (1 mark):** all three elements conveyed:
- *Après avoir nettoyé la plage* (after cleaning the beach);
- *avec ma classe, nous avons compris* (with my class, we understood): accept *on a compris*;
- *qu'il est important de protéger la mer* (that it is important to protect the sea).

**Accurate language (1 mark):** *après avoir* + past participle (*nettoyé*, not *nettoyer*; *après nettoyer* is wrong); *compris* as the past participle of *comprendre*; *il est important de* + infinitive.
:::

**Question 2.** Answer either 2.1 or 2.2. Write approximately **90 words** in French. You must write something about **each** bullet point.

**2.1** Your French friend has asked about your free time. Write about:
- what you do in your free time
- a recent outing with your friends
- an activity you would like to try in the future.

**2.2** You are writing a blog post about holidays. Write about:
- what you usually do in the holidays
- a holiday you enjoyed in the past
- your ideal holiday.

(15)
---
**Content and communication (10 marks)**
- **9–10:** all three bullets covered; communication clear; ideas regularly developed, with a lot of relevant information.
- **7–8:** all three bullets covered; mostly clear, with occasional lapses; ideas often developed, with quite a lot of relevant information.
- **5–6:** at least two bullets covered; generally clear but with likely lapses; a few ideas developed and some relevant information.
- **3–4:** at least one bullet covered; sometimes clear, with regular lapses; little relevant information.
- **1–2:** at least one bullet covered; often unclear; very little relevant information.
- The bullets need not be covered equally.

**Language (5 marks)**
- **5:** a good variety of vocabulary; regular attempts at complex language; past, present and future all used, mainly successfully; errors mainly minor.
- **4:** a variety of vocabulary; some attempts at complexity; at least two time frames, mainly successful; errors mainly minor.
- **3:** some variety; occasional attempts at complexity; at least two time frames, not always successful; more accurate than inaccurate.
- **2:** limited variety; mainly simple language, perhaps longer sentences with linking words; time frames may not work; generally inaccurate.
- **1:** narrow or repetitive vocabulary; short, simple sentences; no successful time frames; highly inaccurate.
- A zero for content means a zero for language.

**Model for 2.1 (about 95 words):** *Pendant mon temps libre, je fais beaucoup de sport. Je joue au foot deux fois par semaine, et j'adore ça parce que c'est bon pour la santé. Samedi dernier, je suis allé en ville avec mes amis. Nous avons vu un film français au cinéma, puis nous avons mangé au restaurant. Après avoir mangé, nous sommes rentrés à pied. C'était une journée géniale ! À l'avenir, j'aimerais essayer le ski, parce que ce serait passionnant, mais c'est assez cher. Je vais peut-être en faire avec le collège l'hiver prochain.*

This covers all three bullets, uses past, present and future, gives reasons, and includes *après avoir*, the conditional and *en*.
:::

::: q 8-9 3.1.1b 3.1.2c 3.2.2b 3.2.2c 4.7
**Question 3.** Answer either 3.1 or 3.2. Write approximately **150 words** in French. You must write something about **both** bullet points.

**3.1** You are writing an article for a French website about young people's health. Write about:
- how healthy your lifestyle has been, and why
- how young people could lead healthier lives in the future.

**3.2** You are writing an article about role models. Write about:
- a person who has had an influence on you, and why
- whether famous people are good role models for young people.

(25)
---
**Content and communication (15 marks)**
- **13–15:** a lot of information relevant to the task; regular successful development of ideas, accounts or description; clear, with very few or no lapses.
- **10–12:** quite a lot of relevant information; regular attempts at development, mostly successful; mostly clear, with a few lapses.
- **7–9:** an adequate amount of information; some successful development; usually clear, with some lapses.
- **4–6:** some information; a little development; sometimes unclear, with regular lapses.
- **1–3:** a limited amount of information; very little or no development; often unclear.
- Coverage need not be equal, but covering only one bullet caps content at 12.

**Range of language (5 marks)**
- **5:** a very good variety of vocabulary and structures; complex language regularly attempted and often successful.
- **4:** a good variety; complex language regularly attempted and generally successful.
- **3:** some variety; complex language occasionally attempted and sometimes successful.
- **2:** little variety; many short, simple sentences, but regular longer ones with linking words.
- **1:** very little variety; mainly short, simple structures.

**Accuracy (5 marks)**
- **5:** usually accurate (occasional major and some minor errors, mostly in complex structures); verbs and tenses secure.
- **4:** generally accurate, with several errors, usually in complex structures; verbs and tenses generally correct.
- **3:** reasonably accurate; errors in both simple and complex structures; verbs sometimes correct.
- **2:** more inaccurate than accurate; verbs often wrong.
- **1:** mostly inaccurate; verbs nearly always wrong.
- A zero for content means a zero for both language grids.

**Model paragraph for 3.1 (both bullets, in short):** *Quand j'étais petit, je mangeais mal et je passais des heures devant l'écran. Depuis l'année dernière, par contre, je fais plus attention : je vais au collège à pied et je ne bois que de l'eau. Le mois dernier, après avoir couru dix kilomètres pour une association, j'étais fatigué mais très fier. À mon avis, il vaut mieux commencer jeune, parce qu'on prend de bonnes habitudes. Si un jeune dormait plus et passait moins de temps sur son portable, il serait en meilleure forme.*

A full answer develops each bullet into its own paragraph of about 75 words, with more detail and examples. The model uses the imperfect, *depuis* + present, *ne...que*, *après avoir*, *il vaut mieux* and a conditional.
:::
